# Industry Financing Page Template

Reusable structure for industry-specific financing landing pages. Each page must follow this exact structure for consistent SEO and scalability.

## Page Structure (in order)

1. **Industry Overview** (H2)
2. **Financing Options for [Industry]** (H2)
3. **Common [Industry] Equipment That Businesses Finance** (H2)
4. **Related Financing Guides** (H2)
5. **Call to Action** (H2 or CTA block)

---

## 1. Industry Overview

- **Length:** 2–3 short paragraphs
- **Content:** Introduce the industry; explain operational costs (equipment, vehicles, payroll, expansion, project costs); mention seasonal cash flow, growth financing, working capital
- **Links:** Include internal links to relevant loan pages where appropriate
- **Goal:** Establish topical relevance; avoid generic copy—tailor to industry

---

## 2. Financing Options for [Industry]

- **Title:** "Financing Options for Construction Companies" (or applicable industry)
- **Format:** For each loan type:
  - Loan name (H3)
  - Short description (2–3 sentences, industry-specific)
  - How businesses in this industry use it
  - Internal link to core loan page

**Loan types to include when relevant:**
- Equipment Financing → /equipment-financing.html
- SBA Loans → /sba-loans.html
- Working Capital Loans → /working-capital-loans.html
- Business Line of Credit → /business-line-of-credit.html
- Commercial Vehicle Financing → /equipment-financing.html (covers vehicles)
- Commercial Real Estate Loans → /commercial-real-estate-loans.html

---

## 3. Common [Industry] Equipment That Businesses Finance

- **Title:** "Common Construction Equipment That Businesses Finance" (or applicable)
- **Items:** 5–8 pieces of equipment
- **Per item:**
  - Equipment image (landscape, high quality)
  - Equipment name (H4 or bold)
  - Short description (2–3 sentences): what it does, typical cost range, why businesses finance it
  - Link to /equipment-financing.html
- **Alt text:** Descriptive, e.g. "construction excavator used for trenching and site work"

---

## 4. [Industry] Financing Guides (prevent cannibalization)

- **Title:** "[Product] Financing Guides for [Industry]" (e.g. "Equipment Financing Guides for Contractors")
- **Intro:** Frame as industry-specific supporting content. Include link to main product page (e.g. equipment-financing.html) with anchor "For a full overview of equipment financing, see Equipment Financing."
- **Items:** 4–6 links to equipment-financing articles or relevant guides
- **Examples:** What Credit Score Is Needed for Equipment Financing, Can You Finance Used Equipment, How Equipment Financing Works
- **Links:** Use /equipment-financing/articles/[slug]/ for existing articles
- **SEO note:** Industry pages should defer to main product pages for broad terms; guides section reinforces hierarchy

---

## 5. Call to Action

- **Messaging:** "Apply for Business Financing" / "Check Your Eligibility" / "Get Matched With a Lender"
- **Include:** Short paragraph encouraging industry businesses to apply
- **Link:** /match.html

---

## SEO Requirements

- H1: "[Industry] Business Financing"
- H2 for each major section
- Internal links to core loan pages, equipment financing, and guide articles
- 1200–1800 words total
- Short readable sections; avoid large text blocks

## Cannibalization Prevention

- **Distinct focus:** Industry page = "[Industry] Business Financing" (e.g. construction, contractors). Product page = "[Product] Financing" (e.g. equipment, all industries).
- **Hierarchy:** Main product pages (equipment-financing.html) should link to industry pages; industry pages should link back with contextual anchors.
- **Related guides:** Frame as industry-specific supporting content, not competing hubs. Include explicit link to main product page for full overview.
