Business Term Loans for All U.S. Companies

Structured Commercial Financing from $10,000 to $5,000,000+

Business term loans provide All companies fixed-term financing from $10,000 to $5,000,000+, with lump-sum capital and predictable repayment. This structure is built for one-time capital needs rather than revolving access.

What Is a Business Term Loan?

A business term loan is a structured commercial loan that delivers a fixed amount upfront and is repaid over a fixed term.

  • Fixed loan amount
  • Fixed repayment schedule
  • Predictable monthly payments
  • Traditional amortized structure
  • Potentially secured or unsecured options

Term loans are best suited for businesses that need capital for a specific investment or defined project.

Common Uses for Business Term Loans

  • Expansion into new markets
  • Acquiring another business
  • Asset-heavy growth projects
  • Purchasing equipment (when cash purchase is preferred)
  • Refinancing existing business debt
  • Funding renovation or relocation
  • Large marketing initiatives

They are ideal for businesses that need fixed capital and clear repayment terms.

Business Term Loan vs Line of Credit

Feature Business Term Loan Line of Credit
Funding Structure Lump sum Revolving
Repayment Fixed schedule Flexible draw & repay
Interest On full amount On amount drawn
Best For Defined capital investment Ongoing operations

If your need is a one-time capital project, a business term loan often provides more stability.

Secured vs Unsecured Business Term Loans

Unsecured Term Loans

  • No specific collateral pledged
  • Usually shorter terms
  • Higher rates than secured options
  • Often capped for larger requests

Secured Term Loans

  • Backed by business assets
  • Lower rates and longer terms possible
  • Higher loan amounts available
  • Often better fit for large structured projects

What Do Lenders Look For?

  • Revenue stability and consistency
  • Debt service coverage ratio (DSCR)
  • Credit profile (typically 680+ preferred)
  • Time in business (2+ years preferred)
  • Industry risk

Stronger businesses typically qualify for lower rates and better pricing.

Typical Approval Timeline

Approval timing depends on structure and amount:

  • Small to mid-sized term loans: often 3-10 business days
  • Large structured facilities: 2-6 weeks
  • Timeline improves with clean financial documentation

When Is a Business Term Loan the Right Choice?

  • You need a defined amount of capital
  • You prefer predictable monthly payments
  • You are funding a specific long-term initiative
  • You are not looking for revolving draw flexibility
  • You want fixed-rate or fixed-term visibility
