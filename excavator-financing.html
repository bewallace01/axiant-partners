What Is Excavator Financing?

Excavator financing is a loan or lease that lets you get the excavator you need without paying cash for the full price. You make payments over time; with a loan you own the machine when it's paid off. Excavators are essential for digging, grading, and site work-but they're expensive. Used excavator financing and new excavator loans are both common. We connect you with lenders that finance excavators so you can get the machine and get back to work.

What Excavators Can Be Financed?

New excavators. Buying from a dealer? Lenders routinely finance new excavators. Get your quote, we match you with a lender, and you secure financing before you buy.

Used excavators. Used excavator financing is widely available. Lenders look at age, hours, condition, and value. Terms may be shorter than for new, but you can still finance used machines. If you're looking for "used excavator financing" you're in the right place.

Private seller. Buying from a private party? Some of our lenders fund private purchases with a bill of sale and equipment details.

Dealer purchase. Dealer deals are the most common. We add options by matching you with our lender network so you can compare rates and terms.

Terms & Requirements

Loan amounts. Excavator loans can run from the tens of thousands into the hundreds of thousands depending on size and type (mini, mid-size, full-size). We match you with lenders that finance in your range.

Credit flexibility. We work with lenders that consider a range of credit. Better credit usually means better rates, but we also work with lenders that offer excavator financing for fair or lower credit.

Time in business. Requirements vary. Some lenders want to see a year or more in business; others work with newer contractors. We match you based on your situation.

Down payment. Down payments of 10-20% are common. More down can help with approval or rate. We connect you with lenders that fit what you can put down.
