# Equipment Financing Page: SEO Review & Mid-America Comparison

**Date:** March 2025  
**Page:** Axiant Partners Equipment Financing (`equipment-financing.html`)  
**Competitor:** Mid-America Commercial Lending Equipment Leasing (`/financing-equipment/`)

---

## Executive Summary

Axiant’s equipment financing page is structurally strong and already implements many SEO best practices. Compared to Mid-America, Axiant is ahead in schema markup, visual design, and some content elements. The main opportunities are in **H1/hero messaging**, **program-specific subsections**, **FAQ depth**, **internal linking**, and **conversion-focused CTAs**.

---

## 1. Title Tag & Meta

| Element | Axiant | Mid-America |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| **Title** | "Equipment Financing for Businesses Nationwide \| Axiant Partners" | "Affordable Equipment Leasing Experts \| Industrial Loans USA" |
| **Meta description** | Present, ~155 chars, includes $10K–$5M, 24hr, industries | Not visible in fetch; typically present |

**Axiant strengths:** Clear, branded, includes key differentiators (amounts, speed).

**Mid-America approach:** Uses alternate terms ("Equipment Leasing," "Industrial Loans"), "USA," and benefit word ("Affordable").

**Recommendation:** A/B test titles such as:
- "Equipment Leasing & Financing for U.S. Businesses \| Axiant Partners"
- "Equipment Financing $10K–$5M \| Fast Approvals \| Axiant Partners"
