# Equipment Financing Page Guideline

**Copy this and send it when you want equipment pages created or updated to match the excavator standard.**

---

## Reference

Use `equipment/excavators/index.html` as the template. Match its structure, depth, and SEO.

## Structure (19 sections, in order)

1. **Hero** — Full-width with bg image, gradient, H1, subhead, 4 bullets, Apply + Back CTAs
2. **Trust stats** — At a Glance: 6 stats (range, approval, terms, down, credit, nationwide)
3. **Intro with image** — Why financing makes sense, 2–3 paragraphs + image
4. **What Is [Equipment]?** — Definition, uses, industries
5. **Why [Equipment] Financing Is Different** — Lender view, resale value, industry underwriting
6. **Financing options** — 3 cards: Equipment Loans, Leasing, SBA + working capital note
7. **How Much Does It Cost?** — New/used pricing, factors, link to calculator
8. **Rates and monthly payments** — 6–15% range, example calculations
9. **Requirements** — Table + 4 paragraphs (credit, down, time in biz, revenue)
10. **What to have ready** — Bullet checklist
11. **When to apply** — Timing, early application
12. **Tips to get approved** — 5–6 bullets
13. **Common mistakes** — 5 bullets
14. **Why finance vs pay cash** — Cash flow, tax benefits
15. **Process steps** — 4 steps (Quote/Apply, Match, Review, Funding)
16. **Related equipment** — 4 cards + Browse/Apply buttons
17. **FAQ** — 6–8 questions
18. **More resources** — 5–6 internal links
19. **Final CTA**

## Requirements

- **2,500–3,000+ words**
- **12–18 internal links** to equipment-financing, match, calculator, equipment hub, industry page, articles (credit, rates, used, loan vs lease, down payment, red flags, approval speed, what lenders look at)
- **Schema:** BreadcrumbList, FinancialService, FAQPage, Article, HowTo
- **Meta:** 150–160 char description, title with "Costs & Rates"
- **Classes:** `form-container equipment-financing-content construction-content ef-content`, `ef-hero`, `ef-page-wrap`, `ef-content`, `trust-section`, `ef-intro-with-img`, `ef-industry-row`, `benefits-grid ef-equipment-cards`, `financing-options-chart`, `steps-grid`, `faq-list`, `services-cta`
