What Is Bucket Truck Financing?

Bucket truck financing is a loan or lease that lets you get a bucket truck (aerial lift truck, utility truck) without paying the full price upfront. You make monthly payments; with a loan you own the truck when it's paid off. Bucket trucks are essential for linemen, tree care, signs, and telecom—but they're expensive. Whether you're searching "bucket truck financing" or "bucket truck financing bad credit," we connect you with lenders that finance aerial and utility trucks so you can get the truck and get to work.

What Bucket Trucks Can Be Financed?

New bucket trucks. Buying from a dealer? Lenders routinely finance new aerial and utility trucks. Get your quote, we match you with a lender, and you secure financing before you buy.

Used bucket trucks. Used bucket truck financing is common. Lenders look at age, condition, and value. Terms may be a bit shorter than for new, but you can still finance used bucket trucks.

Private seller. Some of our lenders fund private-party purchases with a bill of sale and truck details.

Dealer purchase. Dealer deals are the most common. We add options by matching you with our network so you can compare terms.
