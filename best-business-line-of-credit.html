The Best Business Lines of Credit, Reviewed

Best Overall

Business Line of Credit

A line of credit is the Swiss Army knife of business financing: draw what you need, repay, and reuse—paying interest only on the balance you use. It's the best overall tool for managing fluctuating cash flow and standing ready for the unexpected. Explore the full line of credit guides.

Best for: ongoing, variable needs · Watch-out: discipline required to avoid over-drawing

Best Unsecured

Unsecured Line of Credit

When you don't want to pledge specific assets, an unsecured line is best—faster and simpler to obtain, usually with a personal guarantee, at a somewhat higher rate. See unsecured business loans for the broader picture.

Best for: no collateral, speed · Watch-out: higher rates, lower limits

Best for Lowest Rates

Secured / Bank Line of Credit

Backing the line with collateral—receivables, inventory, or other assets—earns lower rates and higher limits. A bank or secured line is the best pick when cost and size matter most and you can wait through a longer approval.

Best for: lowest cost, larger limits · Watch-out: collateral and slower process

Best for Bad Credit

Revenue-Based Line / Financing

When credit is the obstacle, a revenue-based line or financing underwrites on your sales, not your score—the most accessible route for strong-revenue businesses with challenged credit. See bad credit business loans.

Best for: strong revenue, low credit · Watch-out: cost reflects the risk

Best Business Line of Credit FAQs

What is the best business line of credit?

It depends on your priority. A secured or bank line for lowest rates, an unsecured line for no collateral, an online line for speed, and a revenue-based option for challenged credit.

What's the difference between a secured and unsecured line?

A secured line is backed by collateral and usually earns lower rates and higher limits. An unsecured line requires no specific collateral—faster and simpler, but typically higher rates and lower limits, often with a personal guarantee.

Can I get a line of credit with bad credit?

Often yes, though options narrow. Revenue-based lines and financing weigh sales over score, making them the most accessible for strong-revenue businesses. See bad credit business loans.

How fast can I get a line of credit?

Online lines can be approved in a day or two with light documentation; bank lines take longer but offer lower rates.
