A landscape trailer is designed to haul mowers, equipment, mulch, soil, and landscaping materials. Dump trailers have a hydraulically powered bed that tilts to unload. How Much Does a Landscape Trailer Cost?

New utility trailers range from roughly $3,000–$8,000 for 5x8 to 6x12 sizes. New dump trailers typically cost $8,000–$25,000+ depending on bed size and capacity. Used landscape trailers typically cost 25–40% less. A 5-year-old dump trailer might run $5,000–$15,000. Older utility trailers can start around $1,500–$4,000. Many lenders finance used equipment up to 5–7 years old. Obtain a written quote. Estimate payments.

Landscape Trailer Financing FAQ

Can you finance a used landscape trailer?

Yes. Many lenders finance used dump and utility trailers, typically 5–7 years old or newer. Condition, axle rating, and brand affect approval.

What credit score is required?

Most lenders look for 600 or higher. 680+ qualifies for the best rates.

Dump trailer vs utility trailer—does it affect financing?

Lenders finance both. Dump trailers typically cost more and may qualify for slightly longer terms. Price and condition matter more.

How long does approval take?

1–5 business days for equipment loans and leases. SBA adds 30–60+ days.

Is leasing better than buying?

It depends. Leasing offers lower payments and easier upgrades. Buying builds equity. Equipment loan vs lease.

What documents are needed?

3–6 months of bank statements, tax returns, profit and loss, equipment quote, and business formation documents.
