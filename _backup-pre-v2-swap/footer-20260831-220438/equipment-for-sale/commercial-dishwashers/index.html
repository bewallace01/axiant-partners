Commercial Dishwashers financing — FAQ

How much does a commercial dishwasher cost?

Undercounter and single-door machines are the most affordable, while high-volume rack-conveyor and flight-type warewashers are among the larger kitchen equipment investments and are typically quoted per configuration. Because conveyor machines carry a higher ticket, many operators finance them rather than pay cash.

Can I finance a commercial dishwasher through Axiant?

Yes. Axiant arranges financing on new and used commercial dishwashers with terms up to 60 to 72 months and approvals in as little as 24 hours, subject to credit approval.

Conveyor or door-type — which do I need?

Door and undercounter machines suit lower-volume kitchens, while rack-conveyor machines like the Hobart CL series handle high-throughput dish pits with far less labor. Both are financeable; choose by your covers per hour and we finance the quoted unit.

Does financing cover installation?

Financing is arranged on the equipment price quoted by the dealer. Tell us about your full project and we will advise on what can be included, subject to approval.
