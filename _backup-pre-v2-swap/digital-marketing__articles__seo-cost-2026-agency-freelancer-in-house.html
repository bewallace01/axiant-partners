FAQ

What is a realistic monthly SEO retainer for SMBs?

It varies by competition and implementation load; use this guide’s framework to compare line items rather than trusting a single national number.

Why do SEO quotes differ so much?

Different bundles mix audits, content, links, technical fixes, and reporting. Separate one-time remediation from ongoing retainers.

Should SEO include content creation?

Often yes, but specify editorial standards, review workflow, and E-E-A-T expectations—especially for YMYL topics.

Is cheap SEO ever worth it?

Rarely in competitive SERPs; low price often signals outsourcing risk, thin technical work, or link tactics you cannot defend.

How should finance evaluate SEO ROI?

Use qualified traffic, conversion rate, assisted pipeline, and payback—not keyword rank screenshots alone.

Takeaway

SEO price follows scope: technical implementation, content quality, authority work, analytics, and governance time.

Model total cost of ownership including your internal dev queue—otherwise cheap quotes stall.
