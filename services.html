Financial Services We Offer

At Axiant Partners, we provide a wide range of commercial lending and financing solutions to help your business grow, expand, and thrive. Our team connects you with the right lenders for each specific need.

Quick answer: If you know your goal (equipment, liquidity, real estate, expansion), start with the matching service below—then apply once to compare lender programs.

  • Lowest payments / long terms: SBA loans
  • Asset purchases: equipment financing
  • Flexible cash flow: business lines of credit
  • Operating expenses: working capital
  • Property purchases/refi: commercial real estate loans

SBA Loans

Government-backed financing programs with competitive terms designed to support business growth and long-term stability.

Equipment Financing

Flexible financing for essential equipment purchases across industries, with terms built around your business cash flow.

Business Line of Credit

Revolving access to capital for inventory, payroll, and short-term needs with draw flexibility as your business evolves.

Business Term Loans

Structured installment financing for expansion, major purchases, and strategic investments with predictable repayments.

Commercial Real Estate Loans

Financing for owner-occupied properties, expansion locations, refinances, and other business real estate goals.

Commercial Bridge Loans

Short-term capital solutions that help bridge immediate opportunities until long-term financing is in place.

Revenue-Based Financing

Payments tied to your revenue performance, giving your business greater flexibility during growth and seasonal swings.

Merchant Cash Advance

Upfront capital repaid as a percentage of daily card sales or bank deposits. Fast funding for businesses with strong card volume.

Securities-Based Lending

Access working capital by leveraging qualified investment assets while maintaining long-term portfolio positions.

Fix and Flip Financing

Fast project-based financing for real estate investors purchasing, renovating, and selling properties for profit.
