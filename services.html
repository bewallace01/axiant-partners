At Axiant Partners, we provide a wide range of commercial lending and financing solutions to help your business grow, expand, and thrive. Our team connects you with the right lenders for each specific need.

SBA Loans

Government-backed financing programs with competitive terms designed to support business growth and long-term stability.

Equipment Financing

Flexible financing for essential equipment purchases across industries, with terms built around your business cash flow.

Business Line of Credit

Revolving access to capital for inventory, payroll, and short-term needs with draw flexibility as your business evolves.

Business Term Loans

Structured installment financing for expansion, major purchases, and strategic investments with predictable repayments.

Payments tied to your revenue performance, giving your business greater flexibility during growth and seasonal swings.
