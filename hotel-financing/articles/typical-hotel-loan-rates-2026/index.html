What Moves a Hotel's Rate

  • Coverage. Hotels carry more income volatility than offices or warehouses, so lenders price for it. Higher debt coverage gets better pricing.
  • Flag and segment. Strong brands and limited-service hotels usually price better than weak independents. See flagged vs independent.
  • Trend. Lenders read the trailing-12-month revenue per available room against the competitive set.
  • PIP status. An unfunded PIP raises risk. A funded one, held by the lender, lowers it. See PIP financing.
  • Operator. Experienced owners get better terms; see hotel experience.
  • Location. In towns under about 50,000 people, a USDA hotel loan can be another option.

On a 7(a), the recoupment fee applies only if you voluntarily prepay more than 25% of the loan in a year during the first three years. Prepaying 30% of a $3,000,000 loan would cost about $45,000 in year one, $27,000 in year two and $9,000 in year three. 504 details: 504 fees and the amortization schedule, which shows the prepayment premium.

How to Read a Hotel Term Sheet

  1. Find the index and the spread. A 7(a) quote should say prime plus X; check it doesn't exceed prime plus 3% on a loan over $350,000.
  2. Check how often it adjusts. Many 7(a) loans reset quarterly.
  3. Ask for the amortization and the maturity. A low rate with a 5-year balloon is a different loan from a fully amortizing 25-year SBA loan.
  4. Add every fee and compare total cost, not the headline rate.
  5. Read the prepayment section if you might sell or refinance within five years.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are hotel loan rates right now?

In November 2026: SBA 7(a) up to 10.00% variable on loans over $350,000, SBA 504 about 7.0-8.1% blended, CMBS about 7.5-9.0% fixed and bridge loans about 9-12% plus points. These are typical ranges, not quotes.

What is the maximum rate on an SBA hotel loan?

For a variable 7(a) loan over $350,000, prime plus 3.0%. With prime at 7.00%, that is 10.00%. Smaller loans have higher caps.

Are hotel loans more expensive than other commercial real estate loans?

Usually somewhat, because hotel income changes nightly and lenders price for that volatility.

Is a 504 cheaper than a 7(a) for a hotel?

Often on rate, because the debenture is fixed for its full term. A 504 needs at least 15% down on a hotel, though, and 20% for a business two years old or less.

Is there a prepayment penalty on an SBA hotel loan?

On a 7(a) of 15 years or longer, a fee of 5%, 3% or 1% applies to voluntary prepayments over 25% in any of the first three years. A 504 debenture has its own declining premium.
