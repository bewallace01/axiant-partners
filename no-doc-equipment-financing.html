What Is No Doc Equipment Financing?

No doc equipment financing is a loan or lease that requires little or no tax returns, financial statements, or profit-and-loss documentation. "No doc" doesn't always mean zero paperwork—some lenders ask for basic info and bank statements but skip full tax returns. If you're self-employed, have complicated books, or just want a faster process, we connect you with lenders that offer no doc or low doc equipment financing so you can get approved without a stack of financials.

What Equipment Can Be Financed With No Doc?

Equipment-backed deals. When the equipment is the main collateral, some lenders focus on the asset value and your credit rather than deep financials. Construction equipment, trucks, and machinery often qualify.

New and used. No doc and low doc options exist for both new and used equipment. Terms may vary by lender and asset type.

Dealer and private party. Many no doc programs work through dealers. Some of our lenders also fund private-party purchases with minimal documentation.

Not every deal qualifies. We match you with lenders that actually offer no doc or low doc equipment financing so you know your options.

Terms and Requirements

Documentation. "No doc" can mean application plus bank statements, or application plus minimal financials. We'll tell you what each lender typically needs so there are no surprises.

Credit. Lenders that do no doc or low doc often rely more on credit and the equipment. Strong credit can improve approval and terms.

Down payment. Some lenders ask for a larger down payment when documentation is light. We connect you with options that fit what you can provide.

Loan amounts. Amounts vary by lender and equipment. We match you with lenders that finance in your range.
