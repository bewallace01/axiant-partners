FAQ

When is an agency the best first move?

When you need breadth, tooling, and speed and lack internal SEO leadership to set standards.

When should you hire in-house?

When SEO is core to revenue, you have steady dev support, and you want institutional memory embedded daily.

What does fractional SEO leadership do?

Sets roadmap, KPI definitions, vendor oversight, and cross-team governance—often part-time for mid-market firms.

Can we switch models later?

Yes—document access, deliverables, and analytics ownership so transitions do not orphan data.

How do lenders view marketing spend?

They favor coherent plans with measurable milestones; keep receipts and tie spend to pipeline where possible.

Takeaway

Agencies scale execution; in-house embeds context; fractional leaders align stakeholders—many firms blend two of three.

Start from bottlenecks: technical debt, content governance, or analytics truth—then hire for that pain.
