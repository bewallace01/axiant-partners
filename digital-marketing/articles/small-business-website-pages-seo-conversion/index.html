FAQ

What pages are non-negotiable?

Homepage, services with proof, about/trust signals, contact, and policy pages appropriate to your ads and compliance needs.

Are city pages still useful?

Only when each page adds unique value: local proof, FAQs, and distinct service context.

How do I avoid thin content penalties?

Consolidate duplicates, add expert review for sensitive topics, and block low-value parameters from indexing.

Do blogs help every SMB?

Only if you can sustain quality and target real queries; otherwise prioritize service and resource hubs.

What tools validate IA success?

Search Console coverage and queries, landing-page conversion rates, and internal site search gaps.

Takeaway

Search engines reward helpful, distinct pages—not hundreds of thin clones with swapped city names.
