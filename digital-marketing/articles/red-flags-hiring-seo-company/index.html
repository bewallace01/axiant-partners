FAQ

Are guaranteed rankings always bad?

In competitive markets, guarantees are usually misleading; algorithms and competitors change constantly.

What reporting minimums should I require?

Search Console access, analytics events tied to conversions, and technical change logs—not rank-only PDFs.

What link tactics are unacceptable?

Paid link networks, irrelevant guest posts, and opaque PBNs create long-tail penalty risk.

What contract terms matter?

Clear scope, performance review cadence, data ownership, and ethical offboarding assistance.

What if my site already has a manual action?

Demand a documented recovery plan aligned to Google guidelines; avoid vendors who only promise new content volume.

Takeaway

Great SEO is testable: access, methodology, and reporting should withstand scrutiny from your dev and finance leads.

If a vendor dodges implementation responsibility or data access, expect delays—not miracles.
