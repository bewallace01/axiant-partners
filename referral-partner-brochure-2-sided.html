Product Guidelines Snapshot

General qualification bands by product category for referral screening.

Business Line of Credit

Typical profile: 550-620+ FICO, 6-24+ months TIB, minimum revenue often $120K-$300K annual, recurring deposits, and clear short-term working-capital use.

Working Capital / Cash-Flow Products

Typical profile: 500-620+ FICO, 3-12+ months TIB, minimum revenue often $120K-$250K annual (or about $10K-$20K/month), stable deposits, and recent bank statements.

Equipment Financing

Typical profile: 580-680+ FICO, 1-24+ months TIB, minimum revenue often $100K-$500K annual (deal-size dependent), vendor quote/invoice, and asset details.

Business Term Loans

Typical profile: 500-650+ FICO, 3-24+ months TIB, minimum revenue often $150K-$500K annual, and debt-service capacity supported by cash flow.

SBA / Bank Programs

Typical profile: 620-700+ FICO, 2+ years TIB preferred, minimum revenue often $250K-$500K+ annual, stronger tax returns/financials, and lower leverage.

Commercial Real Estate / Bridge

Typical profile: 620-700+ FICO, collateral-driven underwriting, revenue minimums vary by asset/collateral but often $250K+ annual for owner-occupied deals, plus full property docs.
