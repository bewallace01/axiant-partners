How Fast Can You Close a Fix and Flip Loan?

Typical timelines and what drives speed-to-fund

← Back to Fix and Flip Articles

Updated March 2, 2026

Quick Answer

Learn how fast you can close a fix and flip loan, typical timeline ranges, and the key factors that speed up or delay funding. Focus on Typical Fix and Flip Closing Timeline, Conventional Timeline Breakdown, What Impacts Closing Speed–. This guidance applies to most U.S. lenders and programs.

When evaluating a real estate investment opportunity, closing speed can determine whether you win or lose the deal. Fix and flip loans are built for faster execution than traditional mortgages, but exact timelines depend on file quality, property profile, and lender workflow.

Typical Fix and Flip Closing Timeline

For structured fix and flip lenders, many files close in about 15-21 days. In stronger scenarios, timelines can be faster. In more complex files, timelines can extend.

Conventional Timeline Breakdown

  • Initial review and pre-qualification: 24-72 hours
  • Term sheet and underwriting kickoff: 1-3 business days
  • Valuation and due diligence: 5-10 days
  • Final approval and docs: 3-5 days
  • Closing and funding: 1-3 days

What Impacts Closing Speed?

1. Appraisal and ARV Validation

  • Property valuation and comparable support
  • Repair budget and after-repair-value (ARV) confidence
  • Any valuation disputes or revisions

Providing strong comp support early helps avoid re-trades and delays.

2. Borrower Preparedness

  • Entity documents and operating agreement
  • Bank statements and liquidity proof
  • Scope of work and contractor estimates
  • Purchase contract and title details

Incomplete documentation is the most common delay driver.

3. Experience Level

Experienced investors often close faster because their files are cleaner, budgets are more realistic, and exit strategies are better defined.

4. Property Complexity

Heavier rehab scope, title issues, or unusual property characteristics can increase underwriting and legal review time.

Can You Close in Less Than 10 Days?

In select scenarios, yes. This usually requires an exceptionally clean file, simple property profile, strong borrower history, and immediate responsiveness from all parties.

How Fix and Flip Timelines Compare to Conventional Loans

Conventional mortgages typically involve longer underwriting and committee cycles, often 30-60+ days. Fix and flip financing is designed for faster execution and short-term project velocity.

What Slows Down Fix and Flip Closings?

  • Underestimated rehab scope
  • Weak ARV support
  • Missing borrower documents
  • Title, legal, or insurance issues
  • Poor communication between parties

How to Close Faster

  • Prepare complete documentation before applying
  • Use realistic rehab budgets and contractor scopes
  • Submit clear ARV comps early
  • Respond to lender conditions quickly
  • Match loan structure to deal profile

Final Thoughts

In many cases, fix and flip loans close in 15-21 days, with faster outcomes possible for highly prepared borrowers. If timeline is critical, review current fix and flip loan options and align your package to lender expectations before submission.
