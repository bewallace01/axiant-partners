Startup Equipment Financing

New business? Need equipment? We match you with lenders that offer startup equipment financing.

What Is Startup Equipment Financing?

Startup equipment financing is a loan or lease for equipment when your business is new-often under one or two years old. Banks often want to see a long track record. We work with lenders that consider newer businesses so you can get the trucks, machinery, or tools you need without waiting years. We don't promise approval-every lender decides-but we focus on finding options that actually work with startups.

What Equipment Can Startups Finance?

Construction and heavy equipment. Dump trucks, excavators, skid steers, dozers-many of our lenders finance these for newer contractors. Terms may require a down payment or stronger personal credit, but options exist.

Trucks and trailers. Commercial trucks, bucket trucks, and trailers are commonly financed. We match you with lenders that have done deals with new hauling and contracting companies.

Manufacturing and shop equipment. CNC machines, welders, and other shop equipment can be financed. Lenders look at the equipment value and your ability to pay.

Other business equipment. Office, medical, or food service equipment-we have lenders that consider startups in these categories too.

Terms and Requirements

Time in business. Some of our partners work with businesses that are only a few months old; others want at least 6-12 months. We match you based on how long you've been in business.

Credit. Strong personal or business credit can help. We also work with lenders that consider fair credit for startups when the deal makes sense.

Down payment. Startups are often asked for 15-25% down. More down can improve approval odds and terms. We connect you with lenders that fit what you can put down.

Loan amounts. Amounts vary by lender and equipment. We match you with lenders that finance in the range you need.
