What Credit Score Do I Need for Equipment Financing?

Many business owners ask: what credit score do I need? The answer varies by lender. Some require strong credit; others specialize in bad credit equipment financing and consider factors like revenue, down payment, and the equipment itself. We work with lenders across the credit spectrum and match you with those whose criteria fit your profile-including options for fair or poor credit.

Typical eligibility

Lenders that work with lower credit may require a larger down payment, shorter term, or use the equipment as strong collateral. Rates may be higher than for excellent credit. We connect you with lenders that offer equipment financing for bad credit or fair credit so you can get a quote.

Who qualifies

Business owners with fair or poor personal or business credit who need to finance equipment. We don't guarantee approval-each lender decides-but we focus on finding lenders that consider applications from lower-credit borrowers. See also our FAQ for "What credit score do I need?"
