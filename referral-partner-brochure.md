## Funding Source Coverage Snapshot (Internal Directory-Based)

The following examples come from active funding-source profiles and program matrices:

### 1) National Business Capital (Large-Ticket Business Lending)
- Typical ticket profile: **$250K to $15M**
- Common baseline: **620+ FICO**, **1+ year TIB**, **$2M+ revenue**
- Core products: term loan, business LOC, cash-flow financing, equipment financing, sub debt

### 2) South End Capital / Stearns Bank Channel (Portal + Marketplace)
- Intake paths: partner portal and API-enabled workflow
- Program classes include SBA, equipment, LOC, and fast-capital channels
- Routing may be internal or external marketplace based on eligibility

### 3) Go Kapital (Business + Real Estate Mix)
- Business products: approximately **$20K to $5M**
- Real estate programs: approximately **$100K to $50M**
- Covers working capital, equipment, startup/personal, LOC, term loan, and bridge-style real estate options

### 4) ARF Financial (Revolving LOC Programs)
- Credit floor commonly shown around **575** (program dependent)
- Sales floor around **$17K/month** shown in operations docs
- Revolving line products with draw/paydown mechanics and residual-style referral economics

### 5) AV Tech Capital and LOEB Equipment (Larger Equipment/Asset Profiles)
- AV Tech: large-ticket equipment and sale-leaseback structures
- LOEB: asset-based term/line/sale-leaseback, often **$1M+** profile

### 6) BSB Leasing Syndication Network (Equipment Finance Matrix)
- Multiple syndicated equipment sources under BSB program structure
- Broad credit/TIB bands by source (bank-specific)
- App-only and full-financial requirement tracks

## Best Referral Profiles

Send us businesses that have one or more of the following:
- Clear use of funds (growth, equipment, cash-flow support, acquisition, refinance, etc.)
- At least some verifiable operating history and business banking activity
- Owner willing to provide required docs promptly
- Specific timeline or funding objective

Industries served nationwide, including construction, trucking/logistics, manufacturing, restaurants, medical and many others.

---

## Referral Submission Checklist

To speed placement and approvals, share:
- Business legal name, owner name, and contact info
- Requested amount and use of funds
- Time in business and approximate annual revenue
- Credit range (if known)
- Existing debt obligations (if applicable)
- Last 3-6 months bank statements (when available)
- Any time-sensitive context (contract, equipment purchase, closing date, payroll gap, etc.)
