"Working capital loan" is an umbrella term, not a single product. It covers bank and SBA-backed term loans, online term loans, short-term loans, and merchant-cash-advance-style financing — and their rates differ enormously. In 2026, every one of these prices higher than it did before the Federal Reserve's tightening cycle, but the gap between the cheapest and most expensive working capital is still the most important number on this page. How Working Capital Rates Are Set

Bank and SBA-backed working capital prices as prime + a spread; prime tracks the Federal Reserve and sits around 7.5% as of June 2026. Online, short-term, and MCA-style lenders set rates on internal bank-statement models that weigh deposit volume, consistency, negative days, and time in business — their pricing runs higher and is driven more by risk appetite than by Fed moves. Because most working capital is unsecured and fast, the lender carries more risk than on a collateralized or government-guaranteed loan, and the rate reflects it.

What Actually Drives Your Quote

  • Product choice — the dominant factor. The same business can be quoted 11% on a bank loan and 50%+ on an MCA in the same week.
  • Bank-statement strength — deposit volume, consistency, and few negative days matter more than peak revenue.
  • Time in business — 2+ years opens bank and SBA; 6+ months opens most online products.
  • Personal FICO — still a major input, especially for the bank and SBA tiers.
  • Existing debt / stacked positions — multiple open advances raise risk and rate, and can trigger declines. See how to get out of bad business debt.
  • Speed — same-day funding always carries a premium over a 1-3 week close.

How to Compare Working Capital Offers

  1. Convert everything to APR. Factor rates and per-invoice fees are not comparable to an APR until you annualize them. A 1.30 factor over 6 months is roughly 80% APR, not 30%.
  2. Look at total payback, not the rate. On short terms, the dollar cost of capital is what hits cash flow. Compare total payback against the use of funds.
  3. Right-size the term. Funding a long-term need (expansion, hiring) with a short-term product forces refinancing and stacking. Match term to how long the cash is actually needed.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are typical working capital loan rates in 2026?

They vary widely by product: bank lines and term loans price lowest, online term loans and lines mid-range, and short-term advances highest — often quoted as a factor rate rather than an APR. Your profile moves the quote within each band.

How are working capital rates set?

By the product type, your credit and revenue, time in business, and how the lender prices risk. Asset-secured products price below unsecured ones, and short-term speed-focused products cost the most.

What drives my working capital quote?

Bank-statement strength and consistent revenue, credit, time in business, existing debt load, and the product you choose. Cleaning up statements and reducing debt before applying improves the quote.

How do I compare working capital offers?

Convert everything to a comparable cost — total dollars repaid and, where possible, an effective APR — not just the headline rate or factor. Watch fees, payment frequency, and prepayment terms.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are working capital loan rates in 2026?

Working capital loan rates span a wide range in 2026 because the category covers several products. Bank and SBA-backed working capital runs 9-14% APR; online term loans run 14-30%; short-term and MCA-style products run 30-60%+ effective APR. The product you choose matters more than any other single factor.

Why are working capital loan rates so much higher than SBA loans?

Working capital loans are usually unsecured and fast, so lenders price in more risk and speed. SBA loans are partially government-guaranteed, fully collateralized, and slow to fund, which lets them price far lower. The trade-off is speed and documentation versus cost.

Is a working capital loan quoted as APR or a factor rate?

It depends on the product. Bank and online term-style working capital loans quote an APR. Short-term and merchant-cash-advance-style products quote a factor rate (e.g. 1.20-1.40), which must be converted to APR to compare. A 1.30 factor over 6 months is roughly 80% APR.

What credit score do you need for the best working capital rates?

680+ FICO with 2+ years in business and steady revenue opens bank and SBA pricing of 9-14%. Below 600, expect short-term products at 30%+ unless you can secure the loan or show very strong deposits.

How fast can working capital loans fund, and does speed cost more?

Online working capital can fund in 1-3 business days; banks and SBA take weeks. Speed carries a premium: the fastest products are also the most expensive. Matching the product to the actual need keeps cost down.
