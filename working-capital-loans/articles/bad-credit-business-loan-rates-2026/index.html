Quick answer

Bad-credit business loan rates in 2026 depend heavily on FICO band and collateral. 640-679: 15-25% APR on online term loans. 580-639: short-term loans at 30-50% effective APR, or equipment financing at 16-22%. Below 580: mostly merchant cash advances at 1.30-1.50 factor (40-100%+ effective APR), invoice factoring, or secured options. The biggest levers are collateral and bank-statement strength — both can move you a tier regardless of score.

A low credit score doesn't close every door — it narrows which products you qualify for and raises the price. The good news is that most bad-credit business financing is underwritten on revenue and bank statements rather than FICO alone, so a strong, consistent deposit history can offset a weak score. Which Products Stay Available With Bad Credit

  • Equipment financing — the asset secures the loan, so credit matters less. Often the cheapest bad-credit option at 16-22%. See equipment financing rates 2026.
  • Invoice factoring — priced on your customers' credit, not yours. A fit if slow receivables are the real problem. See invoice factoring rates.
  • Short-term loans — faster and looser than bank loans, at 30-50% effective APR.
  • Merchant cash advance — the most accessible and the most expensive; convert the factor rate before signing. See MCA rates 2026.

What Actually Drives Your Rate

  • Bank-statement strength — for bad-credit products this outweighs FICO. Deposit consistency and negative days matter most.
  • Collateral — securing the loan can cut hundreds of basis points and open denied products.
  • Time in business — 2+ years widens options even with a low score.
  • Existing advances — stacked positions raise rate and trigger declines.
  • Industry and revenue trend — growing, stable revenue earns better pricing than a declining trend at the same score.

How to Lower the Cost

  1. Clean up 90 days of statements before applying — steady deposits, no overdrafts.
  2. Offer collateral if you have equipment, receivables, or real estate.
  3. Right-size the product — don't fund a long-term need with a short-term advance.
  4. Avoid stacking — one facility, repaid cleanly, builds the track record that earns a refinance.
  5. Refinance after 6-12 clean months into a cheaper product as your score and history improve. See how to get out of bad business debt.
