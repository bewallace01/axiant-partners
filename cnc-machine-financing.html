What Is CNC Machine Financing?

CNC machine financing is a loan or lease that lets you get a CNC machine (milling, turning, machining center, plasma, laser, etc.) without paying the full price upfront. You make monthly payments; with a loan you own the machine when it's paid off. CNC equipment is the backbone of precision manufacturing-but it's a major investment. We connect you with lenders that offer CNC machine loans and leasing so you can add or upgrade equipment without draining your cash.

What CNC Equipment Can Be Financed?

New CNC machines. Buying from a dealer or OEM? Lenders routinely finance new CNC equipment. Get your quote, we match you with a lender, and you secure financing before you buy.

Used CNC machines. Used CNC machine financing is common. Lenders look at age, condition, and value. Terms may be a bit shorter than for new, but you can still finance used CNC equipment.

Private seller. Some of our lenders fund private-party purchases with a bill of sale and equipment details.

Dealer purchase. Dealer and OEM deals are the most common. We add options by matching you with our network so you can compare terms.

Terms & Requirements

Loan amounts. CNC machine loans can range from roughly $20,000 to $500,000+ depending on the machine. We match you with lenders that finance in your range.

Credit flexibility. We work with lenders that consider a range of credit. We also work with lenders that offer CNC machine financing for fair or lower credit when the deal supports it.

Time in business. Some lenders want to see a year or two in business; others work with newer shops. We match you based on your situation.

Down payment. Down payments of 10-20% are typical. More down can mean better approval or rate. We connect you with lenders that fit your budget.
