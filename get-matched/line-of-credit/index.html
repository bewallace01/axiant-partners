Important disclosures (business line of credit)

Rates, APR, fees, and terms. Business lines of credit are offered and funded by third-party lenders, not by Axiant Partners. Interest rates are often variable (for example, based on a benchmark such as prime or SOFR plus a margin). Your Annual Percentage Rate (APR), finance charge, repayment schedule, draw fees, maintenance or annual fees, and all other costs are disclosed only in the loan documents the lender provides. Review those documents before you accept an offer. For general educational information on how pricing often works, see typical business line of credit rates.

Illustrative cost range (not an offer or guarantee). In the small-business credit market, revolving lines are often marketed with APR figures that can range broadly by lender and risk profile—commonly roughly 10% to 45% APR in many programs, with some offers lower or higher. Your actual rate, APR, and fees depend on the lender’s underwriting and will appear only in your loan agreement.

Not a lender. Axiant Partners is not a direct lender. We are a matching service that connects business owners with third-party funding providers. We do not extend credit and do not make credit decisions.

Eligibility. Minimum 6 months in business and approximately $10,000 in average monthly revenue are typical thresholds for many programs in our network; requirements vary by lender and product. Not all applicants qualify.
