What Is Same Day Equipment Approval?

Same day equipment approval means you get a decision on your equipment loan or lease within the same business day-often within hours-when your application and any required documents are in order. Not every lender works that fast, but many of our partners do. If you're at a dealer, have a tight timeline, or just don't want to wait weeks, we match you with lenders that offer same day equipment approval so you can get funded and get the equipment.

What Equipment Can Get Same Day Approval?

Construction and heavy equipment. Excavators, loaders, dump trucks, skid steers-many of our lenders can turn these around same day when the application is complete and the deal fits their box.

Trucks and trailers. Commercial trucks, bucket trucks, and trailers are often eligible for fast approval. We match you with lenders that prioritize speed.

Other business equipment. Manufacturing, office, and other equipment can qualify. Approval speed depends on lender, amount, and documentation.

Same day doesn't mean every deal. Complex transactions or missing docs can slow things down. We get you in front of lenders that can move fast when the deal is ready.

How to Improve Your Chances of Same Day Approval

Have your info ready. Business name, EIN, revenue, time in business, equipment quote or details-the more complete your application, the faster we can get you in front of lenders.

Respond quickly. If a lender asks for a document or clarification, responding the same day helps keep the process on track.

Know your equipment. A clear quote or spec sheet helps. We match you with lenders that finance your type of equipment so there are no last-minute surprises.

Apply early in the day. Submitting in the morning gives lenders more time to review and decide the same day.
