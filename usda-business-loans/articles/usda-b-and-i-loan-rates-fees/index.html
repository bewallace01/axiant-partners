USDA B&I Loan Rates, Fees and Terms in 2026

The guarantee fee, the annual retention fee, the guarantee percentage by loan size, how rates are set and how long the loan can run, with the math done for you

← Back to USDA Business Loans Articles

Updated November 13, 2026

Quick answer

Under the FY2026 USDA notice, B&I loans carry a one-time 3.0% guarantee fee and a 0.55% annual retention fee, both on the guaranteed share; USDA guarantees 85% of loans under $5 million and 80% from $5 million to $25 million. Rates are negotiated with the lender, and terms run up to the assets' useful life, capped at 40 years. Axiant Partners, a broker in Boca Raton, compares B&I lenders. Rates here are illustrative.

The Fee and Guarantee Table

USDA sets these once a year in a Federal Register notice. The latest published is the FY2026 OneRD notice, effective October 1, 2025. Check for a newer notice before you close; the rates in effect when your guarantee is obligated stay with the loan.

B&I loan typeGuarantee feeAnnual retention feeGuarantee
Under $5 million3.0%0.55%85%
$5 million to $25 million3.0%0.55%80%
Reduced-fee projects1.0%0.50%80%
Guarantee before completionAdd 0.50%--

Both fees are charged on the guaranteed share, not the whole loan (7 CFR 5001.454, 5001.455). The lender pays USDA and may pass the cost to you. The rule caps the B&I guarantee fee at 5%, and the guarantee itself at 90%.

Who gets the reduced fee: the rule lists projects in distressed communities, places with long-term population decline, persistent poverty counties, recent disaster areas, places with unemployment at 125% or more of the national rate, and tribal lands, plus certain local food, value-added and regional plan projects. The lender has to request it, and annual limits apply.

What the Retention Fee Adds Up To

The retention fee is charged every year on the guaranteed share of the outstanding balance, so it shrinks as you pay down. On an assumed $4,000,000 loan at 8.00% over 25 years (85% guaranteed):

ItemAmount
First year$18,700
Over the full term$315,930
Same loan, reduced 0.50% fee$270,320
One-time guarantee fee$102,000

Retention fees not paid within 60 days of the due date are delinquent and can lead to cancellation of the guarantee, so lenders usually build the fee into your payment schedule.

How Rates Are Set

USDA doesn't publish B&I rates. Under 7 CFR 5001.401, the rate is negotiated between you and the lender. It can be fixed, variable or a mix, and:

  • it can't be more than the lender charges on similar loans without a guarantee;
  • a variable rate must follow a published base rate, such as Prime, and can adjust no more often than quarterly;
  • the guaranteed and unguaranteed parts can carry different rates.

With Prime at about 7.00%, here is what a $3,000,000 loan over 25 years costs at three illustrative rates:

Terms, Payments and Other Costs

  • Term. Set by the lender with USDA concurrence, up to the useful life of the assets financed or pledged, never more than 40 years (7 CFR 5001.402). Lenders commonly match shorter terms to equipment and working capital.
  • No balloons. The loan must fully amortize. Payments are at least annual.
  • Interest-only start. Allowed, but the first full payment must begin within 3 years.
  • Lender fees. Reasonable and customary, the same as on the lender's other loans; prepayment penalties are allowed if written into the documents.
  • Broker fees. Loan proceeds can't pay loan finder or broker fees (7 CFR 5001.122).
  • Third-party costs. Appraisals for real estate or existing equipment over $250,000, and a feasibility study when required.

For how these terms compare with SBA, see USDA B&I vs SBA 7(a). For whether your project qualifies at all, start with USDA Business & Industry loans.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the USDA B&amp;I guarantee fee in 2026?

3.0% of the guaranteed share under the FY2026 notice, or 1.0% for projects that qualify for the reduced fee. On a $3 million loan guaranteed at 85%, that's $76,500.

What is the USDA B&amp;I annual renewal fee?

It's called the periodic guarantee retention fee: 0.55% a year on the guaranteed share of the outstanding balance under the FY2026 notice, or 0.50% for reduced-fee projects.

What percentage does USDA guarantee on a B&amp;I loan?

85% on loans under $5 million and 80% from $5 million to $25 million under the FY2026 notice. The rule allows up to 90%.

What interest rate do USDA business loans have?

There's no USDA rate. It's negotiated with the lender, can be fixed or variable, and can't exceed what the lender charges on similar unguaranteed loans.

How long can a USDA B&amp;I loan be?

Up to the useful life of the assets financed or used as collateral, with a 40-year maximum and no balloon payment.

Can the fees be financed?

The lender pays the fees to USDA and may pass them to the borrower. Whether they're rolled into the loan is part of the lender's structure.
