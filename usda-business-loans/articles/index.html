Quick answer

USDA Business & Industry loans are bank loans with a USDA guarantee, for businesses outside cities of more than 50,000 people. They go up to $25 million per borrower, against $5 million on an SBA 7(a). Axiant Partners, a broker in Boca Raton, matches rural projects with lenders that use the program.

The B&I program is narrower than SBA lending on location and wider on size and term. These guides take one question each. Start with USDA business loans for the overview, or see SBA loans if your project is in a city.
