Add an Equipment & Truck Payment Calculator to your site—no cost, just keep the attribution link

Truck dealers, trucking blogs, equipment sites, and industry forums often want a payment calculator but don’t want to build one. We offer ours free for embedding. Visitors can estimate monthly payments for commercial trucks, equipment, or any business loan.

What You Get

  • Loan & Lease calculator (purchase price, down payment, residual, payment, rate, term)
  • Responsive design that works on mobile and desktop
  • No cost—free to use
  • Easy iframe embed—copy, paste, done

Embed Code

Copy and paste this into your page where you want the calculator to appear:

<iframe 
  src="https://www.axiantpartners.com/calculator-embed.html" 
  width="100%" 
  height="540" 
  frameborder="0" 
  style="max-width: 900px; border: 1px solid #ddd; border-radius: 8px;"
  title="Equipment & Truck Payment Calculator"></iframe>

Optional: Track Your Embeds

Add ?ref=your-site-id to the URL to track which sites send traffic. Example:

src="https://www.axiantpartners.com/calculator-embed.html?ref=truck-dealer-xyz"
