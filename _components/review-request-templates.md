# Review / testimonial request templates

Goal: collect **real** testimonials and Google reviews from funded clients.
Send the email 1–2 weeks after funding closes. Keep it short; one clear ask.

> Tip: a public Google review is best — it's verifiable, carries SEO weight,
> and you can quote it on the site (with first name + city). Even 2–3 real
> ones beat a wall of generic copy.

---

## Email — short version (recommended)

**Subject:** Quick favor, {{first_name}}?

Hi {{first_name}},

Glad we could help get {{business_name}} funded. If the experience was a good
one, would you mind leaving a quick review? It genuinely helps other owners
decide whether to work with us.

👉 {{google_review_link}}

Takes about 60 seconds. And if anything fell short, reply here instead — I'd
rather hear it directly so we can fix it.

Thanks,
{{rep_name}}
Axiant Partners · (919) 907-2611

---

## Email — with a couple prompts (if they're stuck on what to say)

**Subject:** Mind sharing how it went, {{first_name}}?

Hi {{first_name}},

Now that {{business_name}} is funded, a short review would mean a lot. Not sure
what to write? A sentence or two on any of these is perfect:

- What you needed the financing for
- What working with us was like vs. going bank-to-bank
- How fast / how simple it was

👉 Leave a review: {{google_review_link}}

If you'd rather I draft something for your approval, just reply and I will.

Thanks,
{{rep_name}}
Axiant Partners · (919) 907-2611

---

## Text / SMS — ultra short

Hi {{first_name}}, it's {{rep_name}} at Axiant. Glad we got {{business_name}}
funded! If you have 60 seconds, a quick review really helps: {{google_review_link}}
— and thank you!

---

## Consent line (use when quoting a review on the website)

Before publishing a name + quote on the site, confirm permission:

> "Mind if we feature your review on our website? We'd show your first name,
> last initial, and city (e.g., 'James M. · Charlotte, NC') — never full
> contact details."

Once they say yes, paste the quote into `_components/testimonials-section.html`.
