What Is Restaurant Prep Equipment?

Restaurant prep equipment includes prep tables (refrigerated and non-refrigerated), planetary mixers, vertical cutter mixers, meat slicers, food processors, and food prep tools. Prep tables provide work surface and often refrigeration. Mixers handle dough, batters, and bulk prep. Slicers and food processors support portioning and prep. Equipment supports daily operations across cuisine types. Brands like Hobart, Robot Coupe, Bizerba, True, and Globe are common. Prep equipment is essential for restaurants and food service.

How Do You Finance Prep Equipment?

Use equipment loans, leases, or vendor programs. Most lenders require credit 600+, down payment 0–20%, and proof of business revenue. Approval typically takes 1–5 days. Prep equipment is widely financed through restaurant equipment lenders.

How Much Does Prep Equipment Cost?

Prep tables run $500–$5,000+. Planetary mixers cost $2,000–$15,000+. Vertical cutter mixers run $5,000–$20,000+. Meat slicers cost $500–$5,000. Food processors run $500–$5,000. Full prep packages can exceed $30,000. Used equipment typically costs 20–35% less. Many lenders finance used equipment. Obtain a written vendor quote.

Financing Options for Prep Equipment

Equipment loans and leases both work. Loans require 0–20% down, terms of 36–60 months. Rates typically range from 6–15%. Leasing offers lower monthly payments. See equipment loan vs lease. Bundling with kitchen or refrigeration may improve terms.

Prep Equipment Financing FAQ

Can you finance used prep equipment?

Yes. Many lenders finance used prep tables, mixers, and slicers in good condition. Used equipment may require a larger down payment and shorter terms.

Can you bundle prep equipment with other kitchen equipment?

Yes. Bundling prep tables, mixers, and slicers with ranges or refrigeration may improve terms and simplify one application.

How long does prep equipment financing approval take?

Approval typically takes 1–5 business days for equipment loans and leases. SBA loans can take 30–60+ days.

Is leasing better than buying prep equipment?

It depends. Leasing offers lower monthly payments. Buying builds equity. Compare both based on your cash flow.
