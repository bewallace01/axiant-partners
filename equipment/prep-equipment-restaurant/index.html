Choosing the Right Prep Equipment for Your Kitchen

Before applying for prep equipment financing, clarify your menu, volume, and prep workflow. Mixers, slicers, food processors, prep tables, reach-ins, and work surfaces range from $1K to $30K+ depending on capacity. Brands like Hobart, Robot Coupe, Blendtec, Bizerba, and True are common. Get written quotes with make, model, and pricing. Compare new vs used—many lenders finance used prep equipment when condition is documented. Bundle with kitchen equipment, refrigeration, or warewashing for better terms. Use our financing calculator to estimate payments.

Documents That Speed Up Prep Equipment Approval

Have ready: bank statements (3–6 months), tax returns, P&L, and a quote listing mixers, slicers, prep tables, or processors—each with make, model, and price. Formation docs and proof of time in business round it out. Lenders focus on credit, revenue, and the equipment. Most complete applications get a response within 24–48 hours.

Prep Equipment: Tax Benefits and When Restaurants Finance

Prep tables, mixers, slicers, and food processors qualify for Section 179 and bonus depreciation; lease payments are typically deductible. See our equipment financing overview for more. New kitchens and capacity expansions are costly—financing keeps capital free for payroll and food costs. Leasing vs loans depends on your cash flow and upgrade plans.
