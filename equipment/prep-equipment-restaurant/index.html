Who May Run a Slicer or Mixer

Federal child-labor rules affect staffing, and staffing affects which machine to buy. Workers under 18 may not operate, set up, adjust or clean power-driven meat slicers and processing machines, whatever they slice, including cheese and vegetables in a deli (29 CFR 570.61). The same applies to power-driven dough and batter mixers (29 CFR 570.62), except small countertop mixers that are not hardwired, have a motor of 1/2 horsepower or less and a bowl of 5 quarts or less, and are not used for meat.

If teenagers staff your prep shift, plan for an adult on the slicer and floor mixer, or choose equipment the rules exempt. Many states add their own rules.

Refrigerated Prep Tables

Sandwich and pizza prep tables hold cold ingredients at the line, so they are refrigeration equipment as much as prep equipment. Ask for NSF/ANSI 7 listing (commercial refrigerators and freezers) on the quote; powered prep machines such as mixers and slicers are listed to NSF/ANSI 8. Health inspectors look for the listing, and lenders look for a commercial model on a dealer quote rather than household equipment.

Walk-ins and reach-ins are on restaurant refrigeration financing.

What Lenders Ask For

  • A dealer quote listing each machine with make, model and price.
  • 3-6 months of bank statements; at this size that is often the whole file.
  • Time in business: 1-2+ years is typical. A new operator may need a larger down payment or to finance prep with the full opening package.
  • Used machines: many lenders finance refurbished mixers and slicers sold by a dealer with a written quote and condition noted; private sales are harder.

Prep Equipment Financing FAQ

How much does it cost to finance restaurant prep equipment?

At an assumed 10%, a $33,000 prep package is about $837 a month over 48 months and a $5,000 machine about $161 a month over 36 months. Illustrations, not quotes.

Can I finance one mixer or slicer by itself?

Yes. Single machines are often approved on an application and a dealer quote. Several machines on one quote usually mean one approval and one payment instead of several.

Can a 16- or 17-year-old employee use a meat slicer?

No. Federal rules bar workers under 18 from operating, setting up or cleaning power-driven meat slicers and processing machines, including slicing cheese or vegetables (29 CFR 570.61).

Which mixers can minors use?

Only small countertop mixers that are not hardwired, have a motor of 1/2 horsepower or less and a bowl of 5 quarts or less, and are not used for meat (29 CFR 570.62). Floor dough mixers are off limits under 18.

Can a new restaurant finance prep equipment?

Often, as part of the opening package. Most lenders prefer 1-2+ years in business, so a new operator may need a larger down payment.

How long are prep equipment financing terms?

Commonly 36-60 months. Single small machines tend toward the shorter end.

Sources & Further Reading

  • 29 CFR 570.61 (Hazardous Occupations Order 10) — Power-driven meat slicers and processing machines: no operation, setup or cleaning by workers under 18.
  • 29 CFR 570.62 (Hazardous Occupations Order 11) — Power-driven bakery machines and dough mixers; the countertop mixer exemption.
  • NSF food equipment standards — NSF/ANSI 7 (commercial refrigerators and freezers) and NSF/ANSI 8 (commercial powered food preparation equipment).
  • IRS Publication 946 — Depreciation and the Section 179 deduction for business equipment.
