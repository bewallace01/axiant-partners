Summary

Zero-turn mower financing—including zero-turn loans and zero-turn leases—helps landscapers and lawn care businesses add or replace commercial mowers without draining working capital. Zero-turns are commonly financed through equipment lenders and SBA programs.

Costs typically run $6,000–$15,000+ for new commercial zero-turns; used zero-turn financing costs less. Equipment financing spreads payments over 36–60 months.
