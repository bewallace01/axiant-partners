How to Finance a POS System

Costs, loan options, requirements, and approval process for point-of-sale systems and payment terminals

Many restaurants finance POS systems to upgrade or add locations without large upfront outlays. Equipment loans and leases spread payments and preserve cash for inventory and payroll.

What Is a Restaurant POS System?

Restaurant POS (point-of-sale) systems include terminals, tablets, handhelds, payment processors, receipt printers, and kitchen display systems. POS hardware handles orders and transactions. Software manages menus, inventory, and reporting. Payment terminals process cards and contactless payments. Equipment supports front-of-house and kitchen operations. Brands like Toast, Square, Clover, TouchBistro, and Aloha are common. POS systems are essential for restaurants and food service.

How Do You Finance a POS System?

Use equipment loans, leases, or vendor programs. Most lenders require credit 600+, down payment 0–20%, and proof of business revenue. Approval typically takes 1–5 days. POS systems are widely financed—many POS vendors offer in-house or partner financing.

How Much Does a POS System Cost?

Basic POS setups run $2,000–$5,000. Mid-range systems cost $5,000–$10,000. Full-service setups with multiple terminals and KDS run $10,000–$15,000+. Software subscriptions add monthly fees. Used hardware typically costs 20–35% less. Many lenders finance used equipment. Obtain a written vendor quote.

Financing Options for POS Systems

Equipment loans and leases both work. Loans require 0–20% down, terms of 24–48 months. Rates typically range from 6–15%. Leasing offers lower monthly payments and can include technology refresh. See equipment loan vs lease. Vendor programs may offer promotional terms.

POS System Financing FAQ

Can you finance POS software or just hardware?

Most equipment financing covers hardware. Some lenders and vendor programs bundle hardware and software. Check with your lender or POS vendor.

Can you finance POS for multiple locations?

Yes. Many lenders finance multi-location POS deployments. Bundle equipment for several locations in one application.

How long does POS financing approval take?

Approval typically takes 1–5 business days for equipment loans and leases.

Is leasing better than buying a POS system?

It depends. Leasing offers lower monthly payments and can include upgrades. Buying builds equity. Technology refresh may favor leasing.
