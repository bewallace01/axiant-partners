Summary

POS system financing—including point-of-sale loans and payment terminal leases—helps restaurants add or upgrade POS hardware without draining working capital. POS systems are commonly financed through equipment lenders, vendor programs, and SBA programs.

Costs typically run $2,000–$15,000+ for hardware and software; used POS equipment financing costs less. Equipment financing spreads payments over 24–48 months.
