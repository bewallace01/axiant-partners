Hay Equipment Financing

Equipment loans and leases for hay balers, mowers, rakes, and tedders—hay and forage operations

Last updated: March 13, 2026

Hay equipment—balers, mower-conditioners, rakes, tedders—is essential for hay and forage operations. A full hay package can run $40,000-$150,000+; hay equipment financing lets farmers add or replace equipment without paying cash. This guide covers costs, financing options, and how to bundle a hay package. See tractor financing and combine financing. Get matched with lenders who finance hay equipment.

Why Farmers Finance Hay Equipment

Hay equipment is high-capital. Financing spreads the cost over 36-84 months. Bundling mower, rake, and baler in one package simplifies approval. See equipment financing by type.

Hay Equipment Costs

Round balers run roughly $15,000-$50,000+; square balers $20,000-$80,000+; mower-conditioners $15,000-$50,000; rakes and tedders $5,000-$25,000. Full packages $40,000-$150,000+. Get a written quote. See equipment financing requirements.

Financing Options: Loans vs Leases

Equipment loans spread the cost; you own at payoff. Leases often have lower payments. See equipment loan vs lease. Use our calculator.

Bundling a Hay Package

Many lenders finance hay equipment packages—mower-conditioner, rake, baler—in one loan or lease. Bundling simplifies approval and payments. Include itemized quote from your dealer.
