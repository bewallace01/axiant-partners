Auto Shop Equipment Package Financing

Bundled equipment loans and leases for lifts, tools, storage, and diagnostic—new and expanding repair shops

Last updated: March 13, 2026

Opening or expanding an auto repair shop requires multiple equipment types—lifts, scan tools, tire equipment, tool boxes, storage. Auto shop equipment package financing lets you bundle these into one loan or lease, simplifying approval and payments. Packages typically run $15,000-$100,000+; this guide covers what to include, how to structure the package, and credit requirements. See auto lift financing and scan tool financing. What to Include in an Equipment Package

Lifts (two-post, four-post, alignment racks), scan tools, tire changers and balancers, tool boxes and workbenches, storage cabinets, brake lathes, alignment equipment, and diagnostic devices. Lenders finance bundled packages when items have clear equipment value and are itemized. Get a detailed quote from your supplier with make, model, and price for each item. Avoid lump-sum "shop package" quotes—itemize for better lender evaluation. See equipment financing by type.

Benefits of Package Financing

One application, one payment, streamlined approval. Bundling may improve terms versus financing small items separately—lenders prefer packages that show a complete, operational shop. A $50,000 package (lifts, scan tool, tire equipment, storage) often qualifies for better rates than three separate $10K-$20K financings. Single credit pull, single payment stream. See equipment financing requirements.

Typical Package Sizes and Costs

New shop packages typically run $15,000-$100,000+. A basic setup: two two-post lifts ($8K-$16K), scan tool ($4K-$10K), tire equipment ($3K-$8K), tool storage ($2K-$5K)—roughly $20K-$40K. Full fit-out with alignment rack, multiple lifts, and premium diagnostic: $75K-$100K+. Match the package to your service mix and capacity.

Financing Options: Loans vs Leases

Equipment loans spread the cost; you own at payoff and can depreciate. Leases often have lower monthly payments and full payment deductibility; at term end you can purchase or return. New shops often prefer leasing to preserve capital. Bundled packages work with both structures. See equipment loan vs lease. Use our calculator to model payments.

Credit and Down Payment Requirements

Most lenders look for 600+ FICO; 680+ qualifies for the best rates. New shops may rely on personal credit; equipment is asset-backed. Down payments: 0-15% for qualified applicants. Bundled packages can improve approval odds—they show a serious, well-planned investment. See credit score for equipment financing and equipment financing for new businesses.

Documentation for Package Financing

Gather: itemized equipment quote (make, model, price for each item), 3-6 months personal and business bank statements, tax returns, P&L, formation documents, business plan for new shops. Complete documentation speeds approval. Lenders want to see the package is real, priced, and ready to fund. See equipment financing approval requirements.

Approval Timeline

Many lenders approve auto shop equipment packages within 24-48 hours when documentation and itemized quote are complete. Bundled packages often move faster than single-item requests. See approval timeline. Get matched with lenders who finance shop equipment packages.
