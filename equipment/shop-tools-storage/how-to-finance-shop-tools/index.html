What Are Shop Tools & Storage?

Shop tools and storage include tool chests, roll cabinets, air compressors, specialty wrenches, socket sets, and shop storage systems. Tool chests and roll cabinets organize hand tools and keep workspaces efficient. Air compressors power impact wrenches, paint sprayers, and pneumatic tools. Shop storage includes shelving, parts bins, and workbenches. Brands like Snap-on, Matco, Mac Tools, and Husky are common. Shop tools are essential for auto repair shops and mechanical operations.

How Do You Finance Shop Tools & Storage?

Use equipment loans, leases, or bundle with other shop equipment. Most lenders require credit 600+, down payment 0–20%, and proof of revenue. Approval typically takes 1–5 days. Shop tools are common equipment—financing is widely available through auto repair lenders.

How Much Do Shop Tools & Storage Cost?

Tool chests and roll cabinets run $1,000–$15,000+ depending on size and brand. Air compressors cost $500–$10,000+. Specialty tool sets run $500–$5,000. Full shop packages and storage systems can reach $50,000+. Used equipment typically costs 20–35% less. Many lenders finance used equipment. Obtain a written quote.

Financing Options for Shop Tools & Storage

Equipment loans and leases both work. Loans require 0–20% down, terms of 24–60 months. Rates typically range from 6–15%. Leasing offers lower monthly payments. See equipment loan vs lease. Bundling with lifts, diagnostic equipment, or tire equipment may improve terms.

Shop Tools & Storage Financing FAQ

Can you finance used shop tools and tool chests?

Yes. Many lenders finance used tool chests, air compressors, and shop storage in good condition. Used equipment may require a larger down payment and shorter terms.

Can you bundle shop tools with other equipment?

Yes. Bundling tool chests, air compressors, and storage with lifts or diagnostic equipment may improve terms and simplify one application.

How long does shop tools financing approval take?

Approval typically takes 1–5 business days for equipment loans and leases. SBA loans can take 30–60+ days.

Is leasing better than buying shop tools?

It depends. Leasing offers lower monthly payments. Buying builds equity. Compare both based on your cash flow and long-term plans.
