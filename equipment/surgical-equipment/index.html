Summary

Surgical equipment financing—including surgical light loans, operating table leases, and scope financing—helps healthcare practices and ASCs add or replace procedure equipment without draining working capital. Surgical equipment is commonly financed through equipment lenders and SBA programs.

Costs typically run $10,000–$200,000+ depending on type; used surgical equipment financing costs less. Equipment financing spreads payments over 36–84 months.
