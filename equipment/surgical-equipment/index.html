Choosing the Right Surgical Equipment for Your OR or Procedure Room

Before applying for surgical equipment financing, clarify your procedure volume, room count, and specialty needs. Surgical lights, operating tables, scopes (endoscopes, arthroscopes, laparoscopes), monitors, and anesthesia systems have different price points and upgrade cycles. Brands such as Stryker, Skytron, Maquet, and Karl Storz dominate—get written quotes that specify make, model, and pricing. Bundled OR packages often improve financing terms and simplify installation. Compare new vs used—many lenders finance used surgical equipment when sterilization, regulatory, and safety requirements are met. Plan for installation, integration with EHR or video systems, and staff training. Use our financing calculator to estimate monthly payments before you apply.

Pre-Approval Checklist for OR and Surgical Equipment

OR packages are large and lenders like to see full financials. Have ready: 3–6 months of practice or ASC bank statements; tax returns; P&L; a line-item quote (lights, tables, scopes, monitors) with makes, models, and prices; formation docs; and proof of time in business. Facility accreditation or licensure helps when it applies. Deals over $100K may trigger extra review. Stryker, Karl Storz, and other vendors sometimes offer their own financing—compare those terms with general equipment financing. Approval depends on credit, revenue, and the equipment itself.

Tax Treatment for Surgical Equipment

Surgical lights, tables, scopes, and anesthesia systems usually qualify for Section 179 and bonus depreciation. Lease payments are typically fully deductible. Full details are in our equipment financing overview—consult your CPA for your situation. ASCs often finance partly to preserve capital for staffing and supplies while still capturing these benefits.

Why ORs and ASCs Often Finance Instead of Paying Cash

Building or upgrading an OR is expensive. Financing spreads the cost so capital stays available for payroll, supplies, and growth. Leasing tends to make sense when technology moves fast—LED lights, 4K video, and scope advances can justify updates every 5–7 years. Big packages ($150K–$200K+) may qualify for SBA or term loan options. Compare leasing vs loans for your cash flow and upgrade plans.

Surgical Equipment Financing FAQ

Can you finance used surgical equipment?

Yes. Many lenders finance used surgical lights, tables, some scopes in good condition. Regulatory and sterilization apply.

Does equipment type affect financing?

Lights and tables typically shorter terms. Scopes and specialty equipment may have longer terms.

How long does approval take?

1–5 days equipment. Larger deals or SBA 30–60+ days.

Is leasing better than buying?

Leasing: capital preservation, upgrades. Buying: equity.
