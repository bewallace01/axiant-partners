What Is Surgical & Procedure Equipment?

Surgical and procedure equipment includes surgical lights, operating tables, scopes (endoscopes, arthroscopes), monitors, and anesthesia systems. Surgical lights provide adjustable illumination for procedures. Operating tables support patient positioning. Scopes enable minimally invasive procedures. Monitors display vital signs and imaging. Equipment supports ambulatory surgery centers (ASCs), specialty offices, and hospital procedure rooms. Brands like Stryker, Skytron, Maquet, and Karl Storz are common. Surgical equipment is essential for medical practices and ASCs performing procedures.

How Do You Finance Surgical & Procedure Equipment?

Use equipment loans, leases, or vendor programs. Most lenders require credit 600+, down payment 0–20%, and proof of practice revenue. Approval typically takes 1–5 days. Surgical equipment is widely financed through healthcare equipment lenders.

How Much Does Surgical & Procedure Equipment Cost?

Surgical lights run $5,000–$30,000+. Operating tables cost $10,000–$75,000+. Scopes and video systems run $15,000–$100,000+. Monitors and anesthesia equipment add $5,000–$50,000+. Full procedure room packages can exceed $200,000. Used equipment typically costs 20–40% less. Many lenders finance used equipment. Obtain a written vendor quote.

Financing Options for Surgical Equipment

Equipment loans and leases both work. Loans require 0–20% down, terms of 36–84 months for higher-ticket assets. Rates typically range from 6–15%. Leasing offers lower monthly payments and can include technology refresh. See equipment loan vs lease. Vendor financing programs may offer promotional terms.

Surgical Equipment Financing FAQ

Can you finance used surgical equipment?

Yes. Many lenders finance used surgical lights, tables, and some scopes in good condition. Used equipment may require a larger down payment and shorter terms. Regulatory and sterilization requirements apply.

Does equipment type affect financing terms?

Yes. Surgical lights and tables typically have shorter terms. Scopes and specialty equipment may have longer terms. Lenders specialize in medical equipment.

How long does surgical equipment financing approval take?

Approval typically takes 1–5 business days for equipment loans and leases. Larger deals or SBA-backed financing can take 30–60+ days.

Is leasing better than buying surgical equipment?

It depends. Leasing preserves capital and can include upgrades. Buying builds equity. Technology refresh cycles often make leasing attractive for surgical equipment.
