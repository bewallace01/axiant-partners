Excavator Financing

Excavator financing—including excavator loans and excavator leases—helps construction and earthmoving companies add or replace heavy equipment without draining working capital. Excavators are commonly financed through equipment lenders and SBA programs.

Costs typically run $80,000–$500,000+ for new equipment depending on size; used excavator financing costs less. Equipment financing spreads payments over 36–60 months.
