Summary

Box truck financing—including box truck loans and box truck leases—helps delivery, moving, and logistics businesses add or replace trucks without draining working capital. Box trucks are commonly financed through equipment lenders and SBA programs.

Costs typically run $55,000–$100,000+ for new trucks; used box truck financing costs less. Equipment financing spreads payments over 36–60 months.
