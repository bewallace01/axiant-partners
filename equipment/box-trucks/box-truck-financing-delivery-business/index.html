Box Truck Financing for Delivery Businesses

Equipment loans and leases for straight trucks—last-mile, freight, and local delivery

← Back to Box Truck Financing | All Articles

Last updated: March 13, 2026

Box trucks—straight trucks with enclosed cargo bodies—are the backbone of last-mile delivery, local freight, moving companies, and distribution operations. New units run $35,000-$95,000; box truck financing lets delivery businesses add capacity without paying cash. This guide covers costs, financing options (loans vs leases), credit and down payment requirements, and how to get approved. See equipment financing for new businesses if you're just starting. Why Delivery Businesses Use Box Trucks

Box trucks fit routes that semi-trucks cannot: urban streets, residential neighborhoods, and delivery docks with limited access. E-commerce, local freight, moving companies, and wholesale distributors rely on box trucks for daily operations. One financed box truck can expand your delivery radius, add routes, or replace an aging unit. See equipment financing by type for related truck categories.

Box Truck Costs: New vs Used

New box trucks run roughly $35,000-$95,000 depending on cargo length (12-26 ft), GVWR, and chassis (e.g., Ford, Freightliner, Isuzu). Used 3-5 year old units typically cost 25-40% less. Lease-to-own programs also exist. Get a written quote with make, model, year, VIN, and specs. See financing used equipment.

Financing Options: Loans vs Leases

Equipment loans spread the cost over 36-72 months; you own the truck at payoff. Leases often have lower payments and full payment deductibility; you can purchase at term end or upgrade. For delivery businesses scaling up, leasing offers flexibility. See equipment loan vs lease. Use our calculator to model payments.

Credit and Down Payment Requirements

Most lenders look for 600+ FICO; 680+ qualifies for the best rates. Down payments: 0-15% for new, 10-20% for used. Time in business and revenue matter—lenders want to see your delivery business can support the payment. See credit score for equipment financing and down payment requirements.

Documentation and Approval Timeline

Gather: truck quote (make, model, year, VIN, price), 3-6 months bank statements, tax returns, P&L, formation documents. CDL requirements depend on GVWR. Complete documentation speeds approval. Many lenders approve in 24-48 hours. See equipment financing requirements and approval timeline.

Rates and Monthly Payments

Rates typically run 6-14%. For a $55,000 box truck at 8% over 60 months with 10% down, expect roughly $950-$1,050/month. Use our calculator. See typical equipment financing rates.

Choosing the Right Box Truck for Delivery

Consider cargo length, GVWR (affects CDL requirements), liftgate need, and fuel type. 12-16 ft trucks suit local delivery; 18-26 ft suit freight and moving. Diesel typically offers better fuel economy for high-mileage routes.

New vs Used Box Trucks

Used box trucks 3-5 years old can save 25-40%. Lenders typically finance used trucks up to 5-7 years old. Get a pre-purchase inspection; document mileage and maintenance. See financing used equipment.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a box truck cost for delivery?

New box trucks run $35,000-$95,000 depending on size and GVWR. Used 3-5 year old units may be 25-40% less.

Can I finance a box truck for a new delivery business?

Some lenders work with new businesses; others prefer 6-12+ months in business. Strong personal credit, down payment, and business plan improve approval.

What credit score do I need for box truck financing?

Most lenders look for 600+ FICO. Scores of 680+ qualify for the best rates. Some programs work with 580+ when revenue and down payment support the application.

Lease or buy a box truck for delivery?

Leases offer lower payments and upgrade flexibility; loans build equity. Match the structure to your growth plans.

How fast can I get approved for box truck financing?

Many lenders approve within 24-48 hours when documentation is complete. Marketplace applications can return multiple offers in 1-3 business days.
