Summary

Tractor financing—including tractor loans and tractor leases—helps farmers and ranchers add or replace row-crop, utility, and specialty tractors without draining working capital. Tractors are commonly financed through equipment lenders and SBA programs.

Costs typically run $80,000–$400,000+ for new tractors; used tractor financing costs less. Equipment financing spreads payments over 36–84 months.
