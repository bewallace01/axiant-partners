What Are Commercial Dishwashers & Warewashing Equipment?

Commercial dishwashers and warewashing equipment include pass-through dishwashers, undercounter dishwashers, flight-type dishwashers, pot sinks, and pre-rinse units. Pass-through and flight-type units handle high volume. Undercounter units suit smaller operations. Pot sinks support manual washing of large items. Equipment meets health codes and volume needs. Brands like Hobart, Ecolab, Champion, and Jackson are common. Warewashing is essential for restaurants and food service.

How Do You Finance Commercial Dishwashers?

Use equipment loans, leases, or vendor programs. Most lenders require credit 600+, down payment 0–20%, and proof of business revenue. Approval typically takes 1–5 days. Warewashing equipment is widely financed through restaurant equipment lenders.

How Much Do Commercial Dishwashers Cost?

Undercounter dishwashers run $3,000–$8,000. Pass-through units cost $8,000–$20,000+. Flight-type dishwashers run $15,000–$25,000+. Pot sinks and pre-rinse add $1,000–$5,000. Used equipment typically costs 20–35% less. Many lenders finance used equipment. Obtain a written vendor quote.

Financing Options for Commercial Dishwashers

Equipment loans and leases both work. Loans require 0–20% down, terms of 36–60 months. Rates typically range from 6–15%. Leasing offers lower monthly payments. See equipment loan vs lease. Bundling with kitchen or refrigeration may improve terms.

Commercial Dishwasher Financing FAQ

Can you finance used commercial dishwashers?

Yes. Many lenders finance used dishwashers and warewashing equipment in good condition. Used equipment may require a larger down payment and shorter terms.

Low-temp vs high-temp dishwasher—does it affect financing?

Lenders finance both. High-temp units may cost more. Capacity and compliance matter more than specific type for financing.

How long does dishwasher financing approval take?

Approval typically takes 1–5 business days for equipment loans and leases. SBA loans can take 30–60+ days.

Is leasing better than buying a commercial dishwasher?

It depends. Leasing offers lower monthly payments. Buying builds equity. Compare both based on your cash flow.
