Summary

Commercial kitchen financing—including range loans, oven leases, and charbroiler financing—helps restaurants outfit or upgrade kitchens without draining working capital. Kitchen equipment is commonly financed through equipment lenders and SBA programs.

Costs typically run $5,000–$200,000+ depending on configuration; used kitchen equipment financing costs less. Equipment financing spreads payments over 36–60 months.
