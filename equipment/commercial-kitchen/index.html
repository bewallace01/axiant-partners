Quick Answer: How Restaurant Equipment Financing Works

Updated November 18, 2026

Restaurant equipment financing is a loan or lease secured by the kitchen equipment you're buying. The lender pays the seller, you make fixed monthly payments, and the equipment is the collateral. Because used kitchen equipment resells for less than a truck or an excavator, lenders price it a little higher and lean more on your credit and deposits.

  • Typical amounts: $25,000 to $150,000 for most restaurants
  • Rates: about 7-22% APR by credit tier, usually 1-3 points above the same tier for equipment that resells easily (see the table)
  • Terms: 2 to 7 years, longer with stronger credit
  • Down payment: 0-20% for most borrowers; $0 down usually takes about 680+ credit and 2+ years in business
  • Credit: 600+ opens most equipment lenders
  • Speed: initial approval typically in 24-72 hours

What Lenders Look At for a Restaurant

You pick the equipment and get a quote or invoice from the dealer. The lender reviews four things:

  • Your personal credit. It's the biggest factor. A 720 and a 620 get very different offers on the same range.
  • Time in business. Two or more years opens the best programs. Six to 24 months still qualifies, at higher rates and shorter terms.
  • Revenue. Most programs want to see about $10,000 or more a month in deposits. Steady card sales help.
  • The equipment. New equipment from an established dealer gets the best terms. Kitchen equipment is a softer asset than trucks or construction machines, which is why it usually costs 1-3 points more.

What Credit Score Do You Need?

There's no set minimum score. In practice:

  • 600+ FICO opens most equipment lenders.
  • 550-599 usually means a specialty lender.
  • Below 550, you'll typically need 25%+ down or a cosigner.

Trucks and construction machines can lower the credit bar because they resell easily. Kitchen equipment usually doesn't get that break, so strong deposits and time in business matter more here.

Under 600? Start with equipment financing with bad credit.

Can You Finance Kitchen Equipment With No Money Down?

Yes, if your profile is strong. 100% financing is common with about 680+ credit and 2+ years in business, buying new or late-model equipment from an established dealer. A 650-679 score sometimes qualifies with strong revenue.

Used equipment usually needs 10-20% down, and a $0-down deal often costs a point or two more than the same deal with money down.

What Restaurant Equipment Can You Finance?

Most equipment that runs the kitchen or the front of house, from one fryer to a full build-out:

  • Cooking line: ranges, convection and combi ovens, fryers, griddles, charbroilers and stoves.
  • Refrigeration: reach-ins, prep tables, ice machines and walk-ins. See restaurant refrigeration financing.
  • Dishwashing: undercounter and conveyor machines. See dishwasher and warewashing financing.
  • Prep: mixers, slicers and food processors. See prep equipment financing.
  • Hoods and ventilation: see hood system financing.
  • POS systems: see POS financing.
  • Food trucks: see food truck financing.

Bundling several pieces into one deal keeps it to one application and one payment. Ask whether installation can go into the financed amount; some lenders allow it.

Financing Used Restaurant Equipment

Used kitchen equipment can save a lot, and most equipment lenders will finance it. Expect a few differences:

  • Rate: about 1-3 points higher than new.
  • Down payment: 10-20% on older equipment.
  • Age: usually under 10-15 years, and many lenders cap the equipment's age plus the loan term at about 15 years.
  • Paperwork: photos, serial numbers and sometimes an inspection.

Equipment Financing for a New Restaurant

A newer restaurant can still qualify, because the equipment secures the loan. Many programs accept businesses with 6+ months of history, 660-680+ credit and steady revenue. Expect about 12-22% APR and 10-20% down, or a personal guarantor.

If you haven't opened yet, start with restaurant opening equipment financing. For a full build-out of $250K or more, compare an SBA loan: a lower long-term rate, but a 6-12 week wait and a personal guarantee (equipment financing vs an SBA loan).

When It Makes Sense, and When It Doesn't

Financing usually wins when the equipment will earn its keep for years and you'd rather keep cash for payroll, inventory and slow weeks. It isn't always the best fit:

  • Small, quick purchases. A business credit card can be faster, especially with a 0% intro APR you'll pay off before it ends.
  • Large build-outs that can wait. On $250K or more, an SBA loan's lower rate can outweigh the wait.
  • Gear you'll replace soon, like POS hardware. A lease usually beats a loan.

If what you need is cash for payroll or inventory rather than equipment, see restaurant working capital loans.

Restaurant Equipment Financing FAQs

What credit score do I need for restaurant equipment financing?

There's no set minimum. A 600+ FICO opens most equipment lenders, 550-599 usually means a specialty lender, and below 550 you'll typically need 25%+ down or a cosigner. Kitchen equipment resells for less than trucks or construction machines, so don't expect a break on the score for the equipment itself. Credit score requirements, in detail.

What are restaurant equipment financing rates?

Equipment rates run about 7-22% APR by credit tier, and restaurant equipment usually prices 1-3 points higher than the same tier on equipment that resells easily. A 720+ borrower with 2+ years in business might see around 10%; a 600s score, mid-to-high teens or more.

Can I finance restaurant equipment with no money down?

Yes, if your profile is strong: about 680+ credit and 2+ years in business, buying new equipment from an established dealer. Used equipment usually needs 10-20% down, and a $0-down deal often costs a point or two more. How no-money-down financing works.

Is it better to lease or buy restaurant equipment?

Buy with a loan if you'll keep it for its useful life, like a range, oven or walk-in. Lease if you'll upgrade it, like a POS system, or want a lower payment; lease payments usually run about 10-20% lower. A $1 buyout lease works like rent-to-own. Commercial kitchen lease vs buy.

Can I finance used restaurant equipment?

Yes. Expect a rate about 1-3 points higher than new and 10-20% down on older equipment, and the lender may ask for photos, serial numbers or an inspection. Financing used equipment.

Can a new restaurant get equipment financing?

Usually once you have about 6+ months of operating history, 660-680+ credit and steady deposits. Under 2 years, expect about 12-22% APR and 10-20% down, or a personal guarantor. If you haven't opened yet, see restaurant opening equipment financing.

How fast can I get approved?

Initial approvals typically come back in 24-72 hours, and funding usually lands within 5-10 business days in total. A complete file (the equipment quote, 3-6 months of bank statements and a signed application) moves fastest. What speeds up approval.
