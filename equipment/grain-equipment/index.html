Grain Equipment Financing Options

Several financing structures work. Choose based on cash flow, tax situation, and whether bins are equipment or real property.

Equipment Loans

Most common for portable equipment (augers, conveyors) and some bin systems. 0–20% down, terms 24–84 months. Rates 6–15%. Equipment financing.

Equipment Leasing

Lower monthly payments. At lease end, return, purchase at fair market value, or upgrade. Operating leases treat payments as operating expenses.

SBA and Ag Real Estate

SBA 504 and agricultural real estate loans can finance grain bins when treated as real property. Terms 10–20 years. USDA guaranteed loans also support grain storage.

Working capital loans suit operating expenses. Use equipment financing for grain equipment to secure better rates tied to the asset.

How Much Does Grain Equipment Cost?

New grain bins range from roughly $2–4 per bushel (e.g., $20,000–$40,000 for 10,000 bushels). Augers and conveyors cost $3,000–$25,000+. Grain dryers $15,000–$80,000+. A complete system can run $50,000–$200,000+. Used grain equipment typically costs 30–50% less. Many lenders finance used equipment. Obtain a written quote·lenders use it to structure financing. Tips to Get Approved

  • Improve your credit score. Pay down balances, correct errors, avoid new credit.
  • Provide strong revenue documentation. Clean bank statements and organized financials.
  • Consider a complete package. Financing bins, augers, conveyors together can simplify and improve terms.
  • Make a larger down payment. Reduces lender risk.
  • Clarify equipment vs real estate. If financing bins, ask whether the lender treats them as equipment or real property.
  • Work with a broker. Apply through Axiant to connect with multiple lenders.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Skipping the equipment quote. Lenders need it to structure the loan.
  • Applying with incomplete financials. Missing documents cause delays.
  • Focusing on rate alone. Terms, fees, flexibility matter.
  • Waiting until the last minute. Rush approvals may limit options.
  • Not clarifying equipment vs real estate. Bins may be financed differently than augers and conveyors.

Grain Equipment Financing FAQ

Can you finance used grain equipment?

Yes. Many lenders finance used grain bins, augers, and conveyors. Age, capacity, and condition affect approval.

What credit score is required?

Most lenders look for 600 or higher. 680+ qualifies for the best rates.

Can you finance grain bins and augers together?

Yes. Lenders often finance a complete package·bins, augers, conveyors, dryers·as a single equipment loan.

Is grain equipment considered real estate or equipment?

Portable equipment (augers, conveyors) is typically equipment. Grain bins may be equipment or real estate depending on lender and whether affixed. Clarify with your lender.

How long does approval take?

1–5 business days for equipment loans. SBA and ag real estate add 30–60+ days.

What documents are needed?

3–6 months of bank statements, tax returns, profit and loss, equipment quote, and business formation documents.
