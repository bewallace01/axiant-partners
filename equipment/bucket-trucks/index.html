What Is a Bucket Truck?

A bucket truck (aerial lift truck, digger derrick, or cherry picker) is a commercial vehicle with a hydraulic or electric boom and work platform (bucket) that elevates workers to overhead heights. Bucket trucks are used for power line work, tree trimming, cable and fiber installation, and other elevated tasks. Working heights range from about 35 feet to 65+ feet depending on boom type and configuration.

Bucket trucks serve utilities (electric, gas, telecom), tree care and arborist companies, landscaping firms, and sign installers. Understanding what bucket trucks are and how they're used helps lenders assess your application-they want to see that the equipment supports revenue-generating work. Boom type (articulating vs telescopic), chassis, and working height affect suitability and cost.

Why Bucket Truck Financing Is Different

Lenders view bucket truck financing favorably because aerial lift trucks hold value well and serve essential industries. Bucket Truck Financing Options

Several financing structures work for bucket trucks. Choose based on cash flow, tax situation, and whether you want to own the truck at the end of the term.

Working capital loans are better suited for payroll, insurance, and operating expenses. Use working capital for day-to-day needs; use equipment financing for the bucket truck itself to secure better rates and terms tied to the asset. Compare equipment loan vs lease in detail.

Equipment Loans

Borrow a set amount, make fixed monthly payments, and own the bucket truck when the loan is paid off. Typically 0-20% down, terms 24-72 months. Interest rates 6-15% depending on credit. Ideal if you plan to use the truck long-term. See typical rates.

Equipment Leasing

Lower monthly payments than loans. At lease end, return the truck, purchase at fair market value, or upgrade. Operating leases treat payments as operating expenses. Leasing suits businesses that want to preserve cash flow or upgrade regularly. Loan vs lease.

SBA Loans

SBA 7(a) and 504 loans offer longer terms (7-10+ years) and lower down payments. Approval typically 30-60+ days. Best for established tree care, utility, or telecom businesses planning fleet expansion. View SBA loans.

How Much Does a Bucket Truck Cost?

Bucket truck prices vary by working height, boom type, chassis, and whether you buy new or used. New bucket trucks range from roughly $100,000-$150,000 for mid-height units (35-45') to $150,000-$200,000+ for high-reach models (55-65+'). Top brands like Altec, Terex, and Versalift command premium prices. Digger derrick configurations (with auger) add cost. Chassis (medium-duty vs heavy-duty) affects total price.

Used bucket trucks typically cost 30-50% less. A 5-year-old unit might run $60,000-$120,000. Boom and hydraulic condition are critical-inspect before purchasing. Many lenders finance used bucket trucks up to 5-7 years old. Obtain a written quote from your dealer-lenders use it to structure financing. Financing used equipment guide.

What to Have Ready Before You Apply

Gathering documentation before you start speeds approval. Have these ready when applying for bucket truck financing:

  • 3-6 months of business bank statements - Lenders want to see consistent deposits and cash flow.
  • Last year's tax returns - Business and personal if required by the lender.
  • Recent profit and loss statement - Shows revenue and expenses.
  • Equipment quote or proposal - Written quote from your dealer with make, model, working height, VIN, and total cost.
  • Business formation documents - Articles of incorporation, operating agreement, or similar.
  • Basic business information - EIN, formation date, ownership structure.

Lenders may ask for a voided check for ACH. The more organized your documentation, the faster you'll get funded. What do lenders look at when approving equipment financing.

Bucket Truck Financing FAQ

Can you finance a used bucket truck?

Yes. Many lenders finance used bucket trucks, typically those 5-7 years old or newer. Boom and hydraulic condition, working height, and mileage affect approval. Used equipment may require a larger down payment and shorter terms.

What credit score is required?

Most lenders look for 600 or higher. Scores of 680+ qualify for the best rates. Asset-backed financing sometimes works with 580+ when revenue and down payment are strong. Credit score requirements.

How long does approval take?

1-5 business days for equipment loans and leases. SBA loans add 30-60+ days. Having documents ready speeds the process. How fast can equipment financing be approved.

Is leasing better than buying?

It depends. Leasing offers lower monthly payments, potential tax benefits, and easier upgrades. Buying builds equity and works if you plan to use the truck long-term. Equipment loan vs lease comparison.

Can startups finance bucket trucks?

Startups face tighter requirements. Most equipment lenders prefer 1-2+ years in business. New businesses may need larger down payments, shorter terms, or a co-signer. SBA loans can help. Apply and we'll match you with lenders that fit your profile.

What documents are needed?

Typical requirements: 3-6 months of business bank statements, tax returns, equipment quote from your dealer, profit and loss statement, and business formation documents. Having these ready speeds approval. What lenders look at.

Does working height affect financing?

Lenders finance bucket trucks of various working heights. Price and equipment specs are in the dealer quote. Higher reach units cost more and affect loan amount. Working height does not typically change approval criteria.
