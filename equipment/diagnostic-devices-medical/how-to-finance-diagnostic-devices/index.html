What Are Medical Diagnostic Devices?

Medical diagnostic devices include ultrasound systems, EKG/ECG machines, spirometers, pulse oximeters, and point-of-care testing devices. Ultrasound provides real-time imaging for cardiac, abdominal, vascular, and obstetric applications. EKG machines record electrical activity of the heart. Spirometers measure lung function. Point-of-care devices support rapid in-office testing for glucose, urinalysis, and other parameters. Equipment supports rapid diagnosis and improved patient throughput. Brands like GE, Philips, Mindray, Welch Allyn, and Masimo are common. Diagnostic devices are essential for medical practices offering in-office diagnostics.

How Do You Finance Diagnostic Devices?

Use equipment loans, leases, or vendor programs. Most lenders require credit 600+, down payment 0–20%, and proof of practice revenue. Approval typically takes 1–5 days. Diagnostic devices are widely financed through healthcare equipment lenders.

How Much Do Diagnostic Devices Cost?

Basic EKG machines run $3,000–$10,000. Spirometers cost $1,000–$8,000. Point-of-care devices run $500–$5,000. Ultrasound systems cost $15,000–$80,000+ depending on application. Used equipment typically costs 20–40% less. Many lenders finance used equipment. Obtain a written vendor quote.

Financing Options for Diagnostic Devices

Equipment loans and leases both work. Loans require 0–20% down, terms of 36–60 months. Rates typically range from 6–15%. Leasing offers lower monthly payments and can include technology refresh. See equipment loan vs lease. Vendor financing programs may offer promotional terms.

Diagnostic Devices Financing FAQ

Can you finance used diagnostic devices?

Yes. Many lenders finance used ultrasound, EKG, and spirometers in good condition. Used equipment may require a larger down payment and shorter terms. Regulatory requirements apply.

Diagnostic devices vs imaging—what's the difference?

Diagnostic devices include ultrasound, EKG, spirometers, and point-of-care tools. Imaging typically refers to X-ray, MRI, CT. Both are medical equipment; diagnostic devices are often lower-ticket.

How long does diagnostic devices financing approval take?

Approval typically takes 1–5 business days for equipment loans and leases. SBA loans can take 30–60+ days.

Is leasing better than buying diagnostic devices?

It depends. Leasing offers lower monthly payments and can include upgrades. Buying builds equity. Technology refresh may favor leasing for some devices.
