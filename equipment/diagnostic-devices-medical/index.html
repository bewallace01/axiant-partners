What Are Medical Diagnostic Devices?

Medical diagnostic devices include ultrasound systems, EKG/ECG machines, spirometers, pulse oximeters, and point-of-care testing devices. Ultrasound provides real-time imaging for cardiac, abdominal, vascular, and obstetric applications. EKG machines record electrical activity of the heart. Spirometers measure lung function. Point-of-care devices support rapid in-office testing for glucose, urinalysis, and other parameters. Brands like GE, Philips, Mindray, Welch Allyn, and Masimo are common. Diagnostic devices are essential for medical practices offering in-office diagnostics.

Diagnostic Devices Financing Options

Several financing structures work for diagnostic devices. Choose based on cash flow, tax situation, and whether you want to own the equipment at the end of the term.

Equipment Loans

Borrow a set amount, make fixed monthly payments, and own the equipment when the loan is paid off. Typically 0–20% down, terms 36–60 months. Rates 6–15%. Ideal if you plan to use the devices long-term. See typical rates.

Equipment Leasing

Lower monthly payments than loans. At lease end, return, purchase at fair market value, or upgrade. Operating leases treat payments as operating expenses. Technology refresh may favor leasing. Loan vs lease.

SBA Loans

SBA 7(a) and 504 loans offer longer terms (7–10+ years) and lower down payments. Approval typically 30–60+ days. Best for larger purchases or bundling with other practice equipment. View SBA loans.

Working capital loans are flexible but generally carry higher rates and shorter terms. Use working capital for operating expenses; use equipment financing for diagnostic devices to secure better rates and terms. Compare equipment loan vs lease.

Tips to Get Approved for Diagnostic Devices Financing

  • Improve your credit score. Pay down balances, correct errors, avoid new credit applications before applying.

  • Consider used equipment. Quality used diagnostic devices may cost less and improve approval odds when credit is borderline.

Common Mistakes to Avoid When Financing Diagnostic Devices

  • Skipping the equipment quote. Lenders need it to structure the loan. Get a written quote before applying.
  • Applying with incomplete financials. Missing bank statements or tax returns causes delays.

Diagnostic Devices Financing FAQ

Can you finance used diagnostic devices?

Yes. Many lenders finance used ultrasound, EKG, spirometers in good condition. Used equipment may require larger down payment and shorter terms. Regulatory requirements apply.

What credit score is required?

Most lenders look for 600 or higher. Scores of 680+ qualify for the best rates. Asset-backed financing sometimes works with 580+ when revenue and down payment are strong.

Diagnostic devices vs imaging·what's the difference?

Diagnostic devices include ultrasound, EKG, spirometers, point-of-care tools. Imaging typically refers to X-ray, MRI, CT. Both are medical equipment; diagnostic devices are often lower-ticket.

How long does approval take?

1–5 business days for equipment loans and leases. SBA loans add 30–60+ days. Having documents ready speeds the process.

Is leasing better than buying diagnostic devices?

It depends. Leasing offers lower monthly payments and can include upgrades. Buying builds equity. Technology refresh may favor leasing for some devices. Equipment loan vs lease comparison.

What documents are needed?

Typical requirements: 3–6 months of business bank statements, tax returns, P&L, equipment quote from vendor, and business formation documents. What lenders look at.
