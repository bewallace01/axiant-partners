What Are Auto Lifts and Hoists?

Auto lifts raise vehicles for undercarriage work, oil changes, tire service, and brake repairs. Two-post lifts use arms under the vehicle; four-post lifts use runways. Capacity typically ranges from 7,000–15,000+ lbs. Scissor lifts and mid-rise lifts suit specific applications. Brands like BendPak, Rotary, and Challenger are common. Auto lifts are essential for auto repair shops.

How Do You Finance an Auto Lift?

Use equipment loans, leases, or SBA loans. Most lenders require credit 600+, down payment 0–20%, and proof of revenue. Approval typically takes 1–5 days. Auto lifts are common shop equipment—financing is widely available through auto repair lenders.

How Much Does an Auto Lift Cost?

Two-post lifts typically cost $3,000–$12,000. Four-post lifts run $5,000–$25,000+ depending on capacity and features. Scissor and mid-rise lifts cost $3,000–$15,000. Used lifts typically cost 20–35% less. Many lenders finance used equipment. Obtain a written quote—lenders use it to structure financing.

Financing Options for Auto Lifts

Equipment loans and leases both work. Loans require 0–20% down, terms of 36–60 months. Rates typically range from 6–15%. Leasing offers lower monthly payments. See equipment loan vs lease. SBA loans can combine lifts with other shop equipment.

Auto Lift Financing FAQ

Can you finance used auto lifts?

Yes. Many lenders finance used two-post and four-post lifts in good condition. Used equipment may require a larger down payment and shorter terms. Condition, capacity, and certification affect approval.

Two-post vs four-post—does it affect financing?

Lenders finance both. Four-post lifts typically cost more. Price and capacity matter more than type.

How long does auto lift financing approval take?

Approval typically takes 1–5 business days for equipment loans and leases. SBA loans can take 30–60+ days.

Is leasing better than buying an auto lift?

It depends. Leasing offers lower monthly payments and easier upgrades. Buying builds equity. Compare both based on your cash flow.
