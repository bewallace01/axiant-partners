Summary

Auto lift financing—including two-post and four-post lift loans—helps auto repair shops add or replace lifts and hoists without draining working capital. Auto lifts are commonly financed through equipment lenders and SBA programs.

Costs typically run $3,000–$25,000+ for new lifts; used auto lift financing costs less. Equipment financing spreads payments over 36–60 months.
