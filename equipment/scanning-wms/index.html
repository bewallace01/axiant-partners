Summary

Scanning and WMS financing—including barcode scanner loans and WMS technology leases—helps warehouses add or replace scanning and warehouse management systems without draining working capital. Scanning and WMS are commonly financed through equipment and technology lenders and SBA programs.

Costs typically run $2,000–$50,000+ for scanners and WMS; hardware and software packages are often financed together. Equipment financing spreads payments over 24–60 months.
