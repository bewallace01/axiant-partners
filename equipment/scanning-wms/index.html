What Are Scanning & WMS?

Scanning and warehouse management systems (WMS) include hardware and software for tracking inventory and workflow:

Barcode scanners

· Handheld, wearable (ring/glove), or fixed-mount. 1D and 2D; often Bluetooth or Wi-Fi. Zebra, Honeywell, Datalogic common.

Mobile computers

· Rugged handhelds and tablets for receiving, picking, putaway. Integrated scanners, keyboards, large screens.

WMS software

· Cloud (SaaS) $2,000–$15,000/month or perpetual license $30,000–$150,000+. Tiers by user count and features. Manages inventory, orders, shipping, labor.

Hardware packages $5,000–$50,000; implementation $25,000–$120,000. Combined hardware-plus-software projects are commonly financed as equipment/technology bundles.

Why Scanning & WMS Financing Is Different

WMS and scanning are an IT/equipment hybrid—many lenders finance hardware and software together when packaged with a vendor quote. Technology leasing often includes upgrade flexibility. Projects are frequently bundled with conveyor systems, forklifts, and WMS for warehouse automation. Equipment approval typically 1–5 days. Some vendors offer 12–72 month financing with 100% financing available.

Tax Benefits: Section 179 and Bonus Depreciation

Scanning Wms typically qualifies for Section 179 (deduct full purchase price in year of purchase, subject to limits) and bonus depreciation. Lease payments are usually fully deductible as operating expenses. These benefits can significantly reduce the net cost of financing—consult your CPA for your situation.

Scanning & WMS Financing FAQ

Can you finance WMS software with hardware?

Yes. Many lenders finance combined hardware-and-software packages. Total project cost determines structure. Include implementation in the quote.

Does handheld vs wearable affect financing?

Price and total scope matter more than device type. Handheld, wearable, and fixed-mount scanners are all commonly financed.

How long does approval take?

1–5 business days for equipment/technology financing. A detailed vendor quote speeds the process. Bundle deals may take slightly longer.

Is leasing better than buying scanning and WMS?

Technology leasing often includes upgrade flexibility as devices age. Buying builds equity. Compare based on cash flow and refresh cycles.

Can I bundle scanning with other warehouse equipment?

Yes. Many lenders finance combined projects: scanning, forklifts, conveyors, racking.
