Summary

Commercial mower financing—including walk-behind and stand-on mower loans—helps landscapers add or replace mowers without draining working capital. Commercial mowers are commonly financed through equipment lenders and SBA programs.

Costs typically run $3,000–$12,000+ for new commercial mowers; used commercial mower financing costs less. Equipment financing spreads payments over 36–60 months.
