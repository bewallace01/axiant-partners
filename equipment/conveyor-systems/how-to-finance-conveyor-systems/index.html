What Are Conveyor Systems?

Conveyor systems move goods through warehouses—belt, roller, and gravity conveyors handle receiving, picking, packing, and shipping. Systems range from simple gravity flow to automated sortation. Length, throughput (units per hour), and integration with WMS drive design. Conveyors reduce labor and increase accuracy. Brands like Hytrol, Bastian, and Dorner are common. Conveyors are essential for logistics and warehousing operations with volume.

How Do You Finance Conveyor Systems?

Use equipment loans, leases, or SBA loans. Most lenders require credit 600+, down payment 0–20%, and proof of revenue. Approval typically takes 1–5 days for equipment financing. SBA loans can take 30–60+ days. Conveyors are common warehouse automation—financing is widely available through logistics lenders.

How Much Do Conveyor Systems Cost?

Simple conveyor lines run $10,000–$50,000. Mid-size systems (picking, packing, sortation) cost $50,000–$150,000. Large automated systems exceed $200,000+. Used conveyors typically cost 25–40% less. Many lenders finance used equipment. Obtain a detailed quote from your integrator—lenders use it to structure financing.

Financing Options for Conveyor Systems

Equipment loans are common—0–20% down, terms of 36–84 months. Rates typically range from 6–15%. Leasing preserves capital and may ease future upgrades. SBA loans can include conveyors with facility or equipment. See equipment loan vs lease.

Conveyor Financing FAQ

Can you finance used conveyor systems?

Yes. Many lenders finance used conveyor systems in good condition. Used equipment may require a larger down payment and shorter terms. Condition and throughput affect approval.

Belt vs roller—does it affect financing?

Lenders consider total project cost and useful life. Different conveyor types have different costs. Price and application matter more than type.

How long does conveyor financing approval take?

Approval typically takes 1–5 business days for equipment loans and leases. SBA loans can take 30–60+ days. Having a detailed quote speeds the process.

Is leasing better than buying conveyor systems?

It depends. Leasing preserves capital and may ease upgrades. Buying builds equity. Compare both based on your cash flow.
