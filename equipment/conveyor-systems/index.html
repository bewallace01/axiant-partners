Summary

Conveyor system financing—including conveyor loans and warehouse automation leases—helps warehouses add or replace belt and roller conveyors without draining working capital. Conveyors are commonly financed through equipment lenders and SBA programs.

Costs typically run $10,000–$200,000+ for new systems; used conveyor financing costs less. Equipment financing spreads payments over 36–60 months.
