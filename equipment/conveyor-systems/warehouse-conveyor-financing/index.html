Why Warehouses Use Conveyor Systems

Conveyors reduce labor, speed picking and packing, and improve accuracy. They complement forklifts and pallet racking. One financed conveyor system can increase throughput, reduce fulfillment costs, and support growth without proportionally adding labor. Belt conveyors move cartons and totes; roller conveyors handle pallets; sortation systems route items to shipping lanes. E-commerce and omnichannel fulfillment have driven demand for warehouse conveyor automation. See equipment financing by type.

Conveyor System Costs

Simple belt or roller conveyors run roughly $25,000-$75,000 depending on length and configuration. Sortation systems (pop-up wheels, sliding shoes, tilt-tray) add complexity: $100,000-$300,000+. Fully integrated systems with WMS integration can run $200,000-$500,000+. Get a written quote from your conveyor supplier with equipment list and specifications. See equipment financing requirements.

Financing Options: Loans vs Leases

Equipment loans spread the cost over 36-72 months; you own at payoff and can depreciate. Leases often have lower monthly payments and full payment deductibility; at term end you can purchase, renew, or return. Conveyor systems have long useful lives—both structures work. Leasing can suit operations that expect layout changes or technology upgrades. See equipment loan vs lease. Use our calculator to model payments.

Including Installation in Financing

Some lenders allow installation and integration to be bundled with equipment; others finance equipment only. Modular, off-the-shelf systems may be easier to finance as tangible equipment. Custom engineering and soft costs (software, integration) may have different treatment. Discuss with your conveyor supplier and lender—many material handling dealers have lender relationships. Document what's included in your quote.

Credit and Down Payment Requirements

Most lenders look for 600+ FICO; 680+ qualifies for the best rates. Down payments: 0-15% for qualified applicants. Conveyor systems are substantial collateral; lenders evaluate credit, revenue, and time in business. See credit score for equipment financing and down payment requirements.

Documentation for Conveyor Financing

Gather: equipment quote (specifications, layout if applicable), 3-6 months business bank statements, tax returns, profit and loss statement, business formation documents. For larger systems: facility information and throughput projections. Complete documentation speeds approval. See equipment financing approval requirements.

Loan Terms and Approval Timeline

Terms for conveyor systems typically run 36-84 months depending on system life and lender. Many lenders approve within 24-48 hours for straightforward packages; larger or custom systems may require additional underwriting. See how fast equipment financing is approved.

Types of Conveyor Systems to Finance

Belt conveyors, roller conveyors, gravity conveyors, sortation systems, and accumulation conveyors are all financeable. Specify make, model, and configuration in your quote. Lenders prefer clearly defined, identifiable equipment. Avoid financing speculative or undefined "conveyor project" costs—itemize the equipment.

Worked example: financing a conveyor system

Take a distributor financing a $150,000 conveyor and sortation system with 10% down, leaving $135,000 over 72 months. At about 10% APR the payment is roughly $2,500 a month — readily covered by the labor savings and throughput a sortation line delivers. Because installation (electrical, controls, structural) can be a major part of a conveyor project, confirm whether your lender will bundle those costs into the financing or finance the equipment only; rolling install into the loan funds the turnkey system in one payment.
