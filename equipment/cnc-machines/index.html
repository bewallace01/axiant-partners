Summary

CNC machine financing—including CNC mill loans, lathe loans, and machining center leases—helps manufacturers add or replace precision equipment without draining working capital. CNC machines are commonly financed through equipment lenders and SBA programs.

Costs typically run $50,000–$500,000+ for new CNC equipment; used CNC financing costs less. Equipment financing spreads payments over 5–7+ years.
