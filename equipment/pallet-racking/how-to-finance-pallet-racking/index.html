What Is Pallet Racking?

Pallet racking is warehouse storage that holds palletized inventory. Types include selective (most common), drive-in, drive-through, push-back, and cantilever. Shelving and mezzanines expand vertical space. Racking maximizes warehouse density and organization. Capacity and layout depend on product, forklift type, and throughput. Brands like Ridg-U-Rak, Steel King, and UNARCO are common. Pallet racking is essential for logistics and warehousing operations.

How Do You Finance Pallet Racking?

Use equipment loans, leases, or SBA loans. Most lenders require credit 600+, down payment 0–20%, and proof of revenue. Approval typically takes 1–5 days for equipment financing. SBA loans can take 30–60+ days. Racking is common warehouse equipment—financing is widely available through logistics lenders.

How Much Does Pallet Racking Cost?

Pallet racking costs $5,000–$100,000+ depending on bay count, height, and configuration. Small installations (20–50 bays) run $5,000–$25,000. Large systems (200+ bays) can exceed $100,000. Mezzanines and shelving add cost. Used racking typically costs 20–35% less. Many lenders finance used equipment. Obtain a written quote from your supplier.

Financing Options for Pallet Racking

Equipment loans are the most common option—0–20% down, terms of 36–84 months. Rates typically range from 6–15%. Leasing preserves capital. SBA 7(a) and 504 loans can include racking with facility or equipment. See equipment loan vs lease.

Pallet Racking Financing FAQ

Can you finance used pallet racking?

Yes. Many lenders finance used pallet racking and storage systems. Condition, capacity, and compatibility affect approval. Used equipment may require shorter terms.

Selective vs drive-in—does it affect financing?

Lenders consider total project cost and useful life. Different rack types have different costs. Price and application matter more than rack type.

Can pallet racking be included in an SBA loan?

Yes. SBA 7(a) and 504 loans can include pallet racking as part of equipment or facility financing. Approval typically takes 30–60+ days.

Is leasing better than buying pallet racking?

It depends. Leasing preserves capital and may offer tax benefits. Buying builds equity. Compare both based on your situation.
