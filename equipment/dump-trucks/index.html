Summary

Dump truck financing—including dump truck loans and dump truck leases—helps construction and trucking businesses add or replace haulers without draining working capital. Dump trucks are commonly financed through equipment lenders and SBA programs.

Costs typically run $70,000–$280,000+ for new trucks; used dump truck financing costs less. Equipment financing spreads payments over 36–60 months.
