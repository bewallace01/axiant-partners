Ventilation & Hood Financing

Ventilation and hood financing—including exhaust hood loans and ductwork leases—helps restaurants meet code requirements for commercial cooking without draining working capital. Ventilation equipment is commonly financed through equipment lenders and SBA programs.

Costs typically run $5,000–$50,000+ depending on size; used ventilation financing costs less. Equipment financing spreads payments over 36–60 months.
