What Are Ventilation & Hood Systems?

Commercial ventilation and hood systems include exhaust hoods (wall-mounted, island, pass-through), ductwork, exhaust fans, make-up air systems, and fire suppression. Exhaust hoods capture grease, smoke, and heat from cooking equipment. Ductwork routes exhaust to the exterior. Make-up air replaces exhausted air. Equipment is required by code for commercial cooking. Brands like Captive-Aire, Greenheck, Systemate, and Vent-A-Hood are common. Ventilation is essential for restaurants and food service kitchens.

How Do You Finance Ventilation & Hood Systems?

Use equipment loans, leases, or bundle with kitchen buildout. Most lenders require credit 600+, down payment 0–20%, and proof of business revenue. Approval typically takes 1–5 days. Ventilation is widely financed through restaurant equipment lenders.

How Much Do Ventilation & Hood Systems Cost?

Basic exhaust hoods run $5,000–$15,000. Larger hoods and ductwork cost $15,000–$35,000+. Make-up air systems add $5,000–$20,000+. Fire suppression add $2,000–$10,000. Full ventilation packages can exceed $50,000. Used equipment typically costs 20–35% less. Many lenders finance used equipment. Obtain a written vendor quote.

Financing Options for Ventilation & Hood Systems

Equipment loans and leases both work. Loans require 0–20% down, terms of 36–60 months. Rates typically range from 6–15%. Leasing offers lower monthly payments. See equipment loan vs lease. Bundling with kitchen equipment may improve terms.

Ventilation Financing FAQ

Can you finance used ventilation and hood systems?

Yes. Many lenders finance used exhaust hoods and ventilation in good condition. Used equipment may require a larger down payment and shorter terms. Code compliance must be verified.

Is ventilation required for restaurant financing?

Exhaust hoods and ventilation are typically required by code for commercial cooking. Lenders understand this and finance ventilation as part of kitchen buildouts.

How long does ventilation financing approval take?

Approval typically takes 1–5 business days for equipment loans and leases. SBA loans can take 30–60+ days.

Is leasing better than buying ventilation equipment?

It depends. Leasing offers lower monthly payments. Buying builds equity. Compare both based on your cash flow.
