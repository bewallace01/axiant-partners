Summary

Lathe and milling machine financing—including lathe loans and mill leases—helps manufacturers add or replace manual and CNC equipment without draining working capital. Lathes and mills are commonly financed through equipment lenders and SBA programs.

Costs typically run $20,000–$150,000+ for new lathes and mills; used lathe/mill financing costs less. Equipment financing spreads payments over 36–84 months.
