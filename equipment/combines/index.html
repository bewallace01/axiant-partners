Summary

Combine financing—including combine loans and combine leases—helps grain and row-crop farmers add or replace harvesters without draining working capital. Combines are commonly financed through equipment lenders and SBA programs.

Costs typically run $300,000–$500,000+ for new combines; used combine financing costs less. Equipment financing spreads payments over 5–7+ years.
