Summary

Trailer financing—including trailer loans and trailer leases—helps carriers and contractors add or replace dry van, flatbed, and utility trailers without draining working capital. Trailers are commonly financed through equipment lenders and SBA programs.

Costs typically run $3,000–$80,000+ for new trailers depending on type; used trailer financing costs less. Equipment financing spreads payments over 36–60 months.
