What Is Restaurant Refrigeration?

Restaurant refrigeration includes walk-in coolers and freezers, reach-in refrigerators and freezers, prep tables (refrigerated work surfaces), and display coolers. Walk-ins provide bulk storage. Reach-ins offer convenient access in kitchen and service areas. Prep tables combine refrigerated storage with work surface. Equipment is essential for food safety and storage. Brands like True, Turbo Air, Beverage-Air, Kolpak, and Nor-Lake are common. Refrigeration is essential for restaurants and food service operations.

How Do You Finance Restaurant Refrigeration?

Use equipment loans, leases, or vendor programs. Most lenders require credit 600+, down payment 0–20%, and proof of business revenue. Approval typically takes 1–5 days. Refrigeration is widely financed through restaurant equipment lenders.

How Much Does Restaurant Refrigeration Cost?

Reach-in refrigerators run $2,000–$8,000. Prep tables cost $2,000–$10,000+. Walk-in coolers run $10,000–$50,000+ depending on size. Walk-in freezers cost $15,000–$80,000+. Used equipment typically costs 20–35% less. Many lenders finance used equipment. Obtain a written vendor quote.

Financing Options for Restaurant Refrigeration

Equipment loans and leases both work. Loans require 0–20% down, terms of 36–60 months. Rates typically range from 6–15%. Leasing offers lower monthly payments. See equipment loan vs lease. Bundling with kitchen or ventilation equipment may improve terms.

Restaurant Refrigeration Financing FAQ

Can you finance used restaurant refrigeration?

Yes. Many lenders finance used walk-ins, reach-ins, and prep tables in good condition. Used equipment may require a larger down payment and shorter terms.

Walk-in vs reach-in—does it affect financing?

Lenders finance both. Walk-ins typically cost more and may have longer terms. Reach-ins and prep tables are common and widely financed.

How long does refrigeration financing approval take?

Approval typically takes 1–5 business days for equipment loans and leases. SBA loans can take 30–60+ days.

Is leasing better than buying refrigeration?

It depends. Leasing offers lower monthly payments. Buying builds equity. Compare both based on your cash flow.
