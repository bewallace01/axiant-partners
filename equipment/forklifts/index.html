Summary

Forklift financing—including forklift loans and forklift leases—helps warehouses, logistics companies, and manufacturers add or replace material handling equipment without draining working capital. Electric, propane, and diesel forklifts are commonly financed through equipment lenders and SBA programs.

Costs typically run $15,000–$80,000+ for new equipment depending on type and capacity; used forklift financing costs less. Equipment financing spreads payments over 36–60 months.
