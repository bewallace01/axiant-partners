Quick answer

New electric forklifts run roughly $30,000 to $80,000+ depending on capacity, lift height and battery type; lithium-ion costs 20 to 40% more upfront but charges faster and needs no battery room. Used three-to-five-year-old units may be 30 to 40% less, though battery condition decides the value — a lead-acid replacement alone runs $3,000 to $8,000+. The battery can be included in the financing.

Why Businesses Choose Electric Forklifts

Electric forklifts produce no emissions—essential for indoor use in warehouses, distribution centers, food handling, pharma, and cold storage. They have lower operating costs (no fuel), less maintenance than internal combustion units, and quieter operation. ESG and sustainability goals increasingly drive electric adoption. Many municipalities and facilities require electric or propane for indoor use. Electric forklifts also avoid exhaust fumes that can affect product quality in food and pharmaceutical environments. See equipment financing by type. Used electric forklift financing is available for well-maintained units with good battery life.

Electric Forklift Costs

New electric forklifts run roughly $30,000-$80,000+ depending on capacity, lift height, battery type (lead-acid vs lithium-ion), and configuration. Lithium-ion models may cost 20-40% more upfront but offer longer life, faster charging, and no battery room requirements. Used 3-5 year old units may be 30-40% less, though battery condition is critical. A full battery replacement for lead-acid can run $3,000-$8,000+; factor that into used purchases. See used forklift financing for age limits and qualification.

Financing Options: Loans vs Leases

Equipment loans spread the cost; you own at payoff and can depreciate the equipment. Leases often have lower monthly payments and full payment deductibility; at term end you can return, renew, or purchase. Electric forklifts suit both structures. Leasing can be attractive when technology (batteries, efficiency) improves quickly—you can upgrade at lease end. See forklift lease vs loan and equipment loan vs lease. Use our calculator to model payments.

Battery Inclusion in Financing

Most lenders finance electric forklifts including the battery as part of the equipment package. Lithium-ion upgrades and replacement batteries can often be included in the quote when purchased with the unit. For used electric forklifts, battery age and remaining life affect approval and terms—lenders consider what they could recover if you default. Document battery warranty, cycles, and condition when applying. Some dealers offer battery-as-a-service or warranty extensions that can be financed.

Credit and Down Payment Requirements

Most lenders look for 600+ FICO; 680+ qualifies for the best rates. Down payments: 0-15% for new equipment. Electric forklifts are standard collateral; approval is typically straightforward when credit and documentation are in order. See credit score for equipment financing and down payment requirements. New businesses may need stronger personal credit or a larger down payment.

Loan Terms and Approval Timeline

Terms for electric forklifts typically run 36-72 months depending on equipment life and lender. Interest rates are similar to other equipment financing. Many lenders approve electric forklift financing within 24-48 hours when documentation is complete. Marketplace applications can return multiple offers in 1-3 business days. See how fast equipment financing is approved.

Electric vs Propane: When Electric Makes Sense

Electric forklifts suit indoor use, cold storage, food handling, and zero-emission requirements. Propane suits outdoor or mixed indoor/outdoor use where refueling speed and range matter. Electric has higher upfront cost but lower operating cost (no fuel). Consider your shift patterns, charging infrastructure, and facility requirements. Both are routinely financed; choose based on application. See forklift financing overview for capacity and cost ranges.

Documentation for Electric Forklift Financing

Gather: equipment quote (make, model, capacity, battery type), 3-6 months business bank statements, tax returns, profit and loss statement, business formation documents. For new businesses: business plan and personal financials. Complete documentation speeds approval. See equipment financing requirements.

Equipment lenders, banks, and material handling specialists offer electric forklift financing. Dealer financing is convenient—many forklift dealers have preferred lender relationships. Frequently Asked Questions

How much does an electric forklift cost?

New electric forklifts run $30,000-$80,000+ depending on capacity and configuration. Used 3-5 year old units may be 30-40% less.

Can I finance an electric forklift with the battery?

Yes. Most lenders finance electric forklifts including the battery as part of the equipment package.

What credit score do I need for electric forklift financing?

Most lenders look for 600+ FICO. Scores of 680+ qualify for the best rates.

Why choose electric over propane forklifts?

Electric suits indoor use (no emissions), has lower operating costs, and less maintenance. Ideal for warehouses and food handling.

How fast can I get approved for electric forklift financing?

Many lenders approve within 24-48 hours when documentation is complete.

Can you include the battery in electric forklift financing?

Usually yes. The battery and charger are a major part of an electric forklift’s cost, and lenders typically finance them as part of the machine so you fund the working package in one payment.

When does an electric forklift make sense over propane?

Electric suits indoor, multi-shift, or emissions-sensitive operations and lowers fuel and maintenance cost over time; propane suits heavy outdoor use and fast refueling. The financing is similar — the choice is operational.
