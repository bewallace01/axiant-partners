Summary

Wheel loader financing—including wheel loader loans and wheel loader leases—helps construction, mining, and material handling companies add or replace equipment without draining working capital. Wheel loaders are commonly financed through equipment lenders and SBA programs.

Costs typically run $80,000–$400,000+ for new equipment depending on size; used wheel loader financing costs less. Equipment financing spreads payments over 36–60 months.
