Summary

Log truck financing—including log truck loans and log truck leases—helps forestry and trucking businesses add or replace tractors and trailers without draining working capital. Log trucks are commonly financed through equipment lenders and SBA programs.

Costs typically run $180,000–$350,000+ for new tractor and trailer; used log truck financing costs less. Equipment financing spreads payments over 36–60 months.
