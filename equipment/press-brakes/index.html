Summary

Press brake financing—including press brake loans and stamping equipment leases—helps metal fabricators add or replace forming equipment without draining working capital. Press brakes are commonly financed through equipment lenders and SBA programs.

Costs typically run $30,000–$300,000+ for new press brakes; used press brake financing costs less. Equipment financing spreads payments over 36–84 months.
