Attachment or Standalone Machine?

If you already run a skid steer with high-flow hydraulics, a stump grinder attachment is the cheaper way in, and it can be financed on its own or added to an equipment loan. The catch is access and wear: the skid steer has to fit through the gate, and every grinding hour is an hour on the loader.

A standalone machine makes more sense when grinding is daily work, when jobs are in fenced yards with narrow gates, or when the loader is needed on another crew. Lenders treat both as ordinary equipment collateral.

If the monthly subcontracting bill is higher than the payment plus wheel teeth and fuel, the machine pays for itself. The price and rate sit inside the typical ranges above.

Buying a Used Stump Grinder

  • Cutting wheel and teeth. The wheel is the costliest wear part; check teeth, pockets and wheel balance.
  • Drive. Belt or clutch wear and how the wheel engages under load.
  • Engine hours and service records.
  • Hydraulics on self-propelled and tracked units: hoses, cylinders and the swing function.
  • Towable frames: the tongue, hitch, tires and lights.

On a private sale, ask for a bill of sale and run a lien search. Lenders and sellers record equipment liens as UCC financing statements with the state, under UCC Article 9, and a lender won't fund a machine with someone else's lien on it.

Seasonal Payments and Storm Work

Tree work comes in waves: storms, spring cleanups and land clearing. Some lenders offer seasonal or skip-payment schedules for equipment in landscaping and tree care, so payments are lower in slow months. Ask for it when you apply, and don't stretch the term past the life you expect from the wheel and engine.

Stump Grinder Financing FAQ

How much does it cost to finance a stump grinder?

It depends on the machine, rate and term. As an illustration, a $30,000 towable at 10% is about $761 a month over 48 months, and a $50,000 self-propelled grinder at 9% is about $1,038 a month over 60.

Can you finance a used stump grinder?

Yes. Lenders look at age, engine hours and condition, especially the cutting wheel. On a private sale, expect to provide a bill of sale and a clean lien search.

Can I finance a stump grinder attachment for my skid steer?

Yes. An attachment can be financed on its own or added to an equipment loan. It needs a skid steer with enough hydraulic flow.

What credit score do you need for stump grinder financing?

Many programs look for about 600 or higher, with the best pricing near 680+. Lower scores can work with more down or strong bank statements.

How long are stump grinder loans?

Commonly 24 to 72 months. Shorter terms suit smaller towable units; longer terms suit larger self-propelled and tracked machines.
