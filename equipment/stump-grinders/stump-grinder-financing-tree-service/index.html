Stump Grinder Costs

Walk-behind units run roughly $2,000-$8,000; self-propelled $8,000-$25,000; tow-behind and skid-steer attachments $5,000-$15,000; large commercial $25,000-$50,000+. Capacity, cutting depth, and power source affect price. Get a written quote with make, model, and specifications. See equipment financing requirements.

Types of Stump Grinders: Walk-Behind, Self-Propelled, Tow-Behind

Walk-behind: Budget-friendly, manual positioning. Self-propelled: Larger cutting capacity, hydraulic or self-propelled for easier positioning. Tow-behind/skid-steer attachment: For operations that already have a skid steer or tractor. All types are routinely financed. Match the machine to your typical stump sizes and access. Financing Options: Loans vs Leases

Equipment loans spread the cost over 36-60 months; you own at payoff. Leases often have lower monthly payments and full payment deductibility; at term end you can purchase or return. Tree service equipment has seasonal cash flow·lower lease payments can ease off-season strain. See equipment loan vs lease. Use our calculator to model payments.

Credit and Down Payment

Most lenders look for 600+ FICO; 680+ qualifies for the best rates. Down payments: 0-15% for new. Tree service revenue, contracts, and time in business improve approval. Equipment is collateral. See credit score for equipment financing and down payment requirements.
