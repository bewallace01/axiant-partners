Why Tree Services Use Stump Grinders

Stump grinding is a standard add-on to tree removal—customers expect clean, grade-level results. One financed stump grinder can expand service offerings, capture add-on revenue, and differentiate from competitors. Tree services without grinding capability often lose jobs or subcontract. See equipment financing by type and bucket truck financing for utility contractors.

Stump Grinder Costs

Walk-behind units run roughly $2,000-$8,000; self-propelled $8,000-$25,000; tow-behind and skid-steer attachments $5,000-$15,000; large commercial $25,000-$50,000+. Capacity, cutting depth, and power source affect price. Get a written quote with make, model, and specifications. See equipment financing requirements.

Types of Stump Grinders: Walk-Behind, Self-Propelled, Tow-Behind

Walk-behind: Budget-friendly, manual positioning. Self-propelled: Larger cutting capacity, hydraulic or self-propelled for easier positioning. Tow-behind/skid-steer attachment: For operations that already have a skid steer or tractor. All types are routinely financed. Match the machine to your typical stump sizes and access. See stump grinder financing overview.

Financing Options: Loans vs Leases

Equipment loans spread the cost over 36-60 months; you own at payoff. Leases often have lower monthly payments and full payment deductibility; at term end you can purchase or return. Tree service equipment has seasonal cash flow—lower lease payments can ease off-season strain. See equipment loan vs lease. Use our calculator to model payments.

Credit and Down Payment

Most lenders look for 600+ FICO; 680+ qualifies for the best rates. Down payments: 0-15% for new. Tree service revenue, contracts, and time in business improve approval. Equipment is collateral. See credit score for equipment financing and down payment requirements.

Documentation and Approval Timeline

Gather: equipment quote, 3-6 months business bank statements, tax returns, P&L, formation documents. Many lenders approve stump grinder financing within 24-48 hours. See approval timeline. Get matched with lenders who finance stump grinders.

What Tree Service Operators Are Usually Trying to Solve

Most readers want one of three outcomes: stop subcontracting stump work and capture margin, upgrade from walk-behind to self-propelled for speed on residential routes, or add a second grinder during peak season without draining reserves. Equipment financing is built for the asset itself. If your real pain is payroll and fuel between storms, also evaluate working capital loans or a business line of credit so you do not stretch the wrong product. For contract structure, compare equipment financing vs SBA when you are funding multiple large purchases in one year.

Seasonal Revenue and Payment Fit

Tree work is often lumpy. When possible, provide 12 months of bank statements so underwriters see both peak and slow months. Choose a term and payment that survives your slowest quarter; leases sometimes offer lower payments if you need flexibility. If statements show NSFs or large irregular draws, clean up before applying—see equipment financing bank statement red flags. Before signing, scan red flags in equipment finance agreements.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a stump grinder cost?

Walk-behind $2,000-$8,000; self-propelled $8,000-$25,000; tow-behind/skid-steer $5,000-$15,000; large commercial $25,000-$50,000+.

Can I finance a stump grinder for my tree service?

Yes. Equipment lenders routinely finance stump grinders. Credit 600+, typical approval in 24-48 hours.

What credit score do I need for stump grinder financing?

Most lenders look for 600+ FICO. Tree service revenue improves approval.

Walk-behind vs self-propelled stump grinder financing?

Both are routinely financed. Include make, model, and specs in your quote.

How fast can I get approved for stump grinder financing?

Many lenders approve within 24-48 hours when documentation is complete.

Can seasonal tree service revenue still qualify for stump grinder financing?

Yes. Show a full year of bank statements when you can, explain seasonality in your file, and keep the payment aligned with average cash flow after slow months.

Can I bundle a stump grinder with other tree equipment on one package?

Often yes. A single quote that lists multiple collateral items can meet minimums and simplify underwriting. Include make, model, and serial numbers when you have them.

Can you finance a stump grinder?

Yes. Walk-behind, self-propelled, and tow-behind stump grinders finance as equipment, typically over 36–60 months, with the machine as collateral. Strong credit and an operating tree service make for easy approvals.

Can I get seasonal payments for a tree service?

Some lenders offer seasonal or skip-payment structures that fit the busier growing season and ease in slow winter months. Expect them to want evidence of your revenue seasonality.

How much does it cost to finance a stump grinder?

Walk-behind grinders run a few thousand dollars while large self-propelled and tow-behind units run tens of thousands; financed over 36–60 months, the payment is modest. Used machines lower both the price and the payment.
