Summary

Refrigerated truck financing—including reefer truck loans and reefer truck leases—helps food distribution and logistics businesses add or replace cold-chain vehicles without draining working capital. Reefers are commonly financed through equipment lenders and SBA programs.

Costs typically run $118,000–$180,000 for new trucks; used refrigerated truck financing costs less. Equipment financing spreads payments over 36–60 months.
