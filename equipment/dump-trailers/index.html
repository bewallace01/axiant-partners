How Much Does a Dump Trailer Cost to Finance?

New dump trailers typically cost $8,000-$25,000+ depending on bed size and capacity. Used ones typically cost 25-40% less, and a 5-year-old dump trailer might run $5,000-$15,000 (figures from landscape trailer financing). Ask for a written quote that lists the trailer, any options, tax and fees.

Dump Trailer Loan vs Lease vs Rent-to-Own

Rent-to-own and financing often get lumped together, but they are different contracts. All four options below are common; they differ in who owns the trailer and in total cost.

OptionOwn at end?Total costFits
Equipment loanYes, after the last paymentPrice plus interestKeeping the trailer for years
$1 buyout lease (lease-to-own)Yes, for $1Close to a loanOwnership with lease paperwork
Fair market value leaseOnly if you buy it at market valueLower payment; buyout extraPlanned upgrade or return
Dealer rent-to-ownYes, if you pay through the planUsually the highestTrying the work before committing
  • Loan: you own the trailer from the start and the lender is recorded on the title until it is paid off.
  • Lease-to-own: a lease with a $1 or small buyout. The payment and total cost are close to a loan.
  • Rent-to-own: a dealer plan where you rent by the month, part of the rent can count toward buying, and you can usually return the trailer. That flexibility is what you pay for. Ask how much of each payment applies and what the total is if you keep it.
  • End dump leases: end dumps are semi-trailers and are leased or financed like other semi-trailers; see trailer financing.

For a business with fair credit, a loan on a used trailer often costs less in total than rent-to-own on a new one. More on the trade-offs: equipment loan vs lease.

Used Dump Trailer Financing

Many lenders finance used dump trailers, typically 5-7 years old or newer. Because a trailer has no engine, the lender is valuing the frame, axles, deck, hoist and hydraulics, so expect questions about each:

  • Title and VIN: a clean title in the seller's name that matches the VIN plate.
  • Hoist and hydraulics: leaks, a weak pump or a bent cylinder are expensive repairs.
  • Frame, floor and axles: cracks, rust-through and worn bearings change the value.
  • Tires and brakes: replacing them is a cost to price in before you buy.

Dump Trailer Financing With Bad Credit

A dump trailer is a modest amount and the trailer secures the loan, which helps when credit is weaker. At 580-639 credit, expect about 16-22% APR, 2-4 year terms and 15-25% down. Scores of 550-599 go to specialty lenders, and below 550 usually takes 25% or more down or a cosigner.

  • Put more down or choose a less expensive used trailer to shrink the amount financed.
  • Show steady deposits: 3-6 months of business bank statements carry weight when the score is low.
  • Compare rent-to-own last, as a fallback rather than the first choice, and on total cost.

Dump Trailer Financing FAQ

How much does it cost to finance a dump trailer?

New dump trailers typically cost $8,000-$25,000+. A $12,000 trailer is about $243 a month at 8% over 60 months with strong credit and about $334 at 15% over 48 months for fair credit, principal and interest only. Taxes, fees and insurance come on top.

Is it hard to finance a dump trailer or a dump truck?

Not usually with 600+ credit, which opens most equipment lenders. Scores of 550-599 go to specialty lenders at higher rates, and below 550 usually takes 25% or more down or a cosigner. A dump truck is a much larger ticket, so lenders look harder at time in business and hauling revenue.

Can you rent to own a dump trailer?

Yes. Many dealers offer rent-to-own: you pay monthly, part of the rent can count toward buying, and you can usually return the trailer. That flexibility usually makes it the most expensive way to end up owning one. Lease-to-own ($1 buyout lease) and an equipment loan both end in ownership and cost about the same as each other.

Is buying a dump trailer a good investment?

It can be if the hauling work covers the payment with room to spare. Compare the monthly payment, about $243 on a $12,000 trailer at 8% over 60 months, with what you spend now renting one or hiring hauling, and add insurance, registration, tires and upkeep. A trailer you own can also be sold or used as collateral later.

Can you finance a used dump trailer?

Yes. Many lenders finance used dump trailers, typically 5-7 years old or newer. The title, frame, axles, hoist and hydraulics affect approval and the amount.

Can you lease an end dump trailer?

Yes. End dumps are semi-trailers pulled by a tractor, a larger ticket than a bumper-pull or gooseneck dump trailer, and they are leased or financed like other semi-trailers. See trailer financing for semi-trailer terms.

What credit score do you need for dump trailer financing?

600+ opens most lenders. The best rates, about 7-9%, go to 720+ credit with 2+ years in business; 680-719 typically sees 10-13%, 640-679 13-16% and 580-639 16-22%.

What documents do you need?

A written quote or bill of sale with the VIN, 3-6 months of business bank statements and business formation documents. Larger amounts can add tax returns and a profit and loss statement.
