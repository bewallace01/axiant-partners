Summary

Pallet jack financing—including manual and electric pallet jack loans—helps warehouses add or replace material handling equipment without draining working capital. Pallet jacks are commonly financed through equipment lenders and SBA programs.

Costs typically run $500–$15,000+ per unit; used pallet jack financing costs less. Equipment financing spreads payments over 24–60 months.
