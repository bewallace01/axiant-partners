Sprinter Van Financing at a Glance

Sprinter van financing (and cargo van financing for Ford Transit, RAM ProMaster, and similar vehicles) helps delivery businesses, couriers, and trades add capacity without paying cash. Unlike larger box trucks, Sprinter-style vans are Class 2-3 cargo vans ideal for urban routes, residential delivery, and smaller payloads. New units run roughly $45,000-$75,000; used 3-5 year old vans can be 25-40% less.

Equipment loans and leases spread the cost over 36-72 months. Many lenders approve within 24-48 hours. See our in-depth guide: Sprinter Van Financing for Delivery Business.
