Summary

Semi truck financing—including semi truck loans and semi truck leases—helps freight and trucking businesses add or replace tractors without draining working capital. Semi trucks are commonly financed through equipment lenders and SBA programs.

Costs typically run $130,000–$270,000+ for new trucks; used semi truck financing costs less. Equipment financing spreads payments over 36–60 months.
