Summary

Tanker truck financing—including tanker truck loans and tanker truck leases—helps fuel delivery and chemical hauling businesses add or replace tankers without draining working capital. Tanker trucks are commonly financed through equipment lenders and SBA programs.

Costs typically run $150,000–$250,000+ for new trucks; used tanker financing costs less. Equipment financing spreads payments over 36–60 months.
