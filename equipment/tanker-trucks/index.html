What Is a Tanker Truck?

A tanker truck is a commercial vehicle designed to transport liquid cargo in a large cylindrical or elliptical tank mounted on a chassis. Tanker types include fuel tankers (gasoline, diesel, propane), chemical tankers (hazardous and non-hazardous liquids), milk tankers, and water haulers. Tanks may be single- or multi-compartment, insulated or non-insulated, and require hazmat certifications for certain materials.

Tanker trucks are used by fuel distributors, chemical haulers, propane suppliers, and bulk liquid transporters. Industries served include oil and gas, agriculture, manufacturing, and logistics. Understanding what tanker trucks are and their certification requirements helps lenders assess your application·they want to see that the equipment supports compliant, revenue-generating operations.

Tanker Truck Financing Options

Several financing structures work for tanker trucks. Choose based on cash flow, tax situation, and whether you want to own the truck at the end of the term.

Equipment Loans

Borrow a set amount, make fixed monthly payments, and own the tanker when the loan is paid off. Typically 0–20% down, terms 24–84 months. Interest rates 6–15% depending on credit. Ideal if you plan to use the truck long-term and want to build equity. See typical rates.

Equipment Leasing

Lower monthly payments than loans. TRAC leases use a residual to reduce the financed amount. At lease end, return the truck, purchase at fair market value, or upgrade. Operating leases treat payments as operating expenses. Loan vs lease.

SBA Loans

SBA 7(a) and 504 loans offer longer terms (7–10+ years) and lower down payments. Approval typically 30–60+ days. Best for established fuel or chemical haulers planning fleet expansion. View SBA loans.

Working capital loans are flexible but generally carry higher rates and shorter terms. Use working capital for fuel, payroll, and operating expenses; use equipment financing for the tanker itself to secure better rates and terms tied to the asset. Compare equipment loan vs lease in detail.

How Much Does a Tanker Truck Cost?

Tanker prices vary by tank type (fuel, chemical, single vs multi-compartment), capacity, chassis, and whether you buy new or used. New tanker trucks range from roughly $150,000–$200,000 for standard fuel tankers to $200,000–$250,000+ for chemical tankers with hazmat specs, multiple compartments, or stainless steel construction. Top chassis brands (Peterbilt, Kenworth, Freightliner) and tank manufacturers add cost.

Used tanker trucks typically cost 30–50% less. A 5-year-old fuel tanker might run $90,000–$150,000. Tank condition, certification status, and inspection history are critical·lenders and buyers require proof of compliance. Many lenders finance used tankers up to 5–7 years old. Obtain a written quote from your dealer; lenders use it to structure financing. Financing used equipment guide.
