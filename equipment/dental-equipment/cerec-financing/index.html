Why Practices Invest in CEREC and CAD/CAM

Same-day restorations reduce patient visits, improve satisfaction, and capture revenue that might go to labs. Digital workflows also support implants, orthodontics, and diagnostic tools. One financed CEREC system can pay for itself through increased crown volume and efficiency. See equipment financing by type.

CEREC and CAD/CAM System Costs

CEREC and similar systems run roughly $80,000-$150,000+ including intraoral scanner, milling unit, oven, and software. Pricing varies by manufacturer (Dentsply Sirona, 3M, Planmeca, Ivoclar) and configuration. Training and support may be included. Get a written quote from your dealer. See dental equipment financing for new practices.

Financing Options: Loans vs Leases

Equipment loans spread the cost; you own at payoff. Leases often have lower payments and full deductibility; you can upgrade at term end. Digital technology evolves; many practices lease to preserve upgrade flexibility. See equipment loan vs lease. Use our calculator.

Credit and Down Payment Requirements

Most lenders look for 600+ FICO; 680+ qualifies for the best rates. Down payments: 0-15%. Established practices typically qualify readily. See credit score for equipment financing and down payment requirements.

Leasing CEREC: Why It's Popular

Leasing preserves capital and offers lower monthly payments. Technology refreshes (new scanners, software, materials) may make upgrading at term end attractive. Full payment deductibility can improve tax treatment. Many practices lease CEREC to maintain flexibility—digital dentistry evolves quickly. See benefits of equipment leasing.

Documentation and Approval Timeline

Gather: equipment quote (make, model, configuration), 3-6 months bank statements, tax returns, P&L, formation documents. For new practices: business plan and personal financials. Complete documentation speeds approval. Many lenders approve in 24-48 hours. See equipment financing requirements, equipment financing approval requirements, and approval timeline.

Rates and Monthly Payments

Rates typically run 6-14%. For a $100,000 CEREC system at 8% over 60 months with 10% down, expect roughly $1,700-$1,900/month. Use our calculator. See typical equipment financing rates.
