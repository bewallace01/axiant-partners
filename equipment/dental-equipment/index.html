Summary

Dental equipment financing—including dental chair loans, delivery unit leases, and CEREC financing—helps dental practices add or replace operatories without draining working capital. Dental equipment is commonly financed through equipment lenders and SBA programs.

Costs typically run $15,000–$100,000+ per operatory; used dental equipment financing costs less. Equipment financing spreads payments over 36–60 months.
