Dental Equipment Financing for New Practices

Equipment loans and leases for startup dental offices—chairs, X-rays, sterilization, and operatories

Opening a new dental practice requires significant equipment investment—operatory chairs, lights, delivery systems, X-ray, sterilization, and often digital systems like CEREC. A full fit-out can run $100,000-$300,000+. Dental equipment financing lets new practitioners launch without paying cash. Why New Practices Need Equipment Financing

New dental practices face high upfront costs: build-out, equipment, and working capital. A full operatory fit-out can exceed $100,000 before the first patient arrives. Financing spreads equipment cost over 36-72 months, preserving cash for payroll, marketing, and reserves. Equipment is collateral—lenders are often willing to work with new practices when credit and down payment support the deal. Some programs specialize in dental startup financing. See equipment financing by type and equipment financing for new businesses.

Dental Equipment Costs for New Practices

A basic operatory (chair, light, delivery system) runs roughly $15,000-$40,000. Add X-ray ($15,000-$50,000+), sterilization ($5,000-$15,000), and digital systems. Full office fit-out with 2-4 operatories can run $100,000-$300,000+. CEREC and other CAD/CAM systems add $80,000-$150,000+. See CEREC financing for digital systems.

Financing Options: Loans vs Leases

Equipment loans spread the cost; you own at payoff. Leases often have lower payments and full deductibility; you can upgrade at term end. New practices often prefer leasing for cash flow and technology refresh. See equipment loan vs lease. Use our calculator.

Credit and Down Payment for New Practices

Most lenders look for 600+ FICO; 680+ qualifies for the best rates. New practices may rely on personal credit. Down payments: 0-15% for new equipment. Strong credit and a business plan help. See credit score for equipment financing and down payment requirements.

Documentation for New Practice Financing

Gather: equipment quote(s), business plan, 3-6 months personal/business bank statements, tax returns, formation documents, lease or build-out agreement. See equipment financing requirements.

Approval Timeline

Many lenders approve dental equipment financing in 24-48 hours when documentation is complete. See approval timeline.

Bundling Equipment for Better Terms

Financing multiple items (chairs, X-ray, sterilization, digital systems) in one package can simplify approval and improve terms. Lenders prefer complete fit-outs—it shows a viable practice plan. Work with a dental equipment dealer who can provide a bundled quote. Bundled packages often qualify for 24-48 hour approval when documentation is complete. See approval timeline.

Essential Equipment for New Practices

Core items lenders expect: operatory chairs and delivery systems, lights, X-ray (panoramic or CBCT), sterilization, and cabinetry. Digital systems (intraoral scanners, CEREC) may be included or added later. Ensure your quote covers code-required and practice-essential items. Missing pieces can delay opening. See CEREC financing for digital systems.
