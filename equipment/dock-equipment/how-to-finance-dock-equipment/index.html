What Is Dock Equipment?

Dock equipment includes dock levelers (bridge the gap between dock and truck bed), dock doors, and truck restraints (secure trailers during loading). Levelers can be mechanical, hydraulic, or air-powered. Doors include roll-up, sectional, and high-speed. Truck restraints prevent trailer creep. Equipment streamlines loading and unloading and improves safety. Brands like Rite-Hite, Blue Giant, and 4Front are common. Dock equipment is essential for logistics and warehousing facilities.

How Do You Finance Dock Equipment?

Use equipment loans, leases, or include in SBA or facility financing. Most lenders require credit 600+, down payment 0–20%, and proof of revenue. Approval typically takes 1–5 days for equipment financing. SBA or facility loans can take 30–60+ days. Dock equipment is common facility equipment—financing is widely available through logistics lenders.

How Much Does Dock Equipment Cost?

Dock equipment costs $5,000–$50,000+ per door depending on leveler type, door type, and truck restraints. Basic mechanical levelers run $3,000–$8,000 per door. Hydraulic levelers cost $8,000–$15,000+. Full door packages (leveler, door, restraint) run $15,000–$50,000+ per door. Used equipment typically costs 20–35% less. Many lenders finance used equipment. Obtain a written quote from your supplier.

Financing Options for Dock Equipment

Equipment loans are common—0–20% down, terms of 36–84 months. Rates typically range from 6–15%. Leasing preserves capital. SBA 504 and commercial real estate loans can include dock equipment as part of facility buildout. See equipment loan vs lease.

Dock Equipment Financing FAQ

Can you finance used dock equipment?

Yes. Many lenders finance used dock levelers, doors, and truck restraints in good condition. Used equipment may require a larger down payment and shorter terms.

Can dock equipment be included in facility financing?

Yes. SBA 504 and conventional commercial real estate loans can include dock equipment as part of facility buildout or improvement.

How long does dock equipment financing approval take?

Approval typically takes 1–5 business days for equipment loans and leases. SBA or facility loans can take 30–60+ days.

Is leasing better than buying dock equipment?

It depends. Leasing preserves capital. Buying builds equity. Compare both based on your cash flow.
