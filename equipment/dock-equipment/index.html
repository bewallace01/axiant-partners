Summary

Dock equipment financing—including dock leveler loans, dock door leases, and truck restraint financing—helps warehouses add or replace loading dock equipment without draining working capital. Dock equipment is commonly financed through equipment lenders and SBA programs.

Costs typically run $5,000–$50,000+ per door; used dock equipment financing costs less. Equipment financing spreads payments over 36–60 months.
