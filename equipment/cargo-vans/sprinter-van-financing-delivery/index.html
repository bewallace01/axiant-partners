What Is Sprinter Van Financing?

Sprinter van financing is equipment loans or leases for cargo vans used in delivery and trades. Lenders treat the van as collateral; approval typically takes 24-48 hours. Brands include Mercedes Sprinter, Ford Transit, RAM ProMaster, and similar high-roof cargo vans. Unlike box trucks (Class 3-7 straight trucks), Sprinter vans are lighter, more maneuverable, and suited to last-mile and residential routes.

Why Delivery Businesses Use Sprinter Vans

Sprinter vans fit routes box trucks cannot: tight urban streets, residential driveways, and parking-limited stops. E-commerce last-mile, courier services, trades (HVAC, plumbing, electrical), and mobile businesses rely on cargo vans. One financed van can expand routes, add capacity, or replace an aging unit. See equipment financing by type for related vehicle categories.

Sprinter Van Costs: New vs Used

New Sprinter-style cargo vans run roughly $45,000-$75,000 depending on wheelbase (144" or 170"), roof height, brand, and options. Mercedes Sprinter, Ford Transit, and RAM ProMaster are in similar ranges. Used 3-5 year old vans typically cost 25-40% less. Get a written quote with make, model, year, VIN, and specs. See financing used equipment.

Financing Options: Loans vs Leases

Equipment loans spread the cost over 36-72 months; you own the van at payoff. Leases often have lower payments and full payment deductibility; you can purchase at term end or upgrade. For delivery businesses scaling up, leasing offers flexibility. See equipment loan vs lease. Use our calculator to model payments.

Credit and Down Payment Requirements

Most lenders look for 600+ FICO; 680+ qualifies for the best rates. Down payments: 0-15% for new, 10-20% for used. Time in business and revenue matter—lenders want to see your delivery business can support the payment. See credit score for equipment financing and down payment requirements.

Documentation and Approval Timeline

Gather: van quote (make, model, year, VIN, price), 3-6 months bank statements, tax returns, P&L, formation documents. Complete documentation speeds approval. Many lenders approve in 24-48 hours. See equipment financing requirements and approval timeline.

Rates and Monthly Payments

Rates typically run 6-14%. For a $55,000 Sprinter van at 8% over 60 months with 10% down, expect roughly $950-$1,050/month. Use our calculator. See typical equipment financing rates.

Sprinter Van vs Box Truck: Which to Finance?

Choose based on cargo size and route type. Sprinter vans suit smaller payloads, urban and residential delivery, and trades. Box trucks suit heavier freight, moving, and routes needing 10-26 ft cargo space. Both are commonly financed; lenders understand both. If you need larger capacity, see box truck financing.

Worked example: financing a Sprinter van

Take a $60,000 new high-roof Sprinter cargo van, 10% down, leaving $54,000 over 60 months. At about 9% APR the payment is near $1,121 a month — comfortably covered by a single delivery or service route. For a courier, contractor, or mobile-service business, the predictable payment beats tying up cash to buy outright, and the van itself secures the loan so approval leans on the asset as much as the borrower.

New vs. used for a delivery operation

Used Sprinter-style vans stretch a fleet budget. A 3–5 year-old van can cost 25–40% less than new and still has years of route life, lowering the financed amount and the payment. Lenders finance used cargo vans readily — weighing mileage, age, and condition like any vehicle — though expect a slightly larger down payment (often 10–20% versus 0–15% on new) and a somewhat shorter term. Many operators run one or two new vans alongside several used ones to balance reliability with cost.
