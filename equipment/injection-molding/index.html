Summary

Injection molding financing—including injection molding machine loans and leases—helps plastic manufacturers add or replace molding equipment without draining working capital. Injection molding machines are commonly financed through equipment lenders and SBA programs.

Costs typically run $50,000–$500,000+ for new machines; used injection molding financing costs less. Equipment financing spreads payments over 5–7+ years.
