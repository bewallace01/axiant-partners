Summary

Backhoe financing—including backhoe loans and backhoe leases—helps construction, excavation, and utility businesses add or replace backhoe loaders without draining working capital. Backhoes are commonly financed through equipment lenders and SBA programs.

Costs typically run $50,000–$180,000+ for new equipment; used backhoe financing costs less. Equipment financing spreads payments over 36–60 months.
