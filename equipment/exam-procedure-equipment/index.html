Choosing the Right Exam & Procedure Equipment for Your Practice

Before applying for exam and procedure equipment financing, clarify your needs: patient volume, room count, and specialty mix. Primary care, urgent care, and specialty practices have different requirements·exam tables with varying weight limits, procedure lights with different lumens, treatment chairs for dermatology or dental. Get written quotes from reputable vendors such as Midmark, Hill-Rom, Stryker, or Skytron. Compare new vs used·many lenders finance used exam equipment when condition and compliance are documented. Consider bundling multiple exam rooms for larger deals that may secure better terms. Plan for installation lead times, calibration, and staff training. Use our financing calculator to estimate monthly payments before you apply.

What Lenders Need to See for Exam Room Financing

Practices that submit complete packets usually get decisions within 24–48 hours. Have ready: recent bank statements (3–6 months); tax returns; current-year P&L; a vendor quote listing each item·exam tables, lights, chairs·with make, model, and price; and your LLC or corporate formation papers. Add licensure proof if your specialty requires it. Lenders weigh credit, revenue, and the equipment itself. Group or multi-site practices may be asked for extra documentation.

Tax Treatment for Exam Room Equipment

Exam tables, procedure lights, and treatment chairs usually qualify for Section 179 and bonus depreciation. Leases are typically fully deductible as an operating expense. See our equipment financing overview for how tax treatment works, and talk to your CPA about your situation. A lot of practices finance partly to keep capital free while still taking advantage of deductions.

Cash vs Finance: How Exam Room Decisions Usually Play Out

New or growing practices often finance so cash can go toward buildout, marketing, or hiring instead of a large upfront equipment spend. Upgrades·e.g., power tables, LED lights·are also commonly financed to preserve reserves. Leasing tends to fit when you expect to refresh equipment every 5–7 years; compare leasing vs loans for your cash flow and upgrade plans.
