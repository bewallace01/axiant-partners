What Is Exam & Procedure Equipment?

Exam and procedure equipment includes exam tables, procedure lights, treatment chairs, and procedure room furnishings. Exam tables support patient positioning for physical exams. Procedure lights provide adjustable illumination for exams and minor procedures. Treatment chairs are used in dental, dermatology, and specialty practices. Equipment supports primary care, urgent care, specialty offices, and ambulatory surgery centers. Brands like Midmark, Hill-Rom, Stryker, and Skytron are common. Exam and procedure equipment is essential for medical practices and healthcare providers.

How Do You Finance Exam & Procedure Equipment?

Use equipment loans, leases, or bundle with other practice equipment. Most lenders require credit 600+, down payment 0–20%, and proof of practice revenue. Approval typically takes 1–5 days. Exam equipment is widely financed through healthcare equipment lenders.

How Much Does Exam & Procedure Equipment Cost?

Basic exam tables run $2,000–$5,000. Power exam tables cost $5,000–$15,000+. Procedure lights run $1,000–$10,000. Treatment chairs cost $2,000–$15,000+. Full exam room packages can reach $50,000+. Used equipment typically costs 20–35% less. Many lenders finance used equipment. Obtain a written vendor quote.

Financing Options for Exam & Procedure Equipment

Equipment loans and leases both work. Loans require 0–20% down, terms of 24–60 months. Rates typically range from 6–15%. Leasing offers lower monthly payments. See equipment loan vs lease. Bundling multiple exam rooms may improve terms.

Exam & Procedure Equipment Financing FAQ

Can you finance used exam and procedure equipment?

Yes. Many lenders finance used exam tables, procedure lights, and treatment chairs in good condition. Used equipment may require a larger down payment and shorter terms.

Can you bundle exam room equipment?

Yes. Bundling exam tables, lights, and treatment chairs for multiple rooms may improve terms and simplify one application.

How long does exam equipment financing approval take?

Approval typically takes 1–5 business days for equipment loans and leases. SBA loans can take 30–60+ days.

Is leasing better than buying exam equipment?

It depends. Leasing offers lower monthly payments. Buying builds equity. Compare both based on your cash flow.
