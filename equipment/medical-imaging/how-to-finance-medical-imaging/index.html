What Is Medical Imaging Equipment?

Medical imaging includes X-ray systems, MRI (magnetic resonance imaging), CT (computed tomography) scanners, and ultrasound machines. X-ray systems capture static images for bones and some soft tissue. MRI provides detailed soft tissue imaging. CT scans produce cross-sectional images. Ultrasound uses sound waves for real-time imaging. Imaging is core to diagnosis in primary care, orthopedics, radiology, and specialty practices. Brands like GE, Siemens, Philips, and Fujifilm are common. Imaging equipment is essential for medical practices offering diagnostic services.

How Do You Finance Medical Imaging Equipment?

Use equipment loans, leases, or vendor programs. Most lenders require credit 600+, down payment 0–20%, and proof of practice revenue. Approval typically takes 1–5 days for standard equipment loans. Medical imaging is a specialized asset class—many lenders specialize in healthcare equipment financing.

How Much Does Medical Imaging Equipment Cost?

Digital X-ray systems run $50,000–$150,000+. Ultrasound systems cost $30,000–$150,000+. CT scanners run $150,000–$500,000+. MRI systems cost $500,000–$2,000,000+. Used equipment typically costs 20–40% less. Many lenders finance used equipment. Obtain a written vendor quote.

Financing Options for Medical Imaging

Equipment loans and leases both work. Loans require 0–20% down, terms of 36–84 months for higher-ticket assets. Rates typically range from 6–15%. Leasing offers lower monthly payments and can include technology refresh. See equipment loan vs lease. Vendor financing programs may offer promotional terms.

Medical Imaging Financing FAQ

Can you finance used medical imaging equipment?

Yes. Many lenders finance used X-ray, ultrasound, and some CT/MRI equipment in good condition. Used equipment may require a larger down payment and shorter terms. Regulatory and certification requirements apply.

Does imaging type (X-ray vs MRI) affect financing?

Yes. MRI and CT systems cost more and may have longer terms. X-ray and ultrasound typically have shorter terms. Lenders specialize in medical equipment and understand the asset classes.

How long does medical imaging financing approval take?

Approval typically takes 1–5 business days for equipment loans and leases. Larger deals or SBA-backed financing can take 30–60+ days.

Is leasing better than buying medical imaging?

It depends. Leasing preserves capital and can include upgrades. Buying builds equity. Technology refresh cycles often make leasing attractive for imaging.
