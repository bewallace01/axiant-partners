## Usage Tips

- **AI video tools:** Paste the "Script" section into your tool. Use [HOOK], [PROBLEM], etc. as scene markers if your tool supports chapters.
- **On-screen text:** Add as captions or lower-thirds in your editor.
- **CTA overlay:** Add in post or use your platform’s link sticker.
- **Customize:** Swap “we” for “Axiant Partners,” adjust numbers, add your logo/custom CTA.
