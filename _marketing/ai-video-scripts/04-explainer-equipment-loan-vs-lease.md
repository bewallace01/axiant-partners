**Script:**
Buying equipment? You’ve got two main options: a loan or a lease. Here’s the difference and when to use each.

[LOAN – 0:10–0:28]
A loan: you borrow the full amount, make payments, and own the equipment at the end. You get the depreciation and the asset on your books. Best when you plan to keep it long-term and want to build equity.

[LEASE – 0:28–0:45]
A lease: you pay to use the equipment for a set term. At the end, you return it, buy it at fair market value, or upgrade. Lower monthly payments. Operating leases can treat payments as an expense. Good for tech that changes fast or when you need to preserve cash.

[DECISION – 0:45–0:58]
Short answer: own it long-term? Loan. Need flexibility or lower payments? Lease. Your situation dictates which.
