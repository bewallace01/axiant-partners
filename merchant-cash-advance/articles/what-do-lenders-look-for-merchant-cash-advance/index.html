Can You Get a Merchant Cash Advance With Bad Credit?

Yes, and that is the main reason merchant cash advances exist. A bank lends against your credit history and collateral and wants to know you can make a fixed payment no matter what sales do. An MCA provider buys a share of your future card sales or deposits. Its question is simpler: will enough money keep flowing through your account to cover the holdback? Strong, steady deposits can offset a weak score, and a clean bank account often matters more than a high FICO.

Many programs work with 500+ FICO, and some have no hard minimum at all. Your score still counts. It moves the factor rate and the advance size, so the same deposits with a 540 score will usually price higher than with a 700. Advances run from about $5,000 to $500,000 depending on volume (see merchant cash advance amounts and requirements).

What Do MCA Funders Check When Your Credit Is Weak?

1. Card volume or bank deposits. This is the main driver. Funders want stable or growing volume over the last 3-6 months. Declines, gaps and unexplained seasonal drops raise questions. How much you can qualify for shows how volume sets the advance.

2. Bank statement quality. Frequent NSFs or overdrafts signal cash flow stress and can shrink or kill an offer. Funders also look at how steady deposits are week to week and whether your average balance stays very low.

3. Time in business. Most providers want at least 6 months, and many prefer 12+. Newer businesses can qualify for smaller advances at higher factors.

4. Existing advances. Every open MCA takes a cut of the same deposits. Funders cap your total exposure as a multiple of monthly sales, so stacked advances lead to smaller offers, higher factors or a decline.

5. Credit. Secondary. It affects price and size more than approval.

6. Industry. Restaurants, retail, salons and other card-heavy businesses fit well. Some providers restrict industries such as adult entertainment, cannabis and gambling, so check eligibility first if you are in a niche.

How Much Does a Bad Credit MCA Cost?

An MCA is priced with a factor rate, not an interest rate. You pay back the advance times the factor, and the difference is a fixed dollar cost. Factors commonly run 1.15-1.50, and weaker files sit at the high end. Is There Such a Thing as a No Credit Check MCA?

Be careful with ads that promise "no credit check" or "guaranteed approval." The FTC has warned small businesses about funders and brokers that misstate costs and terms. What is true: credit is a secondary factor, some providers have no minimum score, and applying through Axiant Partners does not involve a hard credit pull. A provider that skips all review of your business is pricing that risk into the factor. Read red flags in MCA agreements before you sign anything.

What Gets an MCA Application Declined?

  • A pattern of NSFs or overdrafts. One or two can be explained; a pattern usually cannot.
  • Falling deposits. A 30-40% drop over two or three months with no explanation can trigger a decline or a much smaller offer.
  • Several open advances. Stacking raises the risk for every funder in line. If you are already there, MCA debt relief covers the ways out.
  • Recent bank or processor changes. Gaps make history hard to verify. Have the old statements and a short explanation ready.
  • Mismatched data. A different business name, amounts or ownership on the application than on the statements can pause or end the file.
  • A brand-new account. Funders want 3-6+ months of consistent data to analyze.

How Do Funders Verify Your Numbers?

Most connect to your bank and processor through a third-party aggregator with read-only access. They read deposit dates and amounts, NSF markers and card volume trends, and compare them with your application. Use the exact legal name, EIN, bank account and ownership that appear on your statements.

How Can You Improve Your Odds With Bad Credit?

  • Keep card volume steady or growing for 3+ months before you apply.
  • Clear NSFs and overdrafts out of the most recent statements.
  • Pay down or pay off existing advances where you can.
  • Write a one-line explanation for any dip (seasonality, a one-time event).
  • Apply in your stronger months if your business is seasonal.

Documents to have ready: 3-6 months of bank statements, card processing statements, your EIN, legal name, ownership and address, and ID for each signer. Once submitted, many providers review within hours, and straightforward files often fund in 1-3 business days. If you are declined, ask whether it was volume, bank health or existing advances, and fix that before you reapply.

Is There Something Cheaper Than an MCA?

If your deposits are steady, check other options before you sign. Working capital loans generally want at least 6 months in business and $10,000 or more in monthly revenue. A business line of credit typically runs 8-13% APR at a bank or 14-30%+ online, a fraction of what a 1.40 factor costs over 6 months. Merchant cash advance alternatives compares them side by side.
