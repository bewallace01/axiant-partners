Iowa is one of the more distinctive state economies for equipment financing because of its concentrated dominant industries: John Deere supplier base, Pioneer/Corteva, Collins Aerospace, corn/soybeans/pork, ethanol. Underwriting is federal on rates; what differs is the asset mix, the IA-active lenders, and state filing rules.

Dominant Iowa Asset Classes

Agriculture (#1 corn, top-tier soybeans, #1 pork)

Iowa is the #1 U.S. corn producer, #1 pork producer, and a top-3 soybean producer. Tractors, combines, planters, sprayers, grain handling, hog facilities all finance through Farm Credit Services of America, Iowa community banks, and specialty ag lenders. Average farm size and intensity make IA the biggest ag-equipment financing market in the U.S.

John Deere supplier base

John Deere is HQ Moline IL but operates major IA plants in Waterloo (engines, tractors), Davenport (construction), Dubuque (forestry/skid steers), Des Moines (drive trains), and Ottumwa (hay/forage). Tier-1 metalworking, machining, and assembly suppliers finance through specialty industrial lenders.

Seed and biotech (Pioneer/Corteva)

Corteva (formerly DuPont Pioneer) is HQ Indianapolis but its R&D and seed-production base is largely Iowa. Bayer Crop Science also operates major IA seed plants. Seed-cleaning, conditioning, packaging equipment finances through specialty ag-tech lenders.

Aerospace (Collins Aerospace, Cedar Rapids)

Collins Aerospace (RTX) HQ in Cedar Rapids employs ~9,000. Avionics, communications, and surveillance equipment manufacturing. Suppliers finance through aerospace-specialty lenders.

Ethanol and biofuels

Iowa is the #1 U.S. ethanol producer (~30% of national capacity). Renewable fuel processing equipment, fermentation tanks, distillation, and rail loadout finance through specialty energy and ag lenders.

UCC and Title Filings in Iowa

UCC-1 financing statements file with the Iowa Secretary of State at sos.iowa.gov. Filings are central and statewide; real-property fixture filings record with the county recorder. Titled vehicles register with IA DOT.

Iowa Business Structure

IA LLCs and corporations register with the Secretary of State and file biennial reports. Iowa has a graduated corporate income tax (5.5-9.8%, with reductions phasing through 2028 to a flat 5.5%). Sole proprietors operate under SSN or EIN; trade-name filings register with the county recorder.

Iowa-Regional Lenders

Hills Bank, GreenState Credit Union, Bankers Trust (Des Moines), West Bancorporation, Wells Fargo Iowa, and Farm Credit Services of America are IA-active. FCSA is the dominant ag lender. Specialty industrial lenders work the Quad Cities (John Deere) and Cedar Rapids (Collins Aerospace) corridors.
