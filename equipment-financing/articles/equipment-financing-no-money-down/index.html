Equipment Financing No Money Down: 100% Financing Guide

Who qualifies, what lenders look for, and how to improve your odds of zero-down approval

← Back to Equipment Financing Articles

Last updated: March 9, 2026

No-money-down equipment financing—also called 100% financing—lets you acquire equipment without an upfront cash investment. It is available for qualified borrowers: typically strong credit, stable revenue, and equipment with solid resale value. This guide explains who qualifies, what lenders require, and how to improve your odds of approval.

Can You Get Equipment Financing With No Money Down?

Yes. Many equipment financing programs offer 100% financing for qualified businesses. Equipment loans and leases are asset-backed: the equipment itself secures the financing. If you default, the lender repossesses and resells it. That collateral reduces lender risk and allows zero-down programs for borrowers who meet credit and revenue thresholds. No-money-down is more common for equipment than for real estate or unsecured loans. See down payment requirements for the full range of options.

Who Qualifies for No-Money-Down Equipment Financing?

Lenders typically look for:

  • Credit score: 650–700+ FICO. Stronger credit (720+) has the best odds. Below 650, expect a down payment for most programs.
  • Time in business: 1–2+ years preferred. Some lenders accept 6+ months with strong credit and revenue.
  • Revenue: Consistent monthly revenue ($15,000–$25,000+ often helps). Bank statements show ability to repay.
  • Equipment type: Assets with strong resale markets (construction, trucks, manufacturing, medical, restaurant) approve more easily.
  • Clean financials: No major defaults, minimal overdrafts, stable deposit patterns.

If your credit is below 650, a down payment of 10–20% can improve approval. See equipment financing with bad credit for strategies.

Equipment Type: What Qualifies for 100% Financing?

Equipment with established resale markets is easiest to finance with no money down. Lenders can value and liquidate it quickly. Favorable categories include:

  • Construction: Excavators, loaders, skid steers, dump trucks
  • Transportation: Semi-trucks, box trucks, trailers
  • Manufacturing: CNC machines, forklifts, industrial equipment
  • Medical: Imaging, dental, surgical, lab equipment
  • Restaurant: Commercial kitchens, refrigeration, POS systems

New equipment typically qualifies more easily than used. Highly customized or niche equipment may require a down payment. Browse equipment financing by type for industry-specific options.

Loan vs Lease: Which Offers No Money Down?

Both equipment loans and leases can offer 100% financing. Leases sometimes have a slight edge for zero-down because the lessor retains ownership and bears residual risk; they may approve no-money-down when the equipment has strong resale value. Loans also offer 100% financing for qualified borrowers. Compare structures in equipment loan vs lease. Apply to both and see which returns the best terms for your profile.

Is No-Money-Down Equipment Financing More Expensive?

Financing 100% of the equipment means the lender has more exposure if you default. Rates may be slightly higher than with a 10–20% down payment. For strong-credit borrowers, the difference is often modest—perhaps a few points on the rate. For moderate credit, a down payment can meaningfully improve terms. Use our loan calculator to compare monthly payments and total cost with and without a down payment. See typical equipment financing rates for rate ranges.

How to Improve Your Odds of Zero-Down Approval

  • Strengthen your credit: Pay down revolving balances, dispute errors. A 50-point improvement can shift you into a stronger tier.
  • Demonstrate revenue stability: Clean bank statements, consistent deposits, no overdrafts.
  • Choose strong-resale equipment: Construction and trucking equipment approve more easily.
  • Apply when revenue is strong: Avoid applying during your slowest month.
  • Consider a small down payment: Even 5% can improve approval and rates if you are borderline.
  • Apply through a marketplace: One application can reach multiple lenders; some may offer 100% when others do not.

When a Down Payment Makes More Sense

No money down is not always the best choice. A down payment can:

  • Reduce your interest rate and total cost
  • Lower your monthly payment
  • Improve approval odds if your credit is moderate
  • Shorten the payback period

If you have the cash and want to minimize cost, a 10–20% down payment may save thousands over the life of the financing. If preserving capital is the priority and your credit is strong, 100% financing can make sense. See down payment requirements for the full tradeoff.

Documentation for No-Money-Down Applications

Lenders want to verify that you can repay. Typical requirements: 3–6 months of bank statements, equipment quote or invoice, business formation documents, and sometimes tax returns. Strong documentation supports approval. See equipment financing requirements for the full checklist.

Red Flags to Avoid

Beware of "guaranteed" no-money-down with bad credit or "no credit check" programs. Legitimate 100% financing requires qualification. Extremely high rates or confusing terms are red flags. See red flags in equipment finance agreements before signing.

Summary

Equipment financing with no money down is available for qualified borrowers: typically 650–700+ FICO, 1–2+ years in business, stable revenue, and equipment with strong resale value. Strong credit and solid financials improve odds. If your profile does not quite meet zero-down thresholds, a small down payment can unlock approval and better rates.
