SBA Loans

Government-backed small business financing with favorable rates and terms

What Are SBA Loans?

The Small Business Administration (SBA) guarantees loans made by banks and other lenders, which can mean lower interest rates, longer repayment terms, and lower down payments for qualifying businesses. Axiant Partners connects you with SBA lenders in our network so you can access 7(a), 504, and microloan programs.

SBA 7(a) Loans

The most common SBA program. Loans up to $5 million for working capital, equipment, inventory, debt refinancing, and more. Terms and rates are competitive and set within SBA guidelines.

SBA 504 Loans

For major fixed assets—real estate, buildings, and long-term equipment. Often lower down payments and terms of 10–25 years through a partnership with a Certified Development Company.

SBA Microloans

Smaller loans (up to $50,000) for startups and small businesses that need a modest amount of capital to get started or expand.
