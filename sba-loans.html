SBA Loans for Established U.S. Businesses

Long-Term Government-Backed Financing Nationwide

Axiant Partners provides structured SBA loan solutions for established businesses seeking working capital, equipment financing, commercial real estate, and more. We connect you with SBA 7(a) and 504 loan programs designed for long-term, government-backed financing nationwide.

What Is an SBA Loan?

An SBA loan is a business loan partially guaranteed by the Small Business Administration. Because the SBA reduces lender risk, qualifying businesses often receive longer repayment terms, competitive interest rates, lower down payments, and higher leverage than conventional bank loans.

  • Longer repayment terms (10–25 years)
  • Competitive interest rates
  • Lower down payments
  • Flexible use for working capital, equipment, real estate
  • Higher leverage than conventional loans

Our solutions help established businesses access government-backed financing with favorable terms. We navigate 7(a) and 504 programs to find the right fit for your needs.

Types of SBA Loan Programs

We provide several SBA financing options to ensure your business finds the best fit.

SBA 7(a) Loans

The most common SBA program. Flexible for working capital, business acquisition, debt refinancing, and commercial real estate.

  • Loan amounts up to $5 million
  • Terms up to 10–25 years depending on use
  • Variable or fixed rate options
  • Working capital, equipment, real estate, acquisition, refinancing

SBA 504 Loans

For major fixed assets—owner-occupied commercial real estate and long-term equipment. Offers long-term fixed rates and structured financing.

  • 10% borrower down payment
  • 40% SBA-backed second lien
  • 50% bank first lien
  • Long-term fixed rates
  • 10–25 year terms

What Can SBA Loans Be Used For?

SBA financing typically covers a wide range of business needs:

  • Business expansion and growth
  • Commercial property purchase
  • Equipment and machinery
  • Debt refinancing
  • Working capital
  • Business acquisition

What SBA loans are not designed for: Startups without revenue, speculative investing, or short-term bridge financing.

SBA 7(a) Financing

Common uses: Working capital, equipment, inventory, debt refinancing, real estate, business acquisition

7(a) is the most flexible SBA program. Loans up to $5 million with terms and rates set within SBA guidelines. Variable or fixed rate options available.

SBA 504 Financing

Common uses: Owner-occupied commercial real estate, long-term equipment, major fixed assets

504 loans pair a bank first lien (50%) with an SBA-backed second lien (40%), requiring 10% borrower down payment. Long-term fixed rates and 10–25 year terms through Certified Development Companies.

How SBA Loan Financing Works

01

Application & Documentation

Submit your business information and documentation. SBA loans require more paperwork than conventional loans—business plan, tax returns, financial statements, and personal guarantees.

02

Underwriting & SBA Review

Lender evaluates your application and submits to SBA for guarantee. Underwriting typically takes 30–60+ days depending on program and documentation.

03

Approval & Closing

Once approved, closing proceeds. Funds are disbursed for working capital, equipment, real estate, or other approved uses.

04

Funding

Funds are disbursed according to your approved use. Repayment begins per your loan terms—typically 10–25 years for 7(a) and 504.

SBA Loan Requirements

General requirements for most SBA programs:

  • Business Operating History: 2+ years in business (typically)
  • Credit Score: 650–680+ FICO typically
  • Profitability: Demonstrated profitability or strong cash flow
  • U.S.-Based Operations: Business must operate in the United States

Personal Guarantees: Typically required for SBA loans. Lenders evaluate your financial profile holistically when assessing eligibility.

Benefits of SBA Financing

Lower Down Payments

Government backing allows lenders to offer higher leverage and lower down payments than conventional bank loans.

Longer Terms

10–25 year terms help preserve cash flow and align repayment with your business cycle.

Competitive Rates

SBA guarantees reduce lender risk, often resulting in competitive interest rates for qualifying businesses.

Flexible Use

7(a) funds can be used for working capital, equipment, real estate, acquisition, and refinancing—tailored to your needs.

What Determines SBA Loan Approval?

  • Business Profile: Revenue, profitability, years in business, industry
  • Credit Strength: Personal and business credit scores
  • Use of Funds: Loan purpose and structure
  • Collateral: Varies by program and loan amount

Lenders evaluate these factors when underwriting. Strong businesses with clear use of funds typically qualify for favorable terms.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much can I borrow with an SBA loan?

SBA 7(a) loans go up to $5 million. 504 loans vary by project and program guidelines.

How long does SBA loan approval take?

Typically 30–60 days or more, depending on program and documentation. Plan accordingly—SBA loans are not for emergencies.

Is collateral required?

Collateral requirements vary by program and loan amount. Personal guarantees are typically required for SBA loans.

Can startups qualify for SBA loans?

Most SBA programs favor established businesses with 2+ years in business and demonstrated profitability. Startups may find alternatives such as microloans or other products.
