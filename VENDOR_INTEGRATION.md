# Vendor Integration Guide

## Quick Setup

Add your email address to the form URL. When customers submit applications through your link, you'll automatically receive a copy via email.

**Important:** Your email address must be whitelisted in Axiant Partners' EmailJS service settings. Contact alex@axiantpartners.com to have your email added.

### Your Form URL
Replace `your-email@example.com` with your actual email:

```
https://axiantpartners.com/match.html?vendor=your-email@example.com
```

### How to Use

**Option 1: Direct Link (Recommended)**
```html
<a href="https://axiantpartners.com/match.html?vendor=your-email@example.com" target="_blank">
   Apply for Business Financing
</a>
```

**Option 2: Embed in iframe**
```html
<iframe src="https://axiantpartners.com/match.html?vendor=your-email@example.com" 
        width="100%" height="1200" frameborder="0"></iframe>
```

### What You'll Receive

When someone submits through your link, you'll get an email with:
- Customer contact information
- Loan details (amount, type, credit score)
- Business information
- Application reference number
