What Is Working Capital Financing?

Working capital is the money you use to run your business day to day. A working capital loan or line of credit helps you cover payroll, rent, inventory, utilities, and other short-term needs when cash flow is tight or seasonal. Axiant Partners matches you with lenders that offer term loans and lines of credit for working capital.

Term Loans

A lump sum you repay over a set period with fixed or variable payments. Use the funds for operating expenses, seasonal gaps, or one-time needs.

Lines of Credit

Revolving credit: draw when you need it, repay, and draw again. Pay interest only on what you use. Ideal for ongoing cash flow management.

Invoice Financing

Use outstanding invoices to get an advance. Improves cash flow without waiting for customers to pay. Part of our services.
