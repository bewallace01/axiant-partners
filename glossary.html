Business Financing Glossary

Definitions of common business loan and financing terms

Key Terms

APR (Annual Percentage Rate)
The cost of borrowing expressed as a yearly rate, including interest and certain fees. Use it to compare loan offers.
Collateral
Assets pledged to secure a loan. If you default, the lender can seize the collateral (e.g., equipment, real estate).
Term Loan
A loan with a fixed amount disbursed upfront and repaid in regular installments over a set period (the term).
Line of Credit
Revolving credit that lets you draw funds up to a limit, repay, and draw again. Interest is typically paid only on the amount drawn.
Working Capital
Cash and assets used for day-to-day operations (payroll, inventory, rent, utilities). Working capital loans fund these short-term needs.
SBA 7(a) Loan
The SBA's main loan program. The SBA guarantees a portion of loans made by banks, often resulting in lower rates and longer terms for small businesses.
SBA 504 Loan
An SBA program for major fixed assets (real estate, equipment). Involves a lender and a Certified Development Company (CDC); often features lower down payments and long terms.
Equipment Financing
Loans or leases used to acquire business equipment. The equipment often serves as collateral for a loan.
Equipment Lease
An agreement to use equipment for a set period in exchange for payments. May include an option to buy at the end of the term.
Microloan
A small loan (e.g., SBA microloans up to $10,000) aimed at startups and small businesses that need a modest amount of capital.
Amortization
The process of paying off a loan with regular payments that cover both principal and interest over time.
Debt Service
Total principal and interest payments due on debt over a period. Lenders often look at debt service coverage (income vs. debt payments).
