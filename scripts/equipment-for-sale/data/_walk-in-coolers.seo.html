Walk-In Coolers & Freezers financing — FAQ

How much does a walk-in cooler or freezer cost?

Walk-in boxes typically range from around $10,000 for compact indoor coolers to $40,000 or more for large combination cooler and freezer units. Nor-Lake combination boxes commonly land around $17,000 to $23,000. Size, refrigeration type (self-contained or remote), and indoor or outdoor rating drive the final quote.

Can I finance a walk-in cooler with Axiant?

Yes. Axiant finances new and used walk-in coolers and freezers with terms up to 60 to 72 months and approvals in as little as 24 hours, for startups and established operators alike, subject to credit approval.

Should I buy a new or used walk-in?

Both are financeable. A new box comes with full warranty and exact-fit sizing, while a used or quick-ship unit can lower the upfront cost. Financing either way keeps cash available for the rest of your buildout.

How fast can I get a walk-in cooler?

Lead times vary by manufacturer and whether the box is a quick-ship or custom size — the dealer confirms availability. Financing can be pre-approved in about 24 hours, so you are ready to order the moment you choose a unit.
