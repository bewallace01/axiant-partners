Combi Ovens financing — FAQ

How much does a commercial combi oven cost?

Combi ovens generally run from about $14,000 for smaller electric and half-size units up to $50,000 or more for full-size gas production floor models. Mid-size 10- and 20-pan units usually fall between roughly $17,000 and $32,000. Exact pricing is quoted by the dealer for the specific model and configuration.

Can I finance a combi oven through Axiant?

Yes. Axiant arranges financing on new and used combi ovens with terms up to 60 to 72 months and approvals in as little as 24 hours. Startups and established restaurants are both eligible, subject to credit approval.

Should I finance a new or a used combi oven?

Both are financeable. New units carry full warranties and the latest controls, while certified used units lower the upfront figure. Because a combi oven is a long-life, high-ticket asset, financing either way keeps your opening cash free for inventory, labor, and rent.

Does it matter if the combi oven is electric or gas?

Not for financing — both qualify. The choice usually comes down to your kitchen's available utilities and ventilation. We finance either based on the dealer's quoted price.
