Quick answer

A $50K business loan is one of the easier amounts to get. Most lenders want 6+ months in business, about $10,000+ a month in revenue and a 550+ credit score, and they often decide within a day or two on 3-6 months of bank statements. Working capital loans and lines of credit fund in 1-3 days, usually without collateral; an SBA loan costs less but takes 30-60+ days. At about 13% over 24 months the payment is roughly $2,380 a month, or about $1,110 at 12% over 60 months.

How do you qualify for a $50K loan?

Lenders at this size read your bank statements more than anything else. They want to see about $10,000 or more coming in each month, steady from month to month, with few overdrafts. Six months of history is the usual floor, and 550 is the usual credit floor. A 680+ score gets you the lower rates and the longer terms.

To apply, pull 3-6 months of business bank statements, know what the $50,000 is for, and send one application that reaches several lenders instead of applying bank by bank. Collateral usually isn't required at this size, but expect to sign a personal guarantee. Want to check the payment first? Use the business loan calculator.

What are the requirements for a $50,000 business loan?

Requirements vary by lender and program, but most businesses can qualify for $50,000 with:

  • 6+ months in business; some programs accept newer businesses with strong personal credit
  • ~$10,000+ in monthly revenue to show you can repay
  • 550+ FICO; 680+ gets the best rates and longest terms
  • Basic documentation: typically 3-6 months of business bank statements

Have challenged credit? See business loans for bad credit; $50,000 is within reach even while you rebuild. Apply to see what you qualify for.

What changes if you need $100,000 instead?

The bar goes up one step: about 1+ year in business, $15,000-$20,000+ a month in revenue and a 600+ score, and SBA or bank lenders ask for more paperwork. The $100,000 business loan page has payments at that size. Need less? A $25,000 business loan is the easiest size to get.

$50K business loan FAQs

How do I qualify for a $50,000 business loan?

Most lenders want 6+ months in business, about $10,000+ in monthly revenue and a 550+ credit score, plus 3-6 months of business bank statements. A 680+ score gets the best rates and longest terms.

What credit score do I need for a $50,000 business loan?

Many programs accept 550+, and 680+ typically gets lower rates and longer terms. Lenders also work with challenged credit; see business loans for bad credit.

What would the monthly payment be on a $50,000 business loan?

It depends on the rate and term. About $1,730 a month over 36 months at 15%, or about $1,110 over 60 months at 12%. Short-term money can cost far more: at 30% over 12 months the payment is about $4,870.

How fast can I get $50,000 in business funding?

Short-term and working capital programs often fund within 1-3 business days of approval. SBA-backed options take 30-60+ days but have lower rates and longer terms.

Can I get a $50,000 business loan with no collateral?

Yes. Unsecured $50,000 working capital loans and lines of credit are available for qualified businesses. A personal guarantee is common at this size.

Can I get a $50,000 loan to start a new business?

It's harder, because most $50,000 programs want about 6+ months of deposits. Some accept newer businesses with strong personal credit; if you're pre-revenue, look at startup financing, which leans on founder credit, reserves and a clear use of funds.

Is there a $50,000 business loan with no credit check?

A true no-credit-check loan is rare and often a warning sign. Most legitimate lenders run a soft check that doesn't affect your score, and some products lean mostly on revenue, such as merchant cash advances from about 500 credit. See no credit check business loans.
