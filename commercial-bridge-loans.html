Commercial Bridge Loans Nationwide

Short-Term Financing from $10,000 to $10,000,000+

Commercial bridge loans provide short-term financing for time-sensitive transactions, acquisition gaps, and transitional commercial property opportunities.

What Is a Commercial Bridge Loan?

A commercial bridge loan is temporary financing designed to bridge a short-term capital need until permanent financing or a planned exit event occurs.

Common use cases include:

  • Acquisition before conventional financing is available
  • Refinancing maturing debt quickly
  • Construction completion or lease-up periods
  • Value-add or repositioning transactions
  • Asset purchases with deferred takeout financing

When Are Bridge Loans Used?

  • Fast acquisition timelines where permanent financing is not ready
  • Properties needing stabilization before conventional underwriting
  • Debt payoff situations with near-term maturity pressure
  • Interim capital while a property is renovated or improved
  • Complex transactions requiring flexibility in structure

Bridge financing is generally intended for short durations before refinance or asset sale.

Loan Amounts & Terms

Commercial bridge facilities generally range from $10,000 to $10,000,000+, depending on property value and transaction complexity.

  • Term lengths commonly 6 to 36 months
  • Interest-only structures are common
  • LTV/LTC varies by asset type and sponsor profile
  • Rates and fees reflect speed, flexibility, and execution risk

What Do Lenders Look For?

  • Property quality and marketability
  • Exit strategy clarity
  • Sponsor experience and liquidity
  • Asset-level cash flow trends
  • Credit profile

Because bridge loans are short-term instruments, underwriting focuses heavily on collateral and repayment plan certainty.

Exit Strategy Matters

Most bridge loans rely on a defined exit plan, such as:

  • Sale of property asset
  • Refinance into permanent financing
  • Portfolio recapitalization
  • Payoff from business liquidity event

Without a clear exit, approval becomes significantly harder.

Bridge Loan vs Commercial Mortgage

Feature Bridge Loan Commercial Mortgage
Term 6 to 36 months 10 to 25+ years
Purpose Transitional financing Long-term asset financing
Approval Speed Fast Slower
Interest Structure Usually interest-only Amortizing
Cost Profile Higher (for speed/flexibility) Lower for stabilized assets

Typical Closing Timeline

Bridge loans can often close faster than conventional CRE financing.

  • Fast-track transactions may close in 7 to 21 business days
  • Complex cases can take longer due to appraisal, legal, or title issues

Credit Requirements

Bridge lenders evaluate both sponsor profile and collateral. While requirements vary, many structures are available for borrowers below traditional bank thresholds when compensating factors are strong.

  • Common range: 620+ credit profile
  • Strong liquidity and exit strategy can offset moderate credit
  • Asset quality and market demand are key drivers

Risks of Bridge Loans

  • Higher pricing than permanent financing
  • Short maturity windows require strict planning
  • Refinance risk if market conditions shift
  • Potential extension fees or additional covenants

Proper structuring and realistic exit assumptions are essential.

Who Uses Commercial Bridge Loans?

  • Real estate investors and operators
  • Developers and value-add sponsors
  • Business owners acquiring property quickly
  • Borrowers transitioning to permanent debt
