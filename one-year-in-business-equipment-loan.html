What Is a 1 Year In Business Equipment Loan?

A 1 year in business equipment loan is financing for equipment when your company has been operating for at least 12 months. Many lenders use "time in business" as a filter-once you hit one year, more options open up. You're past the earliest startup stage but may not have years of tax returns.
