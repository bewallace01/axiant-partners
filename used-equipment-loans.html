Can I Finance Used Equipment?

Yes. Many lenders offer used equipment loans and leasing for pre-owned machinery, vehicles, and equipment. Used equipment financing can mean lower upfront cost while still spreading payments over time. We connect you with lenders that finance used construction equipment, farm equipment, trucks, and more.

Typical loan terms

Terms are often shorter than for new equipment (e.g., 2-5 years), and loan amounts are based on the equipment's age, condition, and value. Rates may be slightly higher than for new equipment. Use our loan calculator to estimate payments.

Who qualifies

Businesses buying used equipment for operations. Lenders consider the equipment's value and condition, your credit, and business revenue. We match you with lenders that offer used equipment financing for your industry.
