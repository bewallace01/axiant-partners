Quick answer

The Axiant Partners referral program pays partners 35% of what Axiant earns on a funded deal, with written terms and non-circumvention protections that keep the referral relationship yours. You refer qualified business financing opportunities; Axiant places them with lenders and pays commission when the deal funds.
