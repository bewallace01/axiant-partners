Construction Equipment Financing

Loans and leasing for excavators, loaders, bulldozers, and heavy machinery-built for contractors

What Is Construction Equipment Financing?

Construction equipment financing lets you buy or lease excavators, loaders, bulldozers, dump trucks, and other heavy machinery without paying the full cost upfront. Whether you need a loan to own or a lease to preserve cash flow, we connect you with lenders that specialize in construction equipment loans.

Typical loan terms

Terms often range from 2-7 years depending on equipment life. Loan amounts can go from tens of thousands into the millions. Rates and down payments vary by lender, credit, and equipment type; we match you with options that fit your profile.

Who qualifies

Contractors, construction companies, and businesses that use heavy equipment for projects. Lenders look at business revenue, time in business, credit history, and the equipment being financed. We work with established contractors and growing operations alike.
