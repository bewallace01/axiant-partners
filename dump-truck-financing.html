Dump Truck Financing

Need financing for a dump truck? We can help. New or used-we match you with lenders that offer dump truck loans.

What Is Dump Truck Financing?

Dump truck financing is a loan or lease that lets you get behind the wheel of a dump truck without paying the full price upfront. You make monthly payments over a set term; at the end of a loan you own the truck. It's that simple. Whether you're a contractor, hauler, or owner-operator, you need a truck that works-and you don't always have six figures in the bank. That's where dump truck financing comes in. We connect you with lenders that specialize in commercial trucks so you can get approved and get to work.

What Dump Trucks Can Be Financed?

New dump trucks. Buying from a dealer? Most lenders will finance a new dump truck. You'll get a quote, we match you with a lender, and you secure the loan or lease before you sign at the dealer.

Used dump trucks. Yes-used dump truck financing is common. Lenders look at the truck's age, condition, and value. Terms may be a bit shorter than for new, but you can still spread payments over several years. If you're searching "used dump truck financing" or "used dump truck financing with bad credit," we work with lenders that consider both.

Private seller. Buying from a private seller? Some of our lenders fund private-party purchases. You'll need a bill of sale and details on the truck; we'll match you with lenders that allow it.

Dealer purchase. Dealer purchases are the most straightforward. The dealer often works with multiple lenders; we add another option by matching you with our network so you can compare terms.

Terms & Requirements

Loan amounts. Dump truck loans can range from roughly $30,000 to $150,000+ depending on the truck (single axle, tandem, tri-axle). We match you with lenders that finance in the range you need.

Credit flexibility. Your credit doesn't have to be perfect. We work with lenders that consider fair credit and some that offer bad credit dump truck financing. Rates and terms depend on your profile-we find the best fit.

Time in business. Some lenders want to see a year or two in business; others work with newer operations. Tell us your situation and we'll match you accordingly.

Down payment. Down payments vary. Zero down is rare for equipment, but 10-20% is common. More down can mean better rates or approval. We'll connect you with lenders that fit your budget.
