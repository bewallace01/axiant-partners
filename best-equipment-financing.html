The Best Equipment Financing, Reviewed

Best Overall

Equipment Loan

When you want to own the machine, vehicle, or system, an equipment loan is hard to beat. The equipment itself serves as collateral, which keeps rates reasonable and preserves your working capital for everything else. You build equity and keep the asset at the end of the term. Explore the full equipment financing guides.

Best for: long-lived equipment you'll keep · Watch-out: tied to the asset

Best for Preserving Cash

Equipment Lease

A lease keeps your upfront cost low and your cash free, and it makes upgrading easier when equipment becomes outdated. It's the top pick when flexibility and cash flow matter more than ownership—or when the equipment depreciates quickly. Compare against buying with our equipment guides hub.

Best for: fast-depreciating or short-use equipment · Watch-out: you don't build equity unless there's a buyout

Best for Startups & Bad Credit

Vendor or Lease Financing

Because the equipment secures the deal, vendor financing and leasing programs approve a wider range of borrowers—making them the most accessible route for newer businesses or challenged credit. Strong revenue helps offset a weaker score. See bad credit business loans and startup financing.

Best for: under 2 years or rebuilding credit · Watch-out: terms reflect the added risk

Best for Heavy / Used Equipment

Asset-Secured Equipment Loan

For heavy machinery, trucks, or quality used equipment, an asset-secured equipment loan leans on the value of the machine itself—so even big-ticket purchases stay financeable. Common across construction, manufacturing, and transportation. See construction financing and manufacturing financing.

Best for: high-value or used equipment · Watch-out: appraisal/valuation may be required

Best for Fast Funding

Streamlined Equipment Financing

When timing matters, streamlined equipment programs fund quickly—often within a few business days for smaller amounts with light documentation—because the asset reduces lender risk. Ideal when a deal or replacement can't wait.

Best for: urgent purchases · Watch-out: smaller amounts move fastest

Best for Ongoing Purchases

Equipment Line of Credit

If you buy equipment regularly, an equipment-oriented line of credit lets you draw, repay, and reuse—paying only for what you use. Best for businesses scaling a fleet or tooling over time.

Best for: recurring equipment needs · Watch-out: discipline required to manage the balance

Best Equipment Financing FAQs

What is the best way to finance equipment?

It depends on your goal. An equipment loan is best overall when you want to own the asset and build equity. A lease is best when preserving cash and flexibility matter more. For newer businesses or challenged credit, vendor or lease financing is the most accessible.

Is it better to lease or buy equipment?

Buying builds ownership and usually costs less over the life of long-lived equipment you'll keep. Leasing preserves cash and makes upgrading easier for equipment that dates quickly. Long useful life and you'll keep it → buy; fast depreciation or you want flexibility → lease.

Can I finance equipment with bad credit?

Often yes. Because the equipment secures the financing, equipment loans and leases are more accessible than unsecured options. Vendor and lease programs in particular approve a wider range of borrowers, and strong revenue can offset a weaker score. See bad credit business loans.

How fast can I get equipment financing?

Streamlined programs can fund within a few business days for smaller amounts with light documentation, because the asset reduces lender risk. Larger purchases take longer.
