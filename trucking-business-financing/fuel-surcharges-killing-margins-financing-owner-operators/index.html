How Fuel Costs Hit Margins

Diesel prices and fuel surcharges that don’t keep pace with actual costs squeeze owner-operator margins. You pay for fuel upfront; freight pay comes later. That timing gap, combined with volatile diesel prices, can strain cash flow. Axiant connects owner-operators with trucking financing options to bridge the gap.

Equipment Financing for Trucks

Equipment financing for tractors and trailers spreads the cost over time instead of a lump sum. That frees cash for fuel, maintenance, and operating expenses. Axiant can match you with lenders who finance semi-trucks and heavy-duty equipment. See equipment financing for tractors and trailers.

Working Capital for Fuel and Maintenance

Working capital loans provide lump-sum capital for fuel, tires, repairs, and insurance when margins are tight. Use it to cover the gap between fuel costs and freight payment. Axiant matches you with lenders who understand trucking cycles. See fuel advance and cash crunch for more on managing fuel timing.

Fuel Advances and Factoring

Fuel advance programs and freight factoring can accelerate payment on loads so you have cash for the next tank. Axiant can help connect you with options that fit your operation. Combine with equipment financing and working capital for a full financing toolkit.

Final Thoughts

When fuel surcharges and diesel costs squeeze margins, Axiant can help. Equipment financing for tractors and trailers, working capital for fuel and maintenance, and fuel advances can bridge the gap. Get matched with lenders who work with owner-operators.
