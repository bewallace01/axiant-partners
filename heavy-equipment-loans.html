Heavy Equipment Loans

Finance or lease excavators, loaders, cranes, and industrial machinery—loan for heavy equipment that fits your business

What Are Heavy Equipment Loans?

Heavy equipment loans are used to purchase or lease excavators, loaders, cranes, graders, and other large machinery. The equipment typically serves as collateral, and terms are tailored to the asset's useful life. We connect you with lenders that specialize in loans for heavy machinery.

Typical loan terms

Terms often run 3–7 years. Loan amounts can be $10,000 into the millions. Down payments and rates depend on credit, equipment type, and lender. Use our loan calculator to estimate payments.

Who qualifies

Construction, mining, agriculture, manufacturing, and other businesses that use heavy equipment. Lenders consider business revenue, time in business, credit, and the equipment. We match you with lenders whose criteria fit your profile.
