What Is Dozer Financing?

Dozer financing (or bulldozer financing) is a loan or lease that lets you get a dozer without paying the full price upfront. You make monthly payments; with a loan you own the machine when it's paid off. Dozers are the backbone of site work-clearing, grading, pushing material. They're also a big investment. Whether you're searching "dozer financing" or "dozer financing near me," we connect you with lenders that finance bulldozers so you can get the machine and get on the job.

What Dozers Can Be Financed?

New dozers. Buying from a dealer? Lenders routinely finance new bulldozers. Get your quote, we match you with a lender, and you lock in financing before you buy.

Used dozers. Used dozer financing is common. Lenders look at age, hours, condition, and value. Terms may be a bit shorter than for new, but you can still finance used dozers.

Private seller. Buying from a private party? Some of our lenders fund private purchases with a bill of sale and equipment details.

Dealer purchase. Dealer purchases are the most straightforward. We add options by matching you with our network so you can compare terms.
