Quick Questions, Straight Answers

A few common questions business owners ask before applying.

Does it cost anything to apply?

No. Our matching and guidance is free for business owners. Banks compensate us when a match is successful.

How fast will I hear back?

Typically the same day. A specialist follows up to confirm your needs and the best-fit programs before lender conversations move forward.

What happens after I submit?

We review your request, identify likely-fit lenders, and then connect you directly with decision-makers so you can confirm terms and next steps.

What should I have ready?

It helps to know your funding goal, timeline, business basics, and (if relevant) an equipment quote or project details. If anything’s missing, we’ll tell you exactly what’s needed.
