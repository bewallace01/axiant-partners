How Fast Can You Get Revenue-Based Financing?

Typical timeline, what drives speed, and how RBF compares to other options

← Back to Revenue-Based Financing Articles

Last updated: March 3, 2026

Revenue-based financing is typically faster than traditional bank loans, SBA loans, and conventional term loans. Timelines depend on documentation readiness and revenue strength. Here's what to expect.

Typical Revenue-Based Financing Timeline

Many revenue-based financing programs fund within 3-10 business days. A typical sequence:

  • Initial application review: 24-72 hours
  • Conditional approval: 1-3 business days
  • Underwriting review: 2-5 business days
  • Funding: 3-10 business days total

Why Revenue-Based Financing Is Faster

RBF underwriting focuses on revenue history, bank deposits, and cash flow trends. It typically does not require extensive tax return review, appraisals, collateral valuation, or long committee approvals. Revenue-driven underwriting allows quicker decisions.

What Lenders Review During Approval

Common requests: 3-12 months of bank statements, revenue reports, basic business information, and credit authorization. The absence of collateral requirements streamlines the process. See what lenders look for in revenue-based financing for full underwriting factors.

What Can Slow Down Funding?

  • Incomplete documentation
  • Irregular deposits
  • Frequent overdrafts
  • High existing debt
  • Inconsistent revenue trends

Missing financial documentation is the most common delay. Prepared borrowers fund faster.

Fastest Realistic Scenario

In ideal conditions, funding can occur in 3-5 business days: clean banking history, consistent monthly revenue, organized documentation, and a clear growth use case.

Minimum Funding Amount

Revenue-based financing usually starts at $10,000 and scales with monthly revenue performance. See how much you can qualify for for factors that influence funding amounts.

When Speed Should Not Be the Only Factor

Also consider: total repayment amount, revenue share percentage, impact on monthly cash flow, and growth return on capital. Fast capital helps�but sustainable structure matters more.

Final Thoughts

Revenue-based financing is one of the fastest structured growth capital solutions available. If you have consistent monthly revenue and need flexible growth capital, review structured revenue-based financing options to compare programs and timelines.
