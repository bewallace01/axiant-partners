Wood Chipper and Grinder Financing

Need financing for a wood chipper or grinder? We match you with lenders for forestry, land clearing, and tree care equipment.

What Is Wood Chipper and Grinder Financing?

Wood chipper and grinder financing is a loan or lease that lets you get a chipper, stump grinder, tub grinder, or horizontal grinder without paying the full price upfront. You make monthly payments; with a loan you own the machine when it's paid off. Chippers and grinders are essential for tree care, land clearing, and mulch production-but they're a big investment. We connect you with lenders that finance wood chippers and grinders so you can get the equipment and get to work.

What Chippers and Grinders Can Be Financed?

New equipment. Buying from a dealer? Lenders routinely finance new wood chippers, stump grinders, and tub grinders. Get your quote, we match you with a lender, and you secure financing before you buy.

Used equipment. Used wood chipper and grinder financing is common. Lenders look at age, hours, condition, and value. You can finance used machines and spread payments over several years.

Private seller. Some of our lenders fund private-party purchases with a bill of sale and equipment details.

Dealer purchase. Dealer deals are the most common. We add options by matching you with our network so you can compare terms.

Terms and Requirements

Loan amounts. Chipper and grinder loans can range from roughly $15,000 to $300,000+ depending on size and type. We match you with lenders that finance in your range.

Credit flexibility. We work with lenders that consider a range of credit. We also work with lenders that offer grinder financing for fair or lower credit.

Time in business. Some lenders want to see a year in business; others work with newer operations. We match you based on your situation.

Down payment. Down payments of 10-20% are typical. More down can help with approval or rate. We connect you with lenders that fit what you can put down.
