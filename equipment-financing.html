Quick Answer: How Equipment Financing Works

Equipment financing is a loan or lease secured by the equipment you are buying. Because the lender can repossess and resell the asset, approval weighs the equipment's value and your cash flow, not just your credit, and decisions usually come faster than bank or SBA loans.

  • Amounts: $10K to $5M, new or used
  • Rates: about 7-22% APR, set mainly by credit tier (see the table)
  • Terms: 2 to 7 years, longer for stronger credit
  • Down payment: 0-20% for most borrowers; $0 down usually needs about 680+ credit
  • Next steps: use the payment calculator or apply once to get matched

How Equipment Financing Actually Works

You pick the equipment and get a quote or invoice from the seller. The lender pays the seller, and you repay the lender in fixed monthly payments. With a loan you own the equipment from day one; with a lease the lender owns it and you choose at the end whether to buy it, return it or renew.

Lenders price the deal on four things:

  • Your personal credit: the biggest single input. A 720 and a 620 get very different pricing on the same machine.
  • Time in business: 2+ years opens the best programs; 6-24 months still qualifies, at higher rates and shorter terms.
  • Revenue: most programs look for about $10,000+ a month in deposits.
  • The equipment: assets with deep resale markets (trucks, trailers, construction) price best; restaurant equipment and used or high-hour equipment add about 1-3 points.

What Credit Score Do You Need?

There is no universal minimum. In practice:

  • 600+ FICO opens most equipment lenders.
  • 550-599 usually needs a specialty asset-based lender.
  • Below 550 typically means 25%+ down or a cosigner.

Equipment with strong resale value (construction, trucking, manufacturing) loosens the credit bar by about 30-50 points compared with soft assets, and strong revenue or 2+ years in business offsets weaker credit further. See credit score requirements and equipment financing with bad credit.

Can You Get Equipment Financing With No Money Down?

Yes, with the right profile. 100% financing is commonly available to businesses with about 680+ credit and 2+ years in business buying new or late-model equipment from an established dealer. 650-679 sometimes qualifies with strong revenue.

  • Used equipment usually needs 10-20% down.
  • $0-down deals often price a point or two higher than the same deal with money down.
  • "No credit check" equipment financing is rare: most legitimate programs still review credit, often with a soft pull that does not affect your score.

See equipment financing with no money down and no credit check equipment financing.

Financing Used Equipment

Most equipment lenders finance used equipment, especially in liquid categories like construction, trucking, manufacturing and agriculture. What changes:

  • Age: typically under 10-15 years. Many lenders cap age plus loan term at about 15 years.
  • Rate: about 1-3 points higher than new.
  • Down payment: 10-20% for older assets.
  • Paperwork: photos, serial numbers and sometimes an inspection.

See can you finance used equipment and, for construction, used bulldozer financing.

Equipment Financing for Startups and New Businesses

Businesses under 2 years old can qualify, because the equipment secures the loan. Many programs accept 6+ months in business with 660-680+ FICO and steady revenue. Expect about 12-22% APR and 10-20% down, or a personal guarantor. Approvals are routine for construction, trucking, manufacturing, restaurant and medical equipment.

See equipment financing for new businesses and the startup equipment financing guide.

How Fast Is Equipment Financing?

  • Initial approval: typically 24-72 hours.
  • Full underwriting: 3-7 business days.
  • Funding: 5-10 business days in total. Deals under $150K can close in under a week with clean documentation.

The fastest files arrive complete: an equipment quote or invoice, recent bank statements and a signed application. See how fast equipment financing is approved.

Equipment Financing vs a Business Credit Card

Business credit cards run about 18-29% APR after any intro period and have no fixed payoff term; equipment financing runs about 7-22% APR depending on credit, with a fixed 2-7 year term. A card can win for small purchases you pay off quickly, or with a 0% intro APR you clear before it ends. For anything over about $10-15K that you will keep for more than a few months, equipment financing usually costs less. See equipment financing vs business credit card.

Equipment Financing FAQs

What credit score do I need for equipment financing?

There is no universal minimum. A 600+ FICO opens most equipment lenders, 550-599 usually needs a specialty asset-based lender, and below 550 typically means 25%+ down or a cosigner. See credit score requirements and equipment financing with bad credit.

Can I get equipment financing with no money down?

Often, if you have about 680+ credit and 2+ years in business and are buying new or late-model equipment from an established dealer; 650-679 sometimes qualifies with strong revenue. Used equipment usually needs 10-20% down, and $0-down deals often price a point or two higher. See equipment financing with no money down.

Is there equipment financing with no credit check?

Truly no-credit-check equipment financing is rare. Most legitimate programs still review credit, often with a soft pull that does not affect your score, and lean on the equipment's value, time in business and bank deposits. With weaker credit, expect higher rates (see the rate table above) and commonly 10-20% down. See no credit check equipment financing.

How fast can I get equipment financing?

Initial approvals typically come back in 24-72 hours, full underwriting takes 3-7 business days, and funding lands in 5-10 business days in total. Deals under $150K can close in under a week with clean documentation. See how fast equipment financing is approved.

Can I finance used equipment?

Yes. Most equipment lenders finance used equipment, especially construction, trucking, manufacturing and agriculture. Expect the equipment to be under about 10-15 years old, rates 1-3 points higher than new, and 10-20% down for older assets. Many lenders also cap age plus loan term at about 15 years. See can you finance used equipment.

Can a startup get equipment financing?

Yes. Many programs accept businesses with 6+ months in operation, 660-680+ FICO and steady revenue. Under 2 years in business, expect about 12-22% APR and 10-20% down or a personal guarantor. See equipment financing for new businesses.

Should I lease or buy with an equipment loan?

Take a loan when you plan to keep the equipment for its useful life; you own it from day one. Lease when you want to upgrade often or keep payments down: lease payments run roughly 10-20% lower, and at the end you can buy ($1 buyout or 10% purchase option), return, or renew. See equipment leasing vs loan.

How much equipment financing can I get?

Amounts typically run from $10,000 to $5,000,000, depending on the equipment, your revenue and credit, and the lender. Heavy machinery and manufacturing assets often support larger deals; smaller items like restaurant or office equipment may start around $10,000.
