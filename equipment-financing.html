Finance or Lease Your Business Equipment

Whether you need construction equipment, vehicles, manufacturing machinery, or office technology, Axiant Partners connects you with lenders that offer equipment loans and leasing. Flexible terms and competitive rates—without tying up all your cash.

Equipment Loans

Borrow to purchase equipment outright. You own the asset and build equity. Fixed payments; the equipment typically serves as collateral.

Equipment Leasing

Pay to use equipment over a set term. Lower upfront cost and the option to upgrade or buy at the end. Payments are often tax-deductible.
