What Is Equipment Financing?

Equipment financing allows businesses to acquire necessary machinery, vehicles, and specialized equipment through loans or leases rather than paying the full cost upfront. This method preserves working capital and often provides significant tax advantages.

  • Preserves working capital
  • Tax-deductible payments
  • Access to new technology
  • Flexible repayment terms
  • Improved cash flow management

Our solutions are designed to meet diverse business needs, offering both traditional equipment loans and various leasing options tailored to your specific operational and financial goals.

Types of Equipment Financing Solutions

We provide several financing options to ensure your business finds the best fit for its needs.

Equipment Loans (Term Financing)

Traditional loans where businesses own the equipment after repayment.

  • Ownership upon repayment
  • Fixed monthly payments
  • Tax depreciation benefits
  • Ideal for long-term assets

Equipment Leasing Options

Allows businesses to use equipment for a set period, typically with lower monthly payments and flexible end-of-term options.

Capital Lease (Finance Lease)

Functions like a loan for accounting purposes. Business owns equipment at lease end with depreciation benefits. Lower monthly payments than traditional loans.

Operating Lease (True Lease)

Off-balance sheet financing. No ownership option at lease end (or fair market value purchase). Payments treated as operating expenses for tax purposes. Ideal for frequently updated equipment.

Sale-Leaseback Financing

Sell existing equipment for cash and lease it back to free up working capital. Benefits: Immediate cash infusion, maintain use of critical assets, improve liquidity. Ideal for businesses needing quick capital without disrupting operations.

Equipment Financing Requirements

General requirements for most equipment financing programs:

  • Business Operating History: 2+ years in business
  • Credit Score: Minimum 600 FICO (higher for better rates)
  • Annual Revenue: $250,000+
  • Financial Stability: Demonstrated ability to repay the loan

Startup Programs: Available for businesses under 2 years old with strong personal credit and collateral.

Poor Credit Programs: Options for businesses with lower credit scores, potentially with higher down payments or interest rates.

Collateral: The equipment itself serves as collateral. Additional collateral may be required for riskier applications.

Benefits of Equipment Financing

Preserve Working Capital

Avoid large upfront expenditures, keeping your cash flow strong for daily operations and other investments.

Easier Approval Than SBA Financing

Often faster and less stringent than traditional bank loans or SBA programs, making it an accessible option.

Flexible Structuring

Tailored repayment schedules to match your business's cash flow cycles. Options for seasonal payments, deferred payments, skip payments, and balloon payments.

Potential Tax Advantages

Equipment loan interest and lease payments may be tax-deductible, reducing your taxable income. Consult your tax advisor for specific details.

What Determines Equipment Loan Rates?

  • Equipment Type: New vs. used, industry-specific (specialized vs. general use), resale value
  • Borrower's Financial Profile: Credit score, time in business, revenue, debt-to-income ratio
  • Loan Structuring: Term length, down payment, collateral

Lenders evaluate these factors to set rates. Understanding them helps you prepare a stronger application and compare offers effectively.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does approval take?

Often 24-48 hours for complete applications, sometimes same-day for urgent needs.

Can I finance used equipment?

Yes, we finance both new and used equipment from reputable dealers.

What credit score do I need?

Minimum 600, but higher scores (700+) unlock better rates and terms.

Is a down payment required?

Not always. Well-qualified businesses can often get 100% financing. Otherwise, down payments range from 5-20%.

Can startups qualify?

Yes, we have programs tailored for startups with strong personal credit and a clear business plan.
