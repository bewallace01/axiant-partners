Revenue-Based Financing for Growth Companies

Flexible Capital from $10,000 to $5,000,000+

Revenue-based financing provides growth capital repaid as a percentage of monthly revenue. It is designed for companies with consistent sales that want flexible repayment aligned with performance.

What Is Revenue-Based Financing?

Revenue-based financing (RBF) is a funding structure where repayment flexes with top-line revenue performance instead of fixed installment schedules.

  • Repayment is tied to monthly revenue volume
  • Payment burden can decrease during slower months
  • No fixed long-term amortization schedule in most structures
  • Designed for businesses with recurring or predictable sales

How Revenue-Based Financing Works

Typical process:

  • Underwriting review of business deposits and revenue consistency
  • Funding amount offered based on cash flow profile
  • Repayment percentage set against monthly revenue
  • Faster payoff possible when performance is strong

Because repayment adjusts with performance, this structure can preserve flexibility in variable revenue cycles.

Typical Loan Amounts & Terms

Revenue-based financing generally ranges from $10,000 to $5,000,000+ depending on monthly deposits and business profile.

  • Short to medium duration structures are common
  • Funding size scales with revenue consistency
  • Term outcomes vary with repayment velocity

Who Uses Revenue-Based Financing?

Common users include growth-oriented companies with regular sales activity:

  • SaaS and subscription businesses
  • E-commerce companies
  • Service businesses with recurring invoices
  • Marketing and advertising firms
  • Hospitality and consumer-facing brands

Revenue-Based Financing vs Term Loan

Feature Revenue-Based Financing Term Loan
Repayment Structure Variable with revenue Fixed monthly payments
Cash-Flow Flexibility Higher Lower
Use Case Growth initiatives with variable cycles Defined long-term capital projects

Revenue-Based Financing vs Line of Credit

Feature Revenue-Based Financing Line of Credit
Funding Model Lump-sum advance Revolving access
Repayment Revenue-based Interest and minimum payments
Best For Scaling campaigns and growth execution Recurring short-term liquidity needs

What Do Lenders Look For?

  • Monthly revenue consistency
  • Bank deposit history
  • Industry stability
  • Time in business
  • Credit profile (business and/or owner)
  • Existing debt and repayment capacity

Benefits of Revenue-Based Financing

  • Repayment adjusts with revenue cycles
  • No fixed monthly installment in many structures
  • Useful for growth initiatives that generate variable returns
  • Can move faster than traditional long-term financing

Risks & Considerations

  • Effective cost can exceed traditional bank debt
  • Frequent payment drafts may affect operating cash flow
  • Not ideal for long-duration capital projects
  • Requires disciplined cash-flow management

When Revenue-Based Financing Makes Sense

  • You have consistent sales but variable monthly performance
  • You need growth capital quickly for expansion opportunities
  • You prefer flexible repayment versus fixed installments
  • You have a clear use case with measurable return potential

Minimum Funding Amount

Most programs start around $10,000 and scale based on monthly deposits and qualification strength.
