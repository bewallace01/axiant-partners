Business Line of Credit for All U.S. Companies

Flexible Working Capital from $10,000 to $2,000,000+

A business line of credit offers flexible working capital for All, revenue-generating companies nationwide. Draw funds as needed and pay interest only on the amount used. A revolving facility helps manage cash flow, seasonal gaps, and short-term growth without committing to a lump-sum loan.

What Is a Business Line of Credit?

A business line of credit is a revolving credit facility that gives your company access to a pre-approved credit limit. You draw funds as needed and repay them over time. It functions similarly to a credit card-but with significantly higher limits and typically lower interest rates.

  • Access to funds on demand, up to your credit limit
  • Pay interest only on the amount you draw
  • Revolving: as you repay, credit becomes available again
  • Flexible use for payroll, inventory, receivables, and more

How a Commercial Line of Credit Works

01

Approval & Credit Limit

Your company is approved for a maximum revolving credit limit based on annual revenue, years in business, credit profile, and industry risk.

02

Draw Funds as Needed

Capital can be used for payroll management, inventory purchases, invoice payments, managing receivables gaps, seasonal revenue fluctuations, short-term growth initiatives, and refinancing. Repay funds to remain within your credit limit.

03

Repay & Reuse

As you repay the outstanding balance, credit becomes available again. Use the financing as often as needed during the draw period.

Credit Limits, Draw Periods & Structure

Typically millions more available depending on your profile.

  • Credit limits from $10,000 to $2,000,000+
  • Revolving draw periods
  • Interest-only minimum payments (depending on advances)
  • Reserved options for secured borrowers

Structure depends on company strength, credit profile, and whether the facility is secured or unsecured.

Secured vs. Unsecured Business Line of Credit

There are two primary structures available.

Unsecured Business Line of Credit

  • No specific collateral required
  • Based primarily on revenue and credit strength
  • Fewer restrictions
  • Typically higher rates and fees

Common for All businesses with strong revenues and consistent cash flow.

Secured Business Line of Credit

  • Backed by business assets (receivables, inventory, etc.)
  • May require blanket UCC-1 filing
  • Lower rates compared to unsecured options
  • More flexible financing and structure options

Ideal for larger or asset-heavy companies seeking higher credit capacity.

Who Qualifies for a Business Line of Credit?

Minimum requirements for most structured programs include:

  • 2+ years in business
  • Consistent revenue history
  • 680+ FICO scores
  • Consistent cash flow
  • No recent bankruptcies

Some programs have lower guidelines for businesses that meet certain criteria.

Common Uses for a Business Line of Credit

Business lines of credit are typically used for short-term working capital needs:

  • Managing cash flow
  • Covering payroll gaps
  • Inventory purchasing
  • Managing receivables gaps
  • Short-term equipment purchases
  • Strategic expansion

Often used for short-term purchases and operational needs rather than long-term fixed investments.

Business Line of Credit vs. Term Loan

In short, the key difference is easy to see:

  • Term Loan: Lump sum upfront, fixed repayment schedule, pay interest on full amount from day one.
  • Line of Credit: Revolving access, draw and repay as needed, pay interest only on amounts drawn.

Lines of credit suit fluctuating or seasonal needs; term loans suit one-time purchases or projects.

How Lenders Underwrite a Commercial Line of Credit

Lenders evaluate several factors when assessing applications:

  • Revenue stability: Consistent, predictable income
  • Debt Service Coverage: Ability to service existing and new debt
  • Credit History: Personal and business credit
  • Industry Risk: Sector stability and outlook

What Determines Rates & Terms?

Several factors influence the rates and terms you receive:

  • Credit strength
  • Years in business
  • Amount leveraged
  • Debt load
  • Secured vs. unsecured issuance
  • Industry risk

Lenders evaluate your financial profile holistically when setting pricing.

Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can I access funds?

Once approved and the facility is in place, funds are typically available within 24-48 hours of a draw request, depending on the lender.

How much can I qualify for?

Credit limits typically range from $10,000 to $2,000,000+ based on revenue, credit, and structure (secured vs. unsecured).

Is collateral required?

Unsecured lines require no specific collateral; approval is based on revenue and credit. Secured lines use business assets and may offer lower rates.

Can startups qualify?

Most programs require 2+ years in business. Startups may find alternatives such as term loans or other products designed for newer businesses.
