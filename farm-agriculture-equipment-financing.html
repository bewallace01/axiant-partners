What Is Farm & Agriculture Equipment Financing?

Farm and agriculture equipment financing helps you buy or lease tractors, combines, hay equipment, sprayers, and other ag machinery. We connect you with lenders that understand seasonal cash flow and offer terms suited to farming operations.

Typical loan terms

Terms often run 3–7 years. Loan amounts depend on equipment type and value. Some lenders offer seasonal payment structures. Use our loan calculator to estimate payments.

Who qualifies

Farms, ranches, and ag businesses. Lenders consider revenue, time in business, credit, and the equipment. We match you with lenders that offer farm and agriculture equipment loans.
