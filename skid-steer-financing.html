What Is Skid Steer Financing?

Skid steer financing is a loan or lease that lets you get a skid steer loader without paying the full price upfront. You make monthly payments; with a loan you own the machine when it's paid off. Skid steers are versatile—landscaping, construction, agriculture, demolition. They're also a real investment. We connect you with lenders that finance skid steers so you can get the machine and put it to work.

What Skid Steers Can Be Financed?

New skid steers. Buying from a dealer? Lenders routinely finance new skid steer loaders. Get your quote, we match you with a lender, and you secure financing before you buy.

Used skid steers. Used skid steer financing is common. Lenders look at age, hours, condition, and value. You can finance used machines and spread payments over several years.

Private seller. Some of our lenders fund private-party purchases with a bill of sale and equipment details.

Dealer purchase. Dealer deals are the most common. We add options by matching you with our network so you can compare terms.

Terms & Requirements

Loan amounts. Skid steer loans often range from about $25,000 to $80,000+ depending on size and attachments. We match you with lenders that finance in your range.

Credit flexibility. We work with lenders that consider a range of credit. We also work with lenders that offer skid steer financing for fair or lower credit.

Time in business. Some lenders want to see a year in business; others work with newer operations. We match you based on your situation.

Down payment. Down payments of 10–20% are typical. More down can help with approval or rate. We connect you with lenders that fit what you can put down.
