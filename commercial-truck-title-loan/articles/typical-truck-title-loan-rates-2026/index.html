Typical Truck Title Loan Rates and Terms (2026)

What a business-purpose loan against a truck you own commonly costs, how much it advances, how long it runs, and why it is nothing like a consumer car title loan

← Back to Commercial Truck Title Loans Articles

Updated October 26, 2026

Quick answer

A business-purpose truck title loan is priced like a secured equipment loan, not a consumer title loan: commonly about 7-22% APR depending on credit, an advance of roughly 50-80% of forced liquidation value, and 24-72 months to repay. Three Numbers Make Up the Offer

Every truck title loan offer comes down to three figures, and they are set by different things:

TermTypical rangeSet mainly by
Advance50-80% of forced liquidation valueThe truck
RateAbout 7-22% APRYour credit and the business
Term24-72 monthsThe truck's age and the amount

Typical ranges across lenders, not a quote. They are the same ranges the site publishes for equipment refinancing and equipment financing rates, because a business-purpose title loan is underwritten the same way: a titled asset with a resale market, and a business that has to carry the payment.

What Sets the Rate

Credit tier does most of the work. Ranges commonly seen across lenders for equipment-secured business credit:

Credit tierTypical APRTypical term
720+, 2+ yrs in business7-9%Up to 72 mo
680-71910-13%36-72 mo

580-63916-22%24-48 mo
Below 580Limited programs24-36 mo

Within a tier, an older or high-mileage truck commonly adds one to three points, because there is less life left in the collateral. Cash-out against a truck you own tends to sit at the higher end of a tier than financing a purchase, since the money is not buying a new asset. Fleets borrowing against several units usually do better than a single truck; see owner-operator vs fleet terms.

What Sets the Advance

The advance is a share of what the truck would bring at a quick sale, not what you paid or what a dealer is asking. What moves it:

  • Age and mileage - the biggest single factor on any truck.
  • Make, engine and spec - common tractors and straight trucks with a deep buyer pool advance higher than unusual builds.
  • Body and equipment - a dump body, reefer unit or wrecker is valued separately from the chassis; see the dump, box and tow truck guides.
  • Records - documented maintenance raises both the value and the share a lender will advance against it.
  • What is still owed - comes straight off the top.

The full method is in how much you can borrow against a semi.

What the Payments Look Like

Illustrative arithmetic, not a quote. A tractor with a forced liquidation value of $80,000, owned outright, at a 65% advance - $52,000 - over 48 months:

The spread between the best and worst tier on the same truck is about $233 a month. Test whichever figure you are offered against a slow month, not an average one.

Why It Is Nothing Like a Consumer Car Title Loan

Searches for a truck title loan often land on consumer title lenders. The products share a name and little else. The CFPB's 2016 study of single-payment vehicle title loans found APRs around 300% and that about one in five loan sequences ended with the vehicle repossessed.

Consumer title loanCommercial truck title loan
BorrowerAn individualA business or owner-operator
Typical costAPR around 300% (CFPB)About 7-22% APR
RepaymentOften due in about 30 daysAmortized over 24-72 months
UnderwritingThe vehicleThe truck and the business

If an offer for a work truck is priced or structured like the left column, it is the wrong product. Business-purpose credit is also exempt from federal Truth in Lending disclosures, so ask every lender for the APR and the total of payments in writing. A short-term advance against receivables is a different comparison again: see title loan vs MCA.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the interest rate on a commercial truck title loan?

Business-purpose truck title loans are priced like secured equipment loans, commonly about 7-22% APR depending on credit, with older or high-mileage trucks toward the higher end. These are typical ranges across lenders, not a quote.

How much will a lender advance against my truck?

Commonly about 50-80% of the truck's forced liquidation value - what it would bring at a quick sale - less anything still owed on it.

How long are truck title loan terms?

Commonly 24 to 72 months with fixed monthly payments, shorter for older trucks and smaller amounts.

Is a truck title loan the same as a car title loan?

No. A consumer car title loan is a short-term personal loan; the CFPB found APRs around 300%. A commercial truck title loan is business credit secured by a working truck, priced like an equipment loan and repaid over months.

Why doesn't my offer show an APR?

Federal Truth in Lending disclosures do not apply to business-purpose credit. Ask for the APR and the total of payments before you sign.

Sources & Further Reading
