SBA 504 Loan Fees: What Each Fee Costs and Who Pays It

The upfront, ongoing and bank-side fees on a 504 loan, with a worked example, a sample amortization schedule and who has them waived for fiscal 2027

← Back to SBA Loans

Updated November 07, 2026

Quick answer

On the debenture, expect about 2.65% in upfront percentage fees: up to 1.5% CDC processing, 0.5% SBA guaranty, 0.4% underwriter and 0.25% funding, usually financed into the loan. Ongoing, you pay at least a 0.625% servicing fee and a 0.203% SBA annual fee for fiscal 2027. Manufacturers, food-supply businesses and rural businesses have the two SBA fees waived. Every 504 Fee in One Table

A 504 project has three pieces: a bank first mortgage, a Certified Development Company (CDC) loan funded by an SBA-guaranteed debenture, and your own injection. The fees below come from the table in SOP 50 10 8.1 and SBA's FY 2027 fee notice, effective for loans approved October 1, 2026 through September 30, 2027.

FeeAmountWhen and to whom
CDC processingUp to 1.5% of the net debentureOne time, to the CDC
CDC closingA reasonable fee; up to $10,000 may be financedOne time, to the CDC
SBA guaranty0.50% of the net debenture for FY 2027One time, to SBA
Underwriter0.40% on 20- and 25-year debentures; 0.375% on 10-yearOne time, to the underwriter
Funding0.25% of the net debentureOne time, for trustee and agent costs
Participation0.50% of the senior mortgageOne time; the bank, CDC or borrower may pay
CDC servicing0.625% a year minimumMonthly, on the unpaid balance
SBA annual0.203% for FY 2027Yearly, on the unpaid balance
Central servicing agentSet by contract with SBAInitiation and ongoing

The servicing fee can't normally go above 1% a year, or 1.5% in rural areas, without SBA's prior approval, and 2% is the ceiling. Two other fees apply only if something happens: a late fee of 5% of the late payment or $100, whichever is greater, and an assumption fee of up to 1% of the balance. A CDC can also ask for a deposit at application of $2,500 or 1% of the net debenture, whichever is less, and it can be credited toward the processing fee.

Who Has the SBA Fees Waived for Fiscal 2027

The FY 2027 notice waives both the upfront guaranty fee and the annual fee for three groups, including on 504 debt refinance loans:

  • Manufacturers in NAICS sectors 31-33.
  • Food supply chain businesses in the NAICS codes the notice lists, which include farming, fishing, grocery wholesale and refrigerated warehousing. Some trucking codes count only for refrigerated, farm, grain or livestock hauling.
  • Businesses located in a rural area.

Check your NAICS code and location with the CDC early, because the waiver changes the numbers in your quote. It doesn't touch the CDC, underwriter or funding fees. Everyone else pays 0.50% upfront and 0.203% a year. On the 504 Debt Refinance without Expansion program the annual fee is 0.204%. These amounts reset each fiscal year, and your loan keeps the rate for the year it was approved.

Worked Example: A $1,000,000 Building

Illustrative, not a quote. Assume a standard 50/40/10 split on a $1,000,000 owner-occupied purchase, no fee waiver, and an assumed $2,500 CDC closing fee.

ItemBasisAbout
Bank first mortgage50% of project$500,000
Net debenture40% of project$400,000
Your injection10% of project$100,000
CDC processing1.5% of net debenture$6,000
Funding fee0.25% of net debenture$1,000
CDC closingAssumed$2,500
SBA guaranty0.50% of net debenture$2,000
SubtotalNet plus the four fees above$411,500
Gross debentureSubtotal divided by 0.996, rounded up to the next even thousand$414,000
Underwriter0.40% of gross debenture$1,656
Balance to youLeft over from rounding up$844
Participation fee0.50% of bank loan$2,500

So about $13,000 of fees sits in the debenture, which is why you rarely write a check for them. The administrative costs aren't part of project cost: they're added to the net debenture to reach the gross debenture, so they don't raise your 10%. The steps follow the pricing model in the SOP. Your out-of-pocket costs are different: settlement agent fees, delivery, and required certifications, plus the appraisal, environmental report and title work any commercial purchase needs.

What an SBA Debenture Is

A debenture is the bond behind the CDC's loan. SBA guarantees it, and it's sold to investors, which is what lets the CDC offer a long fixed rate. The net debenture is the amount that reaches your project. The gross debenture adds the financed fees, and it's what you repay. Quotes can blur the two, so ask which one a percentage applies to. For the rate side, including how the sale sets your number, see our 504 rates guide; for the program itself, see what an SBA 504 loan is.

How 504 Fees Compare With a 7(a)

The two programs charge in different places, so compare total cost rather than one line. On a 504, the upfront percentages apply mainly to the debenture, about 40% of the project, and the bank sets its own fees on the first mortgage. A 7(a) charges its guaranty fee on the guaranteed share of one larger loan, and it's set by its own annual notice. The right structure usually depends on whether you need more than real estate and equipment. See SBA 7(a) vs 504, and if your project needs no SBA structure at all, compare commercial real estate loans.

Questions to Ask Before You Sign

  1. What's the CDC processing fee? 1.5% is a cap on the net debenture, so ask for the actual number.
  2. What will the closing fee be? Only $10,000 can be financed from the debenture.
  3. What's the servicing fee? The floor is 0.625%; find out what yours is and whether it needs SBA approval.
  4. Do I qualify for the FY 2027 waiver? Give the CDC your NAICS code and address.
  5. Who pays the 0.50% participation fee? It may fall on the bank, the CDC or you.
  6. What does the bank charge on its half? Those fees vary and aren't set by SBA.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much are SBA 504 loan fees?

Upfront percentages on the debenture total about 2.65%: up to 1.5% CDC processing, 0.5% SBA guaranty, 0.4% underwriter on 20- and 25-year debentures, and 0.25% funding. A CDC closing fee and a 0.5% participation fee on the senior mortgage also apply. Ongoing, expect at least 0.625% servicing and a 0.203% SBA annual fee for loans approved in fiscal 2027.

Are SBA 504 fees financed into the loan?

Most are. SBA's rules add administrative costs to the net debenture to reach the gross debenture, so they're repaid over the term, and up to $10,000 of the CDC closing fee can be financed. Settlement, delivery, certification and third-party report costs are often out of pocket.

Who doesn't pay SBA 504 guaranty fees in 2027?

For loans approved October 1, 2026 through September 30, 2027, SBA waives the upfront and annual fees for manufacturers in NAICS 31-33, listed food supply chain businesses and businesses in a rural area.

What is an SBA debenture?

It's the SBA-guaranteed bond that funds the CDC's share of a 504 project, typically about 40%. It's sold to investors and carries a rate fixed for the full 10, 20 or 25 years.

Does a 504 loan have an amortization schedule?

Yes. The debenture amortizes fully with a fixed payment over its term. As an illustration, $414,000 at 6.5% over 25 years is about $2,795 a month, with about $321,000 left after 10 years. The bank loan has its own schedule and may reset.

What are the late fees on a 504 loan?

Payments received after the 15th of the month may be charged 5% of the late payment or $100, whichever is greater.
