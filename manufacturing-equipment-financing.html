What Is Manufacturing Equipment Financing?

Manufacturing equipment financing helps you buy or lease CNC machines, lathes, presses, conveyors, and other production and manufacturing equipment. We connect you with lenders that offer manufacturing equipment finance so you can scale production without large upfront capital.

Typical terms

Terms often range from 3-7 years. Loan amounts can be substantial for heavy machinery. Rates and down payments depend on equipment, credit, and lender. Use our loan calculator to estimate payments.

Who qualifies

Manufacturers, fabricators, and production facilities. Lenders consider revenue, time in business, and the equipment. We match you with lenders that offer manufacturing equipment financing.
