What Credit Score Is Needed for a Commercial Real Estate Loan?

Credit tiers, lender expectations, and what drives CRE approval

← Back to Commercial Real Estate Loans Blog

February 26, 2026 · Axiant Partners

If you are purchasing or refinancing an owner-occupied commercial property, one of the first questions is credit score requirements. Most lenders evaluate clear tier ranges, but credit is only one part of commercial real estate underwriting.

Typical Credit Score Requirements by Loan Type

Loan Type Typical Preferred Credit Score
SBA 504 680-700+
SBA 7(a) 660-680+
Conventional Commercial Mortgage 680-720+
Asset-Based / Alternative CRE 600-650+ (with compensating factors)

Stronger credit profiles typically receive faster approvals, lower pricing, and better leverage options.

Credit Score Tiers Explained

720+ (Strong Tier)

  • Maximum leverage potential
  • Smoother underwriting
  • Stronger terms across lender channels

680-719 (Solid Tier)

  • Good conventional access
  • Strong SBA eligibility
  • Competitive pricing for owner-occupied loans

650-679 (Moderate Tier)

  • SBA programs often remain available
  • Conventional options may tighten
  • Slightly higher pricing likely

600-649 (Challenging Tier)

  • SBA may still be possible with strong compensating factors
  • Higher equity requirements may apply
  • Alternative structures are often considered

Why Credit Score Matters in CRE

Commercial real estate loans are often large, long-term obligations. Lenders use credit profile to evaluate repayment history, financial discipline, and risk behavior over time.

What Matters More Than Credit Score

CRE underwriting is cash-flow driven. Most lenders prioritize:

  • Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR)
  • Business revenue stability
  • Property fundamentals and occupancy quality
  • Time in business
  • Down payment and liquidity reserves

Personal vs Business Credit

Most owner-occupied CRE transactions evaluate both personal and business credit. Maintaining strong payment history in both profiles helps reduce underwriting friction.

How Credit Impacts Down Payment

  • Higher credit often supports lower equity requirements
  • Moderate credit may increase required reserves
  • Weaker profiles usually require additional safeguards

How to Improve Approval Odds

  • Reduce revolving utilization
  • Resolve outstanding collections or reporting errors
  • Improve monthly operating consistency
  • Build stronger liquidity reserves
  • Strengthen DSCR with clear financial reporting

Minimum Loan Amounts

Commercial real estate loan programs generally start around $10,000 and scale based on property and qualification.

Final Thoughts

Most lenders prefer credit scores in the 650-720+ range, depending on structure and risk profile. For many borrowers, cash flow and property fundamentals carry as much weight as score.
