Equipment Leasing vs Equipment Loan: Which Is Better?

Ownership, cash flow, tax, and flexibility—when to choose a loan vs lease for your equipment

← Back to Blog

February 21, 2025 · Axiant Partners

Business owners often ask: equipment leasing vs equipment loan—which is better? The answer depends on your goals: owning the asset, preserving cash flow, tax treatment, and how often you upgrade.

Choose an equipment loan when

You want to own the equipment and plan to use it for many years. Loans build equity; once you pay off the loan, you own the asset. Interest and depreciation can reduce taxable income. Equipment loans work well for construction equipment, trucks, and machinery you’ll keep long term.

Choose equipment leasing when

You want lower upfront cost, flexibility to upgrade, or to preserve capital. Leases often require little or no down payment and may offer lower monthly payments. At the end you can return, upgrade, or buy. Lease payments are often fully deductible as an operating expense (depending on structure). Leasing can be better for technology or equipment that becomes outdated quickly.

Quick comparison

Loan: You own when paid off; build equity; interest + depreciation for tax. Lease: Use, don’t own (unless you buy at end); lower upfront; payments often deductible; easy to upgrade. Your accountant can help with tax implications.
