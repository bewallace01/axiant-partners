What Credit Score Is Needed for a Business Term Loan?

Approval tiers, lender expectations, and qualification factors

← Back to Business Term Loans Blog

February 26, 2026 · Axiant Partners

If you are applying for a lump-sum business term loan, one of the first questions is credit score requirements. Lenders usually follow clear credit tiers, but approval also depends on cash flow, debt load, and whether the facility is secured.

Typical Credit Score Requirements by Structure

Loan Type Typical Preferred Credit Score
Unsecured Business Term Loan 680-700+
Secured Business Term Loan 660-690+
SBA 7(a) Term Loan 650-680+
Bank Conventional Term Loan 680-720+

Stronger credit typically results in lower rates, higher approval odds, larger loan amounts, and longer repayment terms.

Credit Score Tiers Explained

720+ (Strong Tier)

  • Highest approval probability
  • Best pricing flexibility
  • Most lender options

680-719 (Solid Tier)

  • Strong approval profile
  • Good conventional access
  • Competitive pricing

650-679 (Moderate Tier)

  • SBA still possible
  • Secured structures more realistic
  • Slightly higher pricing

600-649 (Challenging Tier)

  • Unsecured options limited
  • Strong revenue required
  • Higher rates likely

Why Credit Score Matters for Term Loans

Because business term loans are usually fully disbursed upfront and repaid over fixed terms, lenders place significant weight on repayment behavior and risk indicators:

  • Payment history
  • Revolving utilization
  • Public records
  • Bankruptcies
  • Overall debt behavior

What Matters Beyond Credit

Credit is important, but it is not the only factor. Lenders prioritize:

  • Revenue stability
  • Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR)
  • Time in business (2+ years preferred)
  • Existing debt load
  • Industry stability

Strong cash flow can offset moderate credit profiles.

Minimum Loan Amount

Business term loans usually start at $10,000 and scale with qualification.

Final Thoughts

Most business term loan programs prefer scores in the 680-720+ range, depending on structure. However, approval depends on the full financial profile, not just score. If your business generates consistent revenue and needs structured lump-sum capital, reviewing available business term loan options can help determine fit by credit tier.
