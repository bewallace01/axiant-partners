What Do Lenders Look for in a Commercial Bridge Loan?

Property, LTV, exit strategy, liquidity, and sponsor profile

← Back to Commercial Bridge Loans Blog

February 26, 2026 - Axiant Partners

Bridge loan underwriting differs from traditional long-term mortgage underwriting. The strongest emphasis is placed on collateral quality and clarity of repayment exit.

Property Value (Primary Factor)

Lenders evaluate:

  • Asset quality and location
  • Current condition and marketability
  • Appraised value and supportable value assumptions

Loan-to-Value (LTV)

Most bridge structures are sized to conservative LTV ranges relative to risk profile. Lower LTV typically improves approval odds and pricing.

Exit Strategy (Critical Factor)

Lenders expect a defined and realistic repayment path:

  • Refinance into conventional or SBA debt
  • Sale of stabilized property
  • Portfolio recapitalization or liquidity event

Without a credible exit, approval is difficult.

Borrower Liquidity

  • Available reserves
  • Ability to carry interest payments
  • Financial cushion for delays or extensions

Credit Profile

Credit is reviewed but usually secondary to collateral and exit execution quality.

  • Payment history
  • Recent delinquencies or public record issues
  • Overall risk behavior

Property Type

Standard asset classes often underwrite more smoothly:

  • Office
  • Industrial
  • Retail
  • Mixed-use (where appropriate)

Special-use properties may require lower leverage and additional conditions.

Experience

Lenders often prioritize sponsors with execution history in acquisitions, repositioning, and refinance outcomes.

What Matters Most?

In bridge underwriting, priorities usually rank as:

  • Property quality
  • LTV discipline
  • Exit strategy certainty
  • Liquidity
  • Credit profile

Risk Considerations

  • Short maturities and potential extension fees
  • Higher carrying cost than permanent debt
  • Refinance risk if assumptions do not materialize

Minimum Loan Amount

Commercial bridge loans usually start around $10,000 and scale based on property value and sponsor profile.

Final Thoughts

Commercial bridge lenders prioritize asset strength and exit certainty over long-term amortization factors. If your transaction requires speed and flexibility, review available commercial bridge programs and align your submission package to bridge-specific underwriting standards.
