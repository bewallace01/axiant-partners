How Fast Can You Close a Commercial Bridge Loan?

Typical timelines from term sheet to funding

← Back to Commercial Bridge Loans Blog

February 26, 2026 - Axiant Partners

Speed is one of the main reasons sponsors use bridge financing. In many cases, bridge loans close materially faster than traditional bank or SBA structures.

Typical Bridge Loan Closing Timeline

Stage Typical Timeline
Initial Review 24-72 hours
Term Sheet Issued 1-3 business days
Appraisal & Due Diligence 7-14 days
Final Approval 3-7 business days
Closing & Funding 7-21 business days total

Why Bridge Loans Close Faster

  • Asset-driven underwriting approach
  • Less committee layering than many traditional lenders
  • Strong focus on collateral, LTV, and exit strategy
  • Less reliance on full SBA-style documentation stacks

What Can Slow Down a Bridge Loan?

  • Appraisal or environmental report delays
  • Title issues
  • Incomplete borrower documentation
  • Unclear exit strategy

The most common delay source is missing borrower documentation.

Fastest Realistic Scenario

Under ideal conditions with clean title, straightforward collateral, and clear exit path, some bridge loans can close in roughly 7-14 days.

Bridge Loan vs SBA Timeline

Loan Type Typical Closing Time
Bridge Loan 7-21 days
Conventional CRE 30-60 days
SBA 7(a) 45-75 days
SBA 504 60-90 days

Minimum Loan Amount

Commercial bridge loans usually start around $10,000 and scale with collateral and profile strength.

Final Thoughts

Bridge loans are built for speed. Most close within 7-21 business days when documentation is ready and the exit strategy is clear. If you need rapid execution, compare current commercial bridge loan options and prepare your underwriting package upfront.
