What Do Lenders Look for in a Business Term Loan?

Revenue, DSCR, credit profile, and underwriting factors that drive approval

← Back to Business Term Loans Blog

February 26, 2026 - Axiant Partners

If your business is applying for a structured term loan, lenders evaluate more than just credit score. Underwriting focuses on repayment strength, consistency of cash flow, and overall risk profile.

Revenue Stability (Primary Factor)

Lenders review:

  • Monthly and annual revenue trends
  • Revenue consistency across seasons
  • Industry concentration risk
  • Business model durability

Most programs prefer stable revenue history over 12-24 months.

Cash Flow & Debt Service Coverage (DSCR)

DSCR measures whether business cash flow can cover required loan payments.

  • 1.20x+ is commonly preferred
  • Higher DSCR improves terms
  • Weak DSCR can reduce approval amounts

Credit Profile

Lenders evaluate both personal and business credit behavior.

  • Payment history
  • Revolving utilization
  • Derogatory events
  • Recent inquiries
  • Credit depth

600-700+ is common for many term structures, with higher scores producing stronger offers.

Existing Debt Obligations

Current liabilities affect approval and pricing:

  • Debt-to-income or debt-to-revenue metrics
  • Current payment burden
  • Refinance exposure
  • Short-term debt pressure

Time in Business

Most lenders prefer operating history:

  • 2+ years in business for standard programs
  • Longer history improves lender confidence
  • Startups may have limited options for term facilities

Industry Risk Profile

Lenders score risk based on sector behavior, volatility, and market outlook.

  • Stable industries usually receive better terms
  • Volatile sectors may require stronger collateral
  • Cyclicality impacts pricing and structure

Collateral (for Secured Loans)

Secured term loans may evaluate:

  • Equipment
  • Receivables
  • Inventory
  • Real estate or other business assets

Collateral can improve pricing and increase borrowing capacity.

Use of Funds

Lenders assess whether loan purpose matches term-loan structure:

  • Expansion
  • Refinancing
  • Acquisition
  • Large project funding
  • Asset-heavy growth initiatives

Banking Activity

Underwriters often review operating account behavior:

  • Deposit consistency
  • Overdraft frequency
  • Liquidity management discipline

Minimum Loan Amount

Business term loans usually start at $10,000 and scale with qualification, financial strength, and project scope.

What Matters Most?

In most files, lenders prioritize:

  • Revenue and consistency
  • DSCR and repayment capacity
  • Credit profile
  • Debt obligations
  • Business stability

How to Improve Approval Odds

  • Strengthen financial reporting
  • Reduce revolving debt
  • Increase operating account consistency
  • Improve credit discipline
  • Clarify use-of-funds documentation

Final Thoughts

Lenders evaluate business term loans through a complete risk framework, not personal score alone. If your company has stable revenue and needs structured lump-sum financing, reviewing available business term loan options can help identify programs aligned with your financial profile.
