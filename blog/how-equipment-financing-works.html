How Equipment Financing Works: A Step-by-Step Guide

From application to funding-what to expect when you finance construction equipment, trucks, or business machinery

← Back to Blog

February 21, 2025 - Axiant Partners

Equipment financing lets you acquire machinery, vehicles, or technology without paying the full cost upfront. Here's how it typically works from start to finish.

Step 1: Decide what you need

Identify the equipment (new or used), the approximate cost, and whether you prefer to own (loan) or use (lease). This shapes which lenders and products fit. See Equipment Financing vs. Lease and our equipment financing page.

Step 2: Apply or get matched

You can apply directly to a lender or use a match service like Axiant Partners. With us, you submit your information once-loan type, amount, business details, credit range-and we connect you with lenders that offer equipment financing for your profile.

Step 3: Get a decision and terms

Lenders review your application and may ask for documents (tax returns, bank statements, equipment quote). Approval timelines vary; some decisions come in days. You'll see rate, term, and payment. Use our loan calculator to compare.

Step 4: Accept and fund

Once you accept an offer, the lender finalizes the agreement. For equipment loans, funds may go to you or the vendor; for leases, the lessor typically pays the vendor. You take possession of the equipment and make payments according to the contract.

Step 5: Repay and maintain

Make payments on time. With a loan, you own the equipment when the loan is paid off. With a lease, you return, upgrade, or buy at the end of the term.
