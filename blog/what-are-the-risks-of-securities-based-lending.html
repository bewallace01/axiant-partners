What Are the Risks of Securities-Based Lending?

Market volatility, collateral pressure, and leverage risks to understand before borrowing

← Back to Securities-Based Lending Blog

February 26, 2026 - Axiant Partners

Securities-based lending can be a powerful liquidity tool, but collateralized borrowing introduces risk when markets move quickly or leverage is set too aggressively.

What Are the Risks of Securities-Based Lending?

Because your investment portfolio secures the facility, market movements can directly impact borrowing capacity and repayment pressure.

1. Market Volatility Risk

The most significant risk is portfolio value fluctuation. If markets decline:

  • Collateral value drops
  • Loan-to-value ratio increases
  • Available credit can shrink

2. Collateral Call Risk

If LTV exceeds approved thresholds, lenders may require:

  • Additional collateral
  • Partial loan paydown
  • Portfolio rebalancing

Failure to meet collateral requirements can increase liquidation risk.

3. Concentration Risk

Highly concentrated portfolios can reduce advance rates and increase exposure to single-asset shocks.

  • Single-stock concentration can increase volatility impact
  • Sector concentration can amplify drawdowns
  • Diversified portfolios generally qualify for better terms

4. Variable Interest Rate Risk

Many SBL facilities use variable-rate pricing. Rising rates can increase carrying costs and pressure cash flow.

5. Over-Leverage Risk

Because SBL provides quick liquidity, borrowers may over-borrow relative to operating cash flow and risk tolerance.

6. Liquidity & Market Shock Risk

In severe market events, assets can move sharply while liquidity conditions tighten. This combination may accelerate collateral stress.

How to Reduce Risk

  • Borrow below the maximum approved LTV
  • Maintain a liquidity reserve for volatility periods
  • Diversify pledged holdings
  • Use proceeds for productive, time-sensitive opportunities

Who Should Use Securities-Based Lending Carefully?

  • Borrowers with concentrated portfolios
  • Those without a liquidity cushion
  • Borrowers using the facility for speculative purposes

Minimum Loan Amount

Securities-based lending facilities typically start at $10,000, with larger facilities available for stronger collateral profiles.

Final Thoughts

SBL can be an efficient way to access capital, but it requires disciplined risk management. The primary risk drivers are market volatility, collateral sensitivity, and leverage. Review current securities-based lending options and structure conservatively to protect downside.
