The Model & The Full Product Menu

How the Business Works

Most owners who need capital apply to a single bank, get denied, and assume they don't qualify anywhere. We fix that. We route each file to the right capital channel the first time, package it cleanly, and advocate for the borrower through underwriting. Because we're paid on funded deals, our incentive is fully aligned with getting money in the borrower's account.

What Makes Us Different

  • One application reaches many lenders
  • Not one rigid credit box — multiple channels
  • Revenue & cash flow often beat credit score
  • We close documentation gaps early
  • Same-day specialist follow-up

The Trade-off We Teach Borrowers

  • Speed & flexibility vs. cost
  • Fast money (MCA, revenue-based) costs more
  • Cheap money (SBA, bank) takes longer
  • More docs = better pricing
  • We help them pick the right trade-off

Baseline Most Short-Term Products Share

  • ~6+ months in business
  • ~$10,000+ monthly revenue
  • 550+ FICO (650–680+ for SBA/bank)
  • Revenue & cash flow often outweigh credit score
  • Rule of thumb: borrow 10–30% of annual revenue

Funding Speed — Be Honest

  • Short-term: decision in 24–48 hrs
  • …funds in 1–3 business days
  • SBA / bank: 30–90 days, lowest rates
  • "Same-day" exists but is the exception
  • Never over-promise speed

How Much Can They Borrow?

Network range is $10K–$5M+. A common short-term guideline is 10–30% of annual revenue; SBA 7(a) goes up to $5M. The exact amount depends on revenue, time in business, credit, and collateral — so frame it as a range and let the lender set the number.

How a Deal Funds & Our Lender Network

The Process, Step by Step

  1. Owner applies at axiantpartners.com/match — each application gets a unique reference number.
  2. We match — the team analyzes the profile and identifies the lenders most likely to approve.
  3. Same-day specialist call to confirm details and use of funds.
  4. Package & submit — we identify gaps early so the file is cleanly packaged.
  5. Underwriting → offers — the borrower compares offers from multiple lenders.
  6. Funding — money hits the account. This is when revenue is earned.

Documents That Make a File Move Fast

  • Business legal name, owner name, contact info
  • Requested amount + clear use of funds
  • Time in business + approximate annual revenue
  • Credit range (if known) & existing debt obligations
  • Last 3–6 months of business bank statements
  • Any time-sensitive context (equipment purchase, closing date, payroll gap, contract)
