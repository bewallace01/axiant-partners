Typical Business Term Loan Ranges

Business term loans typically range from $10,000 to $5,000,000+. Actual approval amounts vary significantly based on financial strength. Explore available business term loan options to understand programs that match your profile.

1. Revenue Is the Primary Driver

Most lenders use revenue-based formulas to calculate maximum loan size. Annual revenue, monthly consistency, and growth trends all factor in. Lenders typically want to see at least 12–24 months of operating history to assess stability. Common guidelines include:

  • 10–30% of annual revenue
  • 1–3 months of gross revenue
  • Cash flow multiple adjusted for risk

Example: A business generating $1,000,000 annually might qualify for $100,000–$300,000, depending on credit and DSCR strength. Revenue consistency is often more important than a single strong month.

2. Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR)

Lenders evaluate whether the business can afford the new loan payment. Most structured lenders prefer 1.20–1.30x DSCR or higher. High existing obligations can reduce loan size; strong free cash flow increases leverage. DSCR is calculated as net operating income divided by total debt service. A ratio of 1.25x means your cash flow exceeds required payments by 25%. Improving DSCR before applying–by paying down existing debt or increasing revenue–can expand your borrowing capacity. Use our loan calculator to model how different loan amounts affect your debt service.

3. Credit Tier Impact

Credit profile impacts both approval and leverage. See credit score requirements for a business term loan for details. General tiers:

  • 720+: Higher limits, strongest pricing
  • 680–719: Strong standard limits
  • 660–679: Moderate leverage
  • 600–649: Conservative limits

Stronger credit allows lenders to extend higher percentages of revenue.

4. Time in Business

Most structured term loan programs prefer businesses with 1–2+ years in operation. Longer history often qualifies for larger loans, longer repayment terms, and more competitive rates. Newer businesses may face conservative limits unless structured under SBA programs.

5. Existing Debt Load

Lenders analyze current loan balances, monthly payment obligations, UCC filings, and overall leverage ratio. High leverage reduces borrowing capacity; a clean debt profile improves maximum loan size. See what lenders look for in a business term loan for a fuller underwriting checklist.

6. Secured vs. Unsecured Structure

Secured term loans (with collateral) typically offer: higher loan amounts, lower interest rates, and longer amortization. Unsecured term loans rely on revenue strength, credit quality, and cash flow stability. Collateral can significantly expand available capital. When you pledge equipment, receivables, real estate, or other assets, lenders can extend more credit because they have a secondary recovery path. Use of Funds Impact

How you plan to use the capital can influence approval and loan size. Expansion, equipment purchases, working capital, and debt refinancing are commonly accepted. Lenders often prefer clear, documented purposes. Some programs have restrictions on certain uses. Providing a detailed use-of-funds explanation can strengthen your application and support larger requests. See what lenders look for in a business term loan for documentation requirements.

Minimum Loan Amount

Business term loans typically start at $10,000 minimum. Minimum limits scale with financial strength and loan structure. Smaller requests may be better suited to working capital loans or lines of credit depending on your needs. Use our loan calculator to model payment scenarios for different amounts.

How to Increase Your Approval Amount

  • Increase revenue consistency
  • Reduce outstanding debt
  • Improve credit score
  • Strengthen DSCR
  • Maintain organized financial statements
  • Consider secured structures when appropriate

Preparation directly impacts leverage. Organize financial statements, tax returns, and bank statements before applying. Clear use-of-funds documentation and a debt schedule help lenders evaluate your request efficiently. Lenders reward businesses that present a complete, coherent picture of their financial position. If you are planning to apply in the future, focus on building revenue consistency, reducing leverage, and maintaining strong credit. Small improvements across multiple factors can significantly increase your borrowing capacity. For timeline expectations, see how fast you can get a business term loan.

When Larger Loans Are Possible

Larger multi-million dollar term loans are typically structured for established companies, strong balance sheets, significant revenue history, asset-backed structures, and SBA-backed programs. Underwriting becomes more detailed as loan size increases. See SBA loan options for structured long-term financing.

Pre-Approval and Soft Inquiry Options

Some lenders offer pre-qualification or soft credit checks that do not affect your score. These tools help you estimate potential loan amounts before formally applying. Use them to compare programs and set expectations without triggering multiple hard inquiries. A financing advisor can help interpret pre-qualification results and match you with lenders whose guidelines align with your profile.

Industry and Lender Variations

Approval amounts can vary by industry. Lenders may have sector preferences, risk tiers, or concentration limits. Some industries with stable cash flow–such as healthcare, professional services, or certain manufacturing–may qualify for higher multiples. Volatile or cyclical industries may face more conservative limits. Additionally, different lenders have different appetites: traditional banks, alternative lenders, and SBA-backed programs each have their own guidelines. Comparing offers from multiple sources helps ensure you receive competitive terms. Get matched with lenders to explore options aligned with your industry and profile.

Final Thoughts

Business term loan approval amounts are primarily based on revenue size, cash flow strength, credit profile, existing debt, and loan structure. Most established businesses qualify for capital proportional to revenue, with minimum starting at $10,000 and scaling significantly upward based on financial performance. If your business generates consistent revenue and needs structured lump-sum capital, reviewing available business term loan options can help determine realistic funding limits aligned with your financial profile.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much can you qualify for with a business term loan?

Business term loans typically range from $10,000 to $5,000,000+. The amount depends on revenue, DSCR, credit tier, time in business, existing debt, and whether the loan is secured.

How do lenders size a business term loan?

Revenue is the primary driver, then debt service coverage. Lenders translate cash flow into a payment you can support, capped by leverage limits and, for secured loans, collateral value.

What DSCR do term loan lenders want?

A DSCR around 1.20x or higher, showing your cash flow can cover the new payment alongside existing debt.

What increases the amount you can borrow?

Higher and steadier revenue, strong DSCR, better credit, 2+ years in business, low existing debt, and pledging collateral on a secured structure.
