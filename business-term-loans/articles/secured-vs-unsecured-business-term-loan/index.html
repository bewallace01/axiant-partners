What Is an Unsecured Business Term Loan?

An unsecured term loan does not require specific collateral. Approval is primarily based on revenue and credit strength. You receive a lump sum upfront with a fixed repayment schedule. Because lenders assume higher risk without collateral, unsecured loans typically offer higher interest rates, moderate loan amounts, and require stronger credit discipline. Approval often depends on consistent revenue, strong credit, a 600-680+ credit profile, and clean payment history.

What Is a Secured Business Term Loan?

A secured term loan requires collateral such as equipment, real estate, accounts receivable, inventory, or other business assets. Because the lender has asset protection, secured loans may offer lower interest rates, higher loan amounts, longer repayment terms, and more flexibility for moderate-credit borrowers. However, default could result in asset recovery actions.

When an Unsecured Term Loan Makes Sense

Appropriate when: capital need is moderate, you prefer not to pledge assets, revenue is strong, credit profile is solid, and speed is important. Often used for expansions, marketing initiatives, working capital injections, and short-to-mid-term growth projects.

When a Secured Term Loan Makes Sense

Appropriate when: you need a larger loan amount, want lower interest rates, have strong established growth, credit score is moderate but revenue is strong, or you have valuable business collateral. Commonly used for larger capital expenditures, debt consolidation, major expansion initiatives, and real estate-backed borrowing.

Credit Differences

Unsecured term loans typically require a stronger credit profile–often 680+ preferred for best terms, with some programs requiring 700+ for competitive pricing. Secured loans may allow slightly more flexibility and lower credit thresholds in some cases, with approval based more heavily on collateral strength. When you pledge assets, the lender has a secondary path to recover funds, which can offset moderate credit. See credit score requirements for a business term loan for tiers by structure.

Loan Amount Differences

Unsecured loans typically range from $10,000 to moderate six figures (depending on revenue). Secured loans may scale significantly higher depending on asset value, revenue, approval profile, and risk tolerance. Collateral can meaningfully expand borrowing capacity.

Blended or Hybrid Structures

Some lenders offer hybrid structures: a portion secured by collateral and a portion unsecured. This can help businesses that have some assets but not enough to fully secure their full capital need. Discuss hybrid options with lenders if you fall between fully secured and fully unsecured profiles.

Risk Considerations

Unsecured loan risk: No specific asset pledged; higher cost; potential personal guarantee required. Your business and personal credit bear the full risk. Secured loan risk: Asset at risk if default; lower cost; potential lien filings. If you default, the lender can pursue the collateral. Understanding risk tolerance is critical before selecting a structure. Weigh the benefit of lower rates and higher limits against the exposure of pledging assets.

Approval Timeline Differences

Unsecured: Often 1-10 business days, with streamlined programs sometimes approving in 1-3 days. Secured: 2-5 weeks on average, as asset verification, appraisals, and lien documentation add time. If speed is critical and your profile supports unsecured credit, that route may be preferable. If you need a larger amount or better rate and have strong collateral, the extra time for a secured facility may be worthwhile. See how fast you can get a business term loan for typical timelines.

Personal Guarantees

Both secured and unsecured business term loans often require a personal guarantee, especially for smaller and mid-sized businesses. The guarantee means the owner is personally liable if the business defaults. Some lenders offer limited or capped guarantees for larger, more established borrowers. Understand the guarantee terms before signing. For qualification context, see credit score requirements for a business term loan and how much you can qualify for.

Which Is Better?

There is no universal "better." The right structure depends on capital need size, risk tolerance, credit strength, available collateral, and cash flow stability. Strong businesses often compare both options before choosing. If you have valuable collateral and want lower cost and higher limits, secured may be the better fit. If you prefer speed, simplicity, and no asset pledge, unsecured may suit you. Discuss your situation with lenders or a financing advisor to determine the best structure. Types of Collateral Lenders Accept

For secured term loans, common collateral includes: equipment and machinery, accounts receivable, inventory, real estate (commercial or owner-occupied), and sometimes cash or marketable securities. The type and quality of collateral affect advance rates, loan-to-value ratios, and appraisal requirements. Equipment and real estate typically support longer terms; receivables and inventory may have shorter structures. Discuss your asset base with lenders to understand how it would structure your facility. For qualification context, see how much you can qualify for with a business term loan.

Minimum Loan Amount

Business term loans typically start at $10,000–$25,000 and scale upward based on qualification. Unsecured programs often have lower minimums; secured programs may start higher. Get matched with lenders to compare secured and unsecured options.

Final Thoughts

Both secured and unsecured business term loans provide structured lump-sum financing but serve different business profiles. Unsecured loans often offer speed, simplicity, and no asset pledge. Secured loans offer larger loan amounts, lower rates, and greater leverage. If your business generates consistent revenue and needs structured capital, reviewing both options helps determine the appropriate structure for your financial strategy. Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between a secured and unsecured business term loan?

Secured term loans pledge equipment, real estate, receivables, or inventory for lower rates, higher amounts, and longer terms. Unsecured term loans approve faster with no appraisal or UCC work but require 600-680+ FICO and stronger revenue, at higher pricing.

Which is better, a secured or unsecured term loan?

Pick secured if you need maximum capacity at the lowest cost, often $500K+ or to refinance multiple obligations. Pick unsecured if speed and keeping assets unencumbered outweigh the higher cost.

When does a secured term loan make sense?

When you need $500K+, want the lowest rate and longest term, or are refinancing multiple obligations, and you have collateral to pledge.

What credit score do you need for an unsecured term loan?

Typically 600-680+ FICO with stronger revenue, since there is no collateral backing the loan.
