Secured Business Loans vs Unsecured: Collateral, Rates and Approval Compared

What you pledge, what it saves you, how long it takes, and when an unsecured loan is the better call

Quick answer

A secured business loan is backed by an asset you pledge: equipment, real estate, receivables or inventory. Because the lender can recover that asset, secured loans usually price 2-5 percentage points below a comparable unsecured loan, go larger and run longer, but take 2-5 weeks for appraisal and lien work. Unsecured loans run about 10-30%+ APR, usually need 600+ credit and fund in 1-7 days, and most still carry a personal guarantee and a general UCC lien. On $50,000 over five years, 8% secured is about $1,014 a month against $1,163 at 14% unsecured.

The choice between a secured and an unsecured business loan comes down to one trade: pledge an asset and get a cheaper, bigger, longer loan, or keep your assets free and pay more for speed. Here is how the two compare and when each one wins. For the full program menu, see business term loans.

What Is a Secured Business Loan?

A secured business loan is backed by specific collateral: equipment and machinery, accounts receivable, inventory, real estate, and sometimes cash or marketable securities. The lender records a lien on that asset. Because it has a second way to get repaid, it can offer a lower rate, a larger amount, a longer term and more room for moderate credit. If you default, the lender can take the collateral.

The type of collateral shapes the loan. Equipment and real estate support longer terms; receivables and inventory usually support shorter ones. With real estate you already own, the lender sizes the loan on combined loan-to-value: a $1,000,000 property with a $500,000 first mortgage, at a 70% ceiling, leaves about $200,000 for a second lien. See real estate secured business loans.

Is a Small Business Loan Secured or Unsecured?

It can be either, and many are partly both. Bank, equipment and real estate loans are usually secured by a specific asset. Unsecured loans skip the specific asset, but most still include a personal guarantee and a general UCC lien on business assets; what you avoid is tying up a particular property or machine. Both kinds often require a personal guarantee from the owners, especially for small and mid-sized businesses.

Are SBA loans secured or unsecured?

SBA loans are generally secured: lenders take the collateral that is available. But an SBA loan is not declined only because collateral falls short when cash flow is strong, because the federal guarantee covers part of that gap. See what SBA lenders look for.

When Does a Secured Loan Make Sense?

When you need a larger amount (often $500,000 or more), want the lowest rate and longest term, are refinancing several debts into one, or have strong revenue but moderate credit, and you have collateral to pledge. Common uses are big equipment purchases, debt consolidation, major expansions and real-estate-backed borrowing.

When Does an Unsecured Loan Make Sense?

When the amount is moderate, revenue and credit are strong, speed matters, and you would rather not pledge assets. Typical uses are expansions, marketing pushes, working capital and short-to-mid-term projects. Below 640 credit, unsecured options shrink and price at 20-30%+.

Credit, Speed and Guarantees

  • Credit: unsecured loans usually want 600-680+, with 680+ for the best terms and some programs asking 700+. Secured loans lean more on the collateral, which can offset moderate credit. See credit score requirements for a business term loan.
  • Speed: unsecured often 1-10 business days, sometimes 1-3; secured 2-5 weeks on average because of asset checks, appraisals and lien documents. See the secured business loan approval timeline.
  • Guarantees: both usually require a personal guarantee; some lenders offer limited or capped guarantees to larger, established borrowers. Read the guarantee before you sign.
  • Hybrids: some lenders secure part of the loan and leave part unsecured, useful when your assets cover only some of the need.
  • Minimums: term loans typically start at $10,000-$25,000; secured programs may start higher.

Which Is Better?

Neither is better across the board. If you have collateral and want the lowest cost and the biggest loan, go secured. If you need money this week, the amount is moderate and your credit is strong, unsecured can be worth the higher rate. Frequently Asked Questions

What is a secured business loan?

A business loan backed by a specific asset you pledge, such as equipment, real estate, receivables or inventory. The lender records a lien and can take the asset if you default, so the loan usually costs less and can be larger than an unsecured one.

Is a small business loan secured or unsecured?

It can be either. Bank, equipment and real estate loans are usually secured by a specific asset, while unsecured loans rely on revenue and credit, though most still include a personal guarantee and a general UCC lien.

Are SBA loans secured or unsecured?

Generally secured: SBA lenders take the collateral that is available. A loan is not declined solely for a collateral shortfall when cash flow is strong, because the federal guarantee covers part of that gap.

How much would a $50,000 business loan cost?

About $1,014 a month as an 8% equipment-secured loan over five years, $1,062 at 10% unsecured from a bank, and about $4,874 a month on a 30% short-term online loan over 12 months. The rate and term matter more than the amount.

Which is better, a secured or unsecured term loan?

Secured if you need maximum capacity at the lowest cost, often $500,000 or more or a refinance of several debts. Unsecured if speed and keeping your assets free outweigh a rate that is typically 2-5 points higher.

What credit score do you need for an unsecured business loan?

Usually 600-680+ with steady revenue, and 680+ for the best terms. Below 640, unsecured options shrink and often price at 20-30% or more.

How long does a secured business loan take?

Typically 2-5 weeks, because the lender has to verify the asset, order an appraisal and prepare lien documents. Unsecured loans often decide in 1-10 business days.
