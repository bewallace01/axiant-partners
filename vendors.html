The co-branded page carries your name in the headline and your customer never lands on a generic form. It reads as a continuation of your sale rather than a hand-off to a stranger: your brand at the top, a short application underneath, and a confirmation with a reference number once it is submitted.

From there the file comes to us. We take the application to lenders that actually fund the category of equipment you sell, rather than sending your customer to shop themselves. There is no cost and no obligation to apply, and the initial match does not affect your customer's credit.

Why dealers put financing in front of the buyer

Most equipment does not fail to sell because the buyer does not want it. It fails to sell because paying cash for it empties an account the business needs for payroll, fuel, or the next job. Financing turns a five- or six-figure decision into a monthly number, which is a much easier conversation to have on the shop floor.

Offering it yourself also keeps the deal moving. A buyer who leaves to arrange their own funding is a buyer who has time to shop your competitor. Send us the equipment and the buyer profile and we will tell you which programs the deal is likely to clear.

What the program pays

Vendor partners earn a 35% commission on funded transactions. When a customer applies through your co-branded page and the loan or lease closes, you receive 35% of what Axiant Partners earns on that deal. Deals that do not fund pay nothing, and there is no upfront enrollment cost.

That is the same rate as our commercial lending referral program, so the choice between the two is about how you want to work rather than what you earn. The vendor program suits sellers who want financing offered inside their own sales process, under their own name; the referral program suits partners who would rather send the deal over and let us take it from there.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a vendor financing program?

It lets an equipment seller offer financing at the point of sale without lending its own money. The buyer applies through a co-branded page, and a finance partner places the deal with lenders. The seller gets paid for the equipment; the lender carries the loan or lease.

Does offering financing mean I am lending my own money?

No. You are introducing the buyer to financing, not funding it. The credit decision and the money come from the lender, so the risk does not sit on your balance sheet.

What does the co-branded page look like?

It carries your company name in the headline and a short business financing application underneath, followed by a confirmation with a reference number. Your customer stays in your brand rather than being handed to a generic form.

How much can my customers finance?

Typically $10,000 to $5 million for new or used equipment, with terms commonly running 24–84 months and down payments often between 0% and 20%. The figures move with credit, equipment type, and age, so confirm on the specific deal.

How fast do decisions come back?

Many equipment lenders return a decision in 24–48 hours when the application and quote are complete. Larger or more complex files take longer, and SBA-backed structures take considerably longer.

Do I get paid for sending deals?

Yes. Vendor partners earn a 35% commission on funded transactions — 35% of what Axiant Partners earns on the deal, paid once it closes. Deals that do not fund pay nothing, and there is no upfront enrollment cost.

Is the vendor program the same as the referral program?

The rate is the same 35% on funded transactions. The difference is structure: the vendor program gives you a co-branded application page your customers apply through, which keeps financing inside your own sale. The referral program is for partners who would rather simply send the deal over. Read the referral terms.
