How Hard Is It to Get a $100,000 Business Loan?

Harder than $25,000, easier than $500,000. At six figures most lenders stop approving on a credit score alone and start checking whether your cash flow carries the payment. A useful test: if the payment from the table above is more than about 10-15% of your average monthly deposits, expect questions, a smaller offer or a longer term. That rule of thumb is ours, not a lender standard.

How to get approved for $100,000: apply to the product that fits the use (equipment financing for equipment, SBA or bank for long-term growth), show three to six months of clean bank statements with no overdrafts, have your last two years of tax returns ready for SBA or bank lenders, pay down or explain any existing daily or weekly advances, and ask for the term that keeps the payment comfortable rather than the shortest one. For SBA lenders, the SBA caps the rate on a loan of $50,001 to $250,000 at the base rate plus 6.0%. More on the SBA side in SBA loan requirements.

$100,000 Business Loan FAQs

How hard is it to get a $100,000 business loan?

Moderately hard. Most lenders commonly look for about 1+ year in business, $15,000-$20,000+ a month in revenue and 600+ credit, and they check that your deposits carry the payment. SBA and bank loans also want tax returns and usually two years of history.

What is the monthly payment on a $100,000 business loan?

It depends on product, rate and term. As an illustration: about $1,294-$1,378 for an SBA 7(a) loan over 10 years at 9.5%-11%, $2,028-$2,275 for a 5-year bank loan at 8%-13%, and $4,568-$5,591 for a 2-year online loan at 9%-30%.

What do I need to qualify for a $100,000 business loan?

Lenders commonly look for around 1+ year in business, roughly $15,000-$20,000+ in monthly revenue and a 600+ FICO for the widest set of options. Bank statements are always required; SBA and bank lenders add tax returns and financial statements.

How do I get approved for a $100,000 business loan?

Match the product to the use, send three to six months of clean bank statements, have two years of tax returns ready for SBA or bank lenders, deal with any existing advances, and ask for a term that keeps the payment comfortable.

What credit score do I need for a $100,000 business loan?

600+ opens most programs; 680+ commonly gets the best rates and longest terms. Options exist below 600 through revenue-based and short-term lenders, at a higher cost.

Is an SBA loan better for $100,000?

If you can wait several weeks and qualify, an SBA 7(a) loan usually has the lowest payment because of its longer term. If you need funds in days, a term loan or line of credit funds faster at a higher rate.

Do I need collateral for a $100,000 business loan?

Unsecured options exist for strong profiles, but collateral or a personal guarantee is common at this size and can improve the rate. Equipment and real estate can serve as collateral when relevant.
