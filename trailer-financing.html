What Is Trailer Financing?

Trailer financing is a loan or lease that lets you get a trailer-flatbed, dump, equipment, lowboy, or cargo-without paying the full price upfront. You make monthly payments; with a loan you own the trailer when it's paid off. Trailers are essential for hauling equipment, materials, and freight, but they're a real investment. We connect you with lenders that offer trailer financing so you can get the trailer and get on the road.

What Trailers Can Be Financed?

New trailers. Buying from a dealer? Lenders routinely finance new flatbeds, dump trailers, equipment trailers, lowboys, and cargo trailers. Get your quote, we match you with a lender, and you secure financing before you buy.

Used trailers. Used trailer financing is common. Lenders look at age, condition, and value. Terms may be a bit shorter than for new, but you can still finance used trailers.

Private seller. Some of our lenders fund private-party purchases with a bill of sale and trailer details.

Dealer purchase. Dealer deals are the most common. We add options by matching you with our network so you can compare terms.

Terms & Requirements

Loan amounts. Trailer loans can range from roughly $5,000 to $150,000+ depending on type and size. We match you with lenders that finance in your range.

Credit flexibility. We work with lenders that consider a range of credit. We also work with lenders that offer trailer financing for fair or lower credit.

Time in business. Some lenders want to see a year in business; others work with newer operations. We match you based on your situation.

Down payment. Down payments of 10-20% are typical. More down can help with approval or rate. We connect you with lenders that fit what you can put down.
