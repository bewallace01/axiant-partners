Adding Improvements to an SBA Building Purchase

What renovation can ride on the purchase loan, what can't, when bonds and an as-completed appraisal kick in, and how the term changes

← Back to 100% SBA Financing Articles

Updated November 13, 2026

Quick answer

As of November 2026, renovation of the space your business will use can be added to an SBA 7(a) building purchase and repaid over the same 25-year real estate term, plus time to build. Space you lease to others can't be improved with SBA money. Work over $350,000 usually needs bonded contractors, and renovation over a third of the price needs an as-completed appraisal. What the Loan Can and Can't Pay For

Buying a building rarely means moving in as is. Under SOP 50 10 8.1, build-out of the space your business uses is part of the real estate project, so it can be financed with the purchase, including under 100% SBA financing.

Can be in the loanCan't be in the loan
Walls, offices, restroomsBuild-out of space leased to a tenant
HVAC, electrical, plumbingWork on excess land
Code, ADA, licensing workCost overruns after approval (lender's call)
Architect and permit costsImprovements to an apartment you rent out

If a seller or landlord gives you an improvement allowance, the SOP says it must first pay down the loan, unless the lender has already counted it in its working capital analysis.

Owned Building Versus Leased Space

The SOP sets different terms for improvements depending on who owns the building:

You areMaximum term on the improvementsWhat the lender also needs
Buying the buildingUp to 25 years with the real estate, plus construction timeAppraisal, environmental review
Improving a building you lease10 years, plus up to 12 months to finishLease and landlord waiver in larger cases

Leasehold improvements over $500,000, or over 30% of the loan (whichever is less), trigger lease review: for 7(a), the lease plus renewal options should run as long as the loan. That's one reason growing companies buy instead of building out a space they rent.

Thresholds That Change the Process

  • Over $350,000 of construction. The lender generally needs a licensed contractor with 100% performance and payment bonds, builder's risk and workers' comp, unless a construction manager controls the funds. Below $350,000 the lender can use its normal construction practices.
  • Renovation over one-third of the price. That's substantial renovation, so the appraisal must value the building as completed. At the end, the lender needs confirmation the work matches the plans, or a new appraisal.
  • Doing it yourself. The SOP calls owner-as-contractor projects generally unsatisfactory. Allowed only if the lender can justify it.
  • Lien waivers. Required from every contractor, subcontractor and supplier before final payment.

Illustrative numbers, not a quote or an offer; rates and fees change, and every loan is subject to approval. A distributor buys a 20,000 sq ft building, occupies 14,000 sq ft and leases 6,000 sq ft. Getting the Budget Right

The lender and SBA aren't obligated to raise the loan for overruns, and before approving a contract change the lender needs proof you can pay the extra. Carry a contingency in the budget and keep cash for surprises. For older buildings, the daycare building guide shows how lead testing adds to the scope.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I add renovation costs to an SBA loan to buy a building?

Yes. Improvements to the space your business will use can be financed with the purchase and repaid over the real estate term.

Can SBA money pay for a tenant's build-out?

No. SBA proceeds can't improve or renovate space leased to a third party.

When do I need bonded contractors?

When the construction part of a 7(a) loan is over $350,000, unless the lender uses a construction manager that controls the funds.

What is an as-completed appraisal?

When renovation costs exceed a third of the purchase price, the appraiser values the property as it will be once the work is done.

What's the term on leasehold improvements if I don't own the building?

Up to 10 years, plus up to 12 months to complete the work.
