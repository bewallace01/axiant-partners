Buying the building your business operates from, by property type and by rule

← All Articles

Quick answer

As of November 2026, some SBA 7(a) lenders finance up to 100% of a building an established business will occupy, on terms up to 25 years. Your business must use at least 51% of an existing building. Property type changes the file: environmental work for shops, licensing space for daycares, used land for yards. Axiant Partners, a broker in Boca Raton, matches owners with these lenders.

The SBA sets the rules, but each lender decides whether to go to 100%. These guides cover what changes by property type and the rules every buyer runs into. Start with 100% SBA financing, or compare SBA 7(a) and 504.
